# HYP-0001 — NQ overnight expectancy and conditional improvements

- Status: evaluated-provisional
- Created: 2026-08-23
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: Equity-index overnight drift may be positive, while trend and implied-volatility state may condition its magnitude; inverse-vol sizing may reduce PnL concentration.
- Exact change: Compare the unconditional 18:00-to-09:30 long with frozen x-day SMA and prior-close VIX range sweeps under four causal sizing rules.
- Frozen baseline: One NQ contract long from exact 18:00 ET bar open to exact next-date 09:30 ET bar open, net of frozen costs.
- Primary metric: Net dollars per trade and daily net Sharpe, with HAC(5) t-statistic.
- Kill test: Baseline is weakened if net mean is nonpositive. A filter or sizing improvement is not accepted if it is isolated to one searched cell, lacks neighbouring support, or is not larger than a selection-aware null in later validation.
- Required controls: Gross versus net, exact-clock coverage, roll exclusion, causal join assertions, neighbouring MA/VIX cells, equal-contract reference.
- Data scope: NQ minute history 2011-08-01 through 2026-07-14 and daily VIX overlap; all dates are discovery/consumed.
- Multiple-testing note: All MA and VIX cells are an exploratory family; best-cell statistics are descriptive until a full-sweep permutation null is run.

## Rationale and evidence

The user requested the baseline, both filters, and four sizing modes in one reproducible study.

inverse-vol arms did not dominate equal sizing. The acceptance gate remains open
