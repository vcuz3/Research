# HYP-0002 — Reproduce 10 percent vol-targeted overnight leg figure

- Status: reproduced-provisional
- Created: 2026-08-24
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: Scaling the overnight leg to a stable ex-ante risk target may make calendar-year performance comparable across volatility regimes.
- Exact change: Apply 10% annualized target volatility using the overnight leg's prior 60 gross returns, lag one trade day, cap leverage at 3x, and charge min(0.5 bp of fill price, one tick) per side.
- Frozen baseline: The audited unfiltered exact-clock 18:00-to-09:30 candidate series from EXP-0003.
- Primary metric: Compounded net return by calendar year, with realized annualized volatility, Sharpe, drawdown, and average leverage.
- Kill test: The reproduction is invalid if any sized trade uses its own/current return, the first 60 observations are not warm-up, leverage exceeds 3x, or either side's cost exceeds one tick.
- Required controls: Hand lag fixture, cost cap fixture, compounded-return fixture, gross versus net return, cap-bind and warm-up counts.
- Data scope: EXP-0003 valid NQ trade candidates, 2011-08-02 through 2026-07-14; full history consumed.
- Multiple-testing note: No parameter sweep; one externally supplied specification. External figure values were not supplied, so numerical parity cannot yet be judged.

## Rationale and evidence

User requested a year-by-year reproduction under a precise volatility-target and cost convention.

EXP-0004 passed all mechanical kill tests and produced the yearly series. Exact
parity with the outside figure is unassessed because its numerical values and
whether it uses overnight-leg or close-to-close realized volatility were not
provided.
