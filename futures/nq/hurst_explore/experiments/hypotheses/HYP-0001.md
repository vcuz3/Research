# HYP-0001 — Measurement-error floor of an intraday Hurst estimate

- Status: tested (descriptive; no trading claim)
- Created: 2026-07-22
- Origin idea: A1 in the Hurst-exploration section (new thread requested 2026-07-22)
- Builder: Claude (Opus 4.8)
- Proposed mechanism: an intraday Hurst exponent estimated on a short
  session-to-date window carries large sampling error; the prior noise_vwap
  Hurst work estimated H at 30-min decision points (windows starting ~30 bars),
  where the estimate may be dominated by noise rather than signal. 1-second
  sampling supplies ~60x more points and should shrink the floor.
- Exact change: measurement only. Four estimators from three families
  (structure-function GHE q=1 & q=2, R/S, DFA) applied to (a) synthetic fBm of
  known H at each realistic intraday window length, and (b) real NQ/ES paths at
  1m (both) and 1s (NQ only).
- Frozen baseline: none (this is a characterization study, not a strategy delta).
- Primary metric: std(Hhat) as a function of window length, estimator, and
  sampling frequency (the "floor"); plus cross-estimator disagreement on real
  windows vs that floor.
- Kill test / what would make the study uninformative: if the synthetic floor
  were negligible at n≈30 (it is not — std≈0.2–0.3) OR if the estimators
  disagreed on real data by less than their own synthetic bias (they do not —
  disagreement is explained BY the biases), the "intraday H is noisy /
  estimator-dependent" claim would be unsupported.
- Required controls: synthetic truth-recovery at long windows (tests/test_hurst),
  matched bias table per estimator, scale-invariance test.
- Data scope: NQ/ES 1m clean 2011-08→2026-07 (3710 sessions each); NQ 1s
  Databento 2015→2026 (high-fill sample), degraded days excluded.
- Multiple-testing note: descriptive; no selection over a strategy family.
