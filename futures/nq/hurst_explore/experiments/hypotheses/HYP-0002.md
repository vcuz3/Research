# HYP-0002 — Hurst term structure across timescale (micro vs meso)

- Status: tested (descriptive; no trading claim)
- Created: 2026-07-22
- Origin idea: A2 in the Hurst-exploration section (new thread requested 2026-07-22)
- Builder: Claude (Opus 4.8)
- Proposed mechanism: a single fixed-lag Hurst estimate blends every scale. The
  stated prior expectation was that the tape is anti-persistent at the
  microstructure (sub-minute) scale and diffusive/persistent at minute scale, so
  the whole-session H is a mixture. Measuring H(tau) locally across the lag axis
  should separate the regimes; 1s data is required to reach sub-minute.
- Exact change: measurement only. Translation-invariant structure function
  K_1(tau)=mean_t|logp[t+tau]-logp[t]| aggregated across sessions; local log-log
  slope = H(tau). NQ 1s (1s..1h), NQ/ES 1m (1..190 min) with a 1s/1m overlap
  consistency check. Matched H=0.5 random-walk control of the real path length to
  subtract the structure-function saturation artifact at large tau.
- Frozen baseline: matched-length synthetic random walk (H=0.5).
- Primary metric: H(tau) curve and per-band H, control-corrected (0.5 + raw −
  control); cross-frequency (1s vs 1m) agreement in the overlap; NQ↔ES transfer.
- Kill test: if the control-corrected H(tau) were flat at 0.5 across all scales,
  there is no scale structure to find. If the coarse-scale anti-persistence
  vanished after subtracting the RW control, it was a saturation artifact.
- Required controls: matched-length RW control (saturation); 1s/1m overlap;
  NQ↔ES sibling transfer; Rule-9a fill-rate filter (>=0.90) so forward-fill
  zero-returns do not deflate the sub-minute points.
- Data scope: as HYP-0001; 1s restricted to era>=2015 & fill_rate>=0.90.
- Multiple-testing note: descriptive.

## Rationale and evidence

See `artifacts/runs/EXP-0002/a2_summary.txt`, `scale_curve.csv`, and
`reports/FINDINGS.md`. NOTE: the stated micro-anti-persistence expectation was
NOT confirmed — 1s micro H≈0.50 (1s OHLCV is too coarse to resolve the bid-ask
bounce; true-tick data would be needed). The real structure is a mild
anti-persistence that GROWS with scale, ~half of which was saturation artifact.
