# HYP-0001 - Partial-overlap futures transfer of closing momentum

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-08-19; materially revised after independent review 2026-08-20
- Origin idea: IDEA-0001
- Builder: Codex
- Primary instrument: ES; NQ is a sibling and cannot rescue an ES failure.
- Scope label: paper-rule futures transfer, **not** a paper-faithful replication.
- Proposed mechanism: opening/closing concentration in informed trading and infrequent rebalancing links the first and final cash half-hours.
- Exact change: transfer Gao et al.'s fixed r1 and joint-sign equations to roll-safe ES/NQ with causal one-second fills and explicit costs.
- Frozen baseline: exact published sign rules; ES r1 is primary and the joint arm and NQ are secondary.
- Primary metric: ES r1 2014-2026 primary-cost zero-filled HAC lag-5 t-statistic.
- Kill test: reject persistence if ES gross or net mean is nonpositive, the
  one-sided Newey-West p-value exceeds 0.05, the paired signal-minus-always-long
  mean is nonpositive, or (after machinery validation) the circular-null
  p-value exceeds 0.05.
- Required controls: always-long identical window, joint arm, NQ sibling, cost/era/exit sensitivities, MDE, and a full injected positive control.
- Data scope: local NQ/ES one-minute signals and one-second execution from 2011-08-02 through 2026-07-14 only.
- Multiple-testing note: one ES r1 primary; the joint arm, NQ, costs, and eras cannot rescue it.

## Hypothesis and mechanism

Gao et al. argue that infrequent rebalancing and informed trading concentrated
near high-volume opening and closing windows make the first half-hour return
predict the final half-hour. The exact published sign rules are transferred to
local futures to test persistence outside the paper's SPY universe.

## Exact rule

- `r1 = P(10:00) / P(previous 16:00) - 1`.
- `r12 = P(15:30) / P(15:00) - 1`.
- `r13 = P(16:00) / P(15:30) - 1`.
- Main paper arm: long if `r1 > 0`, short if `r1 <= 0`; exact zero is short.
- Joint arm: long if both signals are positive, short if both are non-positive,
  and flat otherwise.
- For the main arm, the entry is scheduled hours after `r1` is known and is the
  first one-second trade at/after 15:30:00 ET. The joint arm becomes known at
  15:30:00 and uses the first trade at/after 15:30:01.
- Both arms use a pre-scheduled liquidation active at 15:59:59, filled by the
  first record strictly before 16:00:00; absence hard-fails the run.
  Adjacent-second exits at 15:59:58 and 16:00:00 are sensitivities.
- Same-instrument identity is required from previous close through every feature
  and fill. No forward filling is allowed.
- The frozen local eligibility cohort is a scheduled full NYSE session with
  `available` source condition on it and the previous expected NYSE session, a
  present previous 15:59 endpoint, and exactly one unique one-minute record at
  every 09:30-09:59 clock (30 bars, no gap over 60 seconds), plus a present
  10:00 one-minute open and symbol. The previous-close, 09:30, 09:59, and 10:00
  records must share one contract, and the held RTH session must contain
  no roll row or contract switch, and the arm's required one-second fills must
  exist. Missing feature endpoints hard-fail rather than silently select days.
  Unrelated midday bars are not an eligibility filter; the strict 390-bar cohort
  is reported only as a data-quality sensitivity.

## Estimand, costs, and inference

- Deployment estimand: one-contract daily P&L with every eligible flat day kept
  as zero; annualized Sharpe uses `sqrt(252)`.
- Primary costs: one tick adverse per side, representing spread plus execution
  slippage, and $2.25 fees per side. Report gross and 0.5/1/2-tick stress.
- Mean inference: one-sided Newey-West lag-5 t-test.
- Primary era: 2014-01-01 through 2026-07-14. Label 2014-2018 as after the paper
  sample/first draft, and 2019 onward as post-JFE publication.
- 2011-08 through 2013 is only a short partial overlap diagnostic.

## Controls and null

- Always long during the identical last-half-hour window.
- Joint `r1+r12` arm, gross/cost stress, era/year, and zero-`r1` count.
- Claim-matched 4,999-draw year-stratified circular shift of `r13` outcomes,
  fixed seed 20260820. The null statistic is the ES r1 arm's 2014-2026
  primary-cost, zero-filled Newey-West lag-5 t-statistic. It
  preserves within-year outcome paths, signals, costs, and trade counts while
  destroying the date pairing between `r1` and `r13`.
- Before interpreting the real null, report `2.49*HAC_SE` as the one-sided 80%
  MDE and run one full injected positive control. Solve a constant side-aligned
  effect that gives zero-filled net Sharpe 0.50 and require null p<=0.05.
- Any unusable draw, invariant failure, identity donor map, or zero null variance
  makes the baseline null INCONCLUSIVE. The joint arm remains secondary.

## Conclusion rule

Reject futures persistence if ES 2014-2026 gross mean or primary-cost net mean
is non-positive, its one-sided Newey-West lag-5 p-value exceeds 0.05, or the
paired signal-minus-always-long daily mean is nonpositive. These observed
economic/HAC failures take precedence and remain REJECT even if the injected
positive control fails. Only after the observed gates pass may an injected
positive-control or null-machinery failure produce INCONCLUSIVE. With valid
machinery, reject if the circular-shift p-value exceeds 0.05; otherwise call the
futures persistence transfer supported.
NQ is corroborative only. No result can reproduce or refute the published SPY
claim under the user's local-only data restriction.
