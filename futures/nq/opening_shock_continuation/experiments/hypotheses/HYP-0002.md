# HYP-0002 - Opening-gap agreement continuation

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-08-19; materially revised after independent review 2026-08-20
- Origin idea: IDEA-0002
- Builder: Codex
- Primary traded book: one full-size NQ contract; ES is validation only.
- Historical status: all observations are globally consumed. The dated splits
  below are historical validation diagnostics, not clean out-of-sample data.
- Proposed mechanism: same-sign overnight and opening moves identify persistent two-stage price discovery rather than the usual opening reversal.
- Exact change: trade sign(m) only when g*m is positive, enter after 10:00:00 ET information is complete, and liquidate at the cash close.
- Frozen baseline: reconstructed 23-arm opening-feature family with C004 confirmed_gap as the declared primary candidate.
- Primary metric: combined 2019-2026 NQ primary-cost zero-filled daily Sharpe.
- Kill test: require every economic, two-null, simultaneous-control, sibling, and power gate below; otherwise reject or mark machinery failures inconclusive.
- Required controls: opposition, selected-day always-long, three deployable magnitude matches, ungated/overnight/total-r1 rules, ES, latency, cost, era, and side tests.
- Data scope: local roll-safe NQ/ES one-minute signals and one-second execution from 2011-08-02 through 2026-07-14 only.
- Multiple-testing note: rerun the complete bounded 23-candidate discovery pipeline inside both 4,999-draw paired nulls; historical inference remains diagnostic.

## Hypothesis and mechanism

Typical overnight moves partially reverse after the cash open. If the overnight
gap and the first cash half-hour instead have the same sign, two distinct price-
discovery stages may be responding to persistent information or slow capital.
That agreement should predict continuation from shortly after 10:00 through the
16:00 cash close.

## Exact deployable rule

- `g = log(09:30 open / previous session's 15:59 close)`.
- `m = log(09:59 close / 09:30 open)`.
- Trade only if `g*m > 0`; side is `sign(m)`. If either is zero, stay flat.
- The 09:59 bar is complete and the signal available at 10:00:00 ET.
- Primary entry is the first one-second trade at/after 10:00:01, then one tick
  adverse execution adjustment.
- Entry sensitivities activate at 10:00:00 (optimistic bound), 10:00:02,
  10:00:05, 10:00:30, and 10:01:00.
- Primary exit is a liquidation instruction scheduled when the position opens,
  active at 15:59:59, and represented by the first one-second trade thereafter
  but strictly before 16:00:00, plus adverse execution. Absence of that record
  after an otherwise eligible entry is a run-level data failure, never a quiet
  day exclusion. Adjacent 15:59:58 and 16:00:00 activations are sensitivities.
- No stops, targets, intraday flips, scaling, or compounding.
- Primary NQ calendar is determined independently of ES and future same-day bar
  completeness. Before entry it requires a known full-day NYSE schedule, all 30
  opening bars, the prior expected session's 15:59 endpoint, current entry, and
  identical instrument ID through the causal endpoints. Databento current/prior
  condition must be `available` as a separately reported measurement-quality
  gate. No signal or price is forward-filled.
- Post-run validity requires the prespecified exit and one symbol across every
  represented RTH bar and both fills; failure invalidates the run rather than
  deleting the day. The exact-390-bar cohort is a secondary QA sensitivity,
  not the primary selector. ES is evaluated on its own causal panel; paired
  null/cross-market analyses use the common causal NQ/ES signal panel.

## Frozen samples and costs

- Discovery: 2011-08-02 through 2018-12-31.
- Historical validation A: 2019-01-01 through 2022-12-31.
- Historical validation B: 2023-01-01 through 2026-07-14.
- Primary costs: one 0.25-point tick adverse per side plus $2.25 fees per side.
  The tick adjustment represents spread plus execution slippage.
- Stress: gross and 0.5, 1, and 2 ticks per side. Multipliers are $20/point NQ
  and $50/point ES. Report points, ticks, dollars, costs, and gross/cost ratios.

## Primary and mechanism estimands

1. Deployment: NQ one-contract primary-cost daily net P&L, including zero on
   every eligible no-trade day; primary statistic is combined 2019-2026
   `sqrt(252)` annualized Sharpe.
2. Mechanism: gross signed P&L per selected day and its difference from matched
   controls/opposition.

Mean inference uses Newey-West lag 5. The five prespecified mean-difference
controls use simultaneous stationary-bootstrap inference with 4,999 draws and
expected block length 20 sessions, as specified below. Sharpe and maximum
drawdown are reported as descriptive point estimates; no confidence interval is
claimed for either statistic and neither receives a bootstrap-based decision
gate.

## Required controls

- Ungated opening momentum: `sign(m)` every eligible day.
- Total-r1 momentum: `sign(g+m)` every eligible day.
- Overnight-only: `sign(g)` every eligible day.
- Opposition mirror: when `g*m<0`, trade `sign(m)`.
- Always long on the exact agreement-selected dates.
- NQ long/short decomposition; ES on its own panel and common-panel sensitivity.
- All entry/exit latency and cost sensitivities above.
- Three matching dimensions, always with side `sign(m)`:
  causal-normalized `|m|`, `|g+m|`, and `min(|g|,|m|)`.
  - Deployable controls use thresholds fitted only on discovery.
  - Diagnostic controls select exactly the agreement count within each
    validation era, sorting score descending then date ascending. These are
    explicitly nondeployable.
- The deployment comparison is primary-cost, zero-filled daily NQ dollars on an
  identical causal calendar: `Delta_j=mean(P_agreement,t-P_j,t)`. One simultaneous
  family contains opposition, selected-date always-long, and the three deployable
  magnitude controls. With 4,999 stationary-bootstrap draws (expected block 20),
  resample jointly by date and separately inside validation A and B. Let `c` be
  the 95th percentile of `max_j(Delta_j-Delta*_b,j)` and `L_j=Delta_j-c`; GO
  requires `min(L_j)>0`. Exact validation-era top-K controls remain diagnostic
  and nondeployable. Gross per-selected-trade effects are reported separately.

## Selective-search manifest and nulls

`experiments/candidate_manifest.json` is a frozen, bounded best-effort
reconstruction of the informal discovery family, not an exhaustive record. The original human selection was not logged
before first look; consequently even corrected historical selective p-values are
diagnostic and cannot manufacture a clean holdout.

The observed statistic `T` is combined 2019-2026 NQ primary-cost, zero-filled
daily Sharpe. The same deterministic discovery pipeline is applied to real data
and must select C004 `confirmed_gap`; otherwise the run is INCONCLUSIVE. Within
every real/null pipeline, gap-derived features and discovery rate-matched
thresholds are recomputed, selection occurs on discovery, then those thresholds
are frozen before validation scoring.

Both nulls use 4,999 draws, seed 20260820, and
`p=(1+count(T_null>=T_real))/5000`:

1. Conditional permutation: within `calendar year x joint NQ/ES opening-side`,
   permute paired `(g_NQ,g_ES)` vectors as a unit. Recompute gap-derived features,
   rerun the complete discovery selection rule, then score its selected arm in
   2019-2026. This preserves each stratum's gap-vector multiset, exact yearly
   agreement and long/short counts, NQ/ES gap pairing, opening/future paths,
   fills, and costs; it breaks which overnight gap belongs to which opening/day.
2. Bivariate circular shift: within each calendar year, rotate paired NQ/ES gap
   vectors by one nonzero common offset, rerun selection, and score validation.
   This preserves local volatility regimes and serial gap ordering.

Required assertions: identical eligible dates/execution paths, gap-vector
multisets, gap pairing, declared count invariants for conditional permutation,
no NaN-as-pass, nonidentity donor maps, nonzero null variance, unusable-draw
count, null center/dispersion, selected-candidate frequencies, and Monte Carlo
exact Clopper-Pearson Monte Carlo intervals. Any unusable/identity draw,
invariant failure, all-invalid candidate set, or zero null variance makes the
run INCONCLUSIVE. Invalid individual candidates receive `-inf`; all 4,999 final
draw statistics must be finite and none may be silently dropped.

## Power gate

- Compute `MDE_daily=2.49*HAC_SE` separately for daily dollars, points, and bps;
  divide by observed trade rate for per-trade units and divide points by 0.25 for
  ticks.
- Demean unsigned paired forward outcomes within calendar year x joint opening
  side. Inject a side-aligned NQ agreement effect across all eras and solve one
  fixed delta so the 2019-2026 template has zero-filled net Sharpe exactly 0.50.
- Stationary-bootstrap paired records independently within discovery,
  validation A, and validation B. Each of 200 outer trials must select C004 and
  reject both declared nulls at 0.05 using 499 inner draws. Require at least
  160 successes and zero invalid trials.
- One full 4,999-draw injected run must also select C004 and have both p-values
  at most 0.05.
- Power RNG streams are distinct and frozen: full-template conditional seed
  20260821, full-template circular seed 20260822, and outer-bootstrap seed
  20260823. For one-based outer trial `i`, construct each RNG exactly as
  `numpy.random.default_rng(numpy.random.SeedSequence([20260823, i, s]))`, with
  stream IDs `s=0` for the paired donor map, `s=1` for the conditional inner
  null, and `s=2` for the circular inner null. The runner records the generated
  uint64 audit seed for every stream; scheduling or early failure cannot shift
  a later trial's stream.

## Kill hierarchy

Machinery failure (data, fill, null invariants, or power) means INCONCLUSIVE.
Otherwise a historical GO requires every condition:

- NQ 2019-2026 primary-cost zero-filled net Sharpe at least 0.50.
- Positive primary-cost mean in both validation eras.
- Positive combined net mean at two ticks per side and at 10:01 entry.
- Both NQ long and short combined gross means positive; otherwise relabel as a
  directional-regime effect.
- All five simultaneous deployment-control lower bounds strictly above zero,
  including selected-date always-long.
- Both selective-null p-values at most 0.05.
- ES combined gross mean and ES agreement-minus-opposition mean positive.

An economic/statistical failure means REJECT. Even a full historical GO remains
provisional until future-only shadow observations accrue.

Evaluation is hierarchical and frozen before validation is opened. Run the
data/engine gate, baseline, reconstructed discovery, and primary economic
experiment first. If any primary economic, cost, era, side, or ES gate already
fails, the hypothesis is REJECTED and the expensive selective-null/control/
power stages may stop without their statistics being interpreted. A GO, or any
claim based on the selective-null p-values, always requires the full control and
power machinery above; a stopped run can never be called inconclusive merely to
avoid an observed economic rejection.
