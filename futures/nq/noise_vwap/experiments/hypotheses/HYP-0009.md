# HYP-0009 — A frozen overnight-gap and RVOL interaction schedule improves NQ capital allocation

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-07-19
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: Contemporaneous participation should modulate conviction: reduce risk when a breakout opposes the overnight gap on low RVOL and modestly increase risk when gap alignment is confirmed by high RVOL.
- Exact change: Keep every baseline trade and assign entry-frozen risk weights: 0.5x for aligned_gap<0 and RVOL90<1.0; 0.75x for aligned_gap<0 and 1.0<=RVOL90<1.5; 1.25x for aligned_gap>=0 and RVOL90>1.5; 1.0x otherwise or when RVOL is unavailable. No threshold or weight search.
- Frozen baseline: NQ continuous-stop baseline: lookback 90, RTH, 30-minute decisions, VWAP gate, every-bar stop, next-open fills, 1.0x risk.
- Primary metric: Zero-eligible-session daily net-ATR-R Sharpe uplift and max-drawdown improvement, with paired full-pipeline Null-C Sharpe uplift distribution.
- Kill test: Reject unless full-sample daily Sharpe improves, total weighted net R is at least baseline, max drawdown improves, and Sharpe uplift beats 30 paired Null-C draws at z>=2; weaken if net-R uplift is not positive in both pre-2023 and 2023+ eras.
- Required controls: Same engine trades and state in both arms; entry-frozen causal weights; costs scale with size; all eligible zero-trade sessions retained; average exposure reported; 30 corrected paired Null-C draws rebuilding bands, RVOL, signals, trades, and weights.
- Data scope: NQ clean Databento RTH history through 2026, post-lookback-90 eligible sessions; historically consumed discovery sample.
- Multiple-testing note: One frozen four-level schedule stated to the user before the run; no threshold or weight grid.

## Rationale and evidence

A sizing test preserves trade coverage and asks whether the EXP-0015 interaction allocates risk better than equal sizing, while EXP-0016 warns that apparent selectivity gains may be reproduced by Null C.
