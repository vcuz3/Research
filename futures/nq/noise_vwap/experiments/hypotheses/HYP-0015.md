# HYP-0015 — A symmetric overnight-gap MAGNITUDE whole-day veto improves the NQ continuous-stop baseline

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-07-21
- Origin idea: user — "if there's an overnight gap, we just don't trade" (a hard
  GATE, not the previously-tried gap/RVOL scaling).
- Builder: Claude (Opus 4.8)
- Proposed mechanism: a large overnight gap distorts the construct's OWN anchor.
  The noise band is a same-time-of-day dispersion built from the trailing 90
  sessions, and the day's `rth_open` / VWAP are the reference the breakout is
  measured against. On a big-gap day the open sits far from the prior close, the
  trailing same-slot band never "saw" a gap of that size, and VWAP starts far
  from where the band is centred — so the earliest breakouts are gap-continuation
  noise the band mis-sizes. Skipping the whole day removes those mis-sized
  decisions. This is an ANCHOR-CONTAMINATION claim, distinct from a volatility
  claim (EXP-0017 vol/gap sizing) and from a directional claim (EXP-0016
  gap-vs-signal + low RVOL veto).
- Exact change: whole-day skip. Per day compute the causal overnight gap
  `gap_atr = |rth_open - prior_close| / atr_pts` (`atr_pts` = the session's
  prior-14-session mean RTH range, already causal). If `gap_atr > T`, veto EVERY
  entry that day (implemented as an `entry_gate` that excludes all decision keys
  on skipped days). Symmetric in gap sign; no RVOL term; no direction term.
  Threshold family T ∈ {0.50, 0.75, 1.00, 1.50, 2.00} ATR (family-max reported).
- Frozen baseline: NQ continuous-stop baseline — lookback 90, RTH, 30-minute
  decisions, VWAP gate, every-bar stop, next-open fills.
- Primary metric: full-sample zero-trade-session daily net-ATR-R Sharpe uplift
  over baseline, with total net R co-primary; family-max over the T grid.
- Kill test: REJECT unless, at the family-best T, full-sample daily Sharpe uplift
  ≥ +0.10 AND total net R ≥ baseline net R. If (and only if) that real gate
  passes, the uplift must additionally beat BOTH:
    (a) 30 paired drift-preserving Null-C draws at z ≥ 2 (machinery test), AND
    (b) a 200-draw MATCHED-COUNT random-day-drop null at frac(null ≥ real) < 0.05
        (drop the same NUMBER of whole days at random — the rarity/variance-filter
        discriminator that killed EXP-0016/0017 and every prior selectivity gate).
  WEAKEN / treat as rarity-filter if gross points-per-trade on the KEPT trades
  does NOT rise (falling gross with rising Sharpe = cutting exposure, not
  information), or if the sign does not transfer to the ES sibling (the
  anchor-contamination mechanism, being structural, should transfer).
- Required controls: stateful engine rerun with `entry_gate` (identical fills,
  exits, costs, dates); causal ATR and causal band; (a) 30 paired corrected
  Null-C draws recomputing bands, gaps, signals, gate, trades; (b) 200 matched-
  count random-day-drop draws on the real tape; ES sibling as the mechanism test;
  removed-trade mean-R BY ENTRY SLOT as the anchor-contamination diagnostic
  (damage should concentrate in early slots if the mechanism is real).
- Data scope: NQ primary + ES sibling, clean Databento RTH history through 2026,
  post-lookback-90 eligible sessions. Historically CONSUMED sample — a survivor
  is at best a default-off forward-shadow candidate, never validated alpha.
- Multiple-testing note: 5-cell T grid; report the family-max and gate the Null-C
  on the family-best clearing the real metric (standing rule
  [[gate-nullc-on-success-metric]]). Prior: strong NO-GO lean — gap magnitude is
  a volatility proxy and the edge is volatility-INDEPENDENT (prop-challenge
  finding), so a magnitude gate likely removes days ~at random w.r.t. profit =
  the rarity filter this project keeps rediscovering. The one thing that could
  make it real and NOT just vol is the anchor-contamination mechanism, which is
  why the slot-localization + ES-transfer diagnostics are the load-bearing reads.

## Rationale and evidence

Separates a hard GATE ("don't trade on a gapped day") from the SCALING the user
already tried (EXP-0017, rejected as machinery). Distinct from EXP-0016 in three
ways: symmetric in gap sign (not gap-vs-signal), no RVOL term, and whole-day
(not per-signal). If the effect is real it is the mis-anchoring of the band/VWAP
on gap days, testable via early-slot concentration of removed-trade damage and
ES transfer; if it is machinery it dies to the matched-count random-day null.

## Outcome (EXP-0024, 2026-07-21)

REJECTED — cross-market NO-GO, fails inverted. Every T degrades net R AND daily
Sharpe on NQ and ES, monotone in days-skipped. Removed trades are profitable
(NQ removed meanR +0.206 vs kept +0.021; removed early +0.367R) — a big gap is a
directional-conviction open the momentum breakout rides, not mis-anchored noise;
the anchor-contamination mechanism is inverted. Matched-count random-day null:
random dropping beats gap-dropping (frac(rand≥real)=0.805 NQ / 0.870 ES) =
anti-selection, worse than a rarity filter. Real gate fails → no Null-C spent.
Retain unconditioned baseline. Evidence: `artifacts/runs/EXP-0024/review.md`.
