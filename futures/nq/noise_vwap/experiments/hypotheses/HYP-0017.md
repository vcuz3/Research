# HYP-0017 — A causal intraday Hurst-exponent (and its 1st/2nd derivative) regime filter improves the NQ continuous-stop baseline

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-07-21
- Origin idea: user — "add the Hurst exponent as a filter to trades; test the
  sensitivity of the Hurst exponent to Sharpe and expected R; test not just the
  raw Hurst but the ROC (1st derivative) and the 2nd derivative of Hurst."
- Builder: Claude (Opus 4.8)
- Proposed mechanism: the Noise-Area + VWAP entry is a momentum BREAKOUT. A
  breakout should continue when the intraday tape is PERSISTENT/trending
  (Hurst H > 0.5) and fail (snap back) when the tape is anti-persistent /
  mean-reverting (H < 0.5). So gating entries on a causal, intraday-to-date Hurst
  estimate should keep the trend-regime breakouts and drop the chop-regime ones,
  raising per-trade quality and Sharpe. The 1st derivative (ROC of H along the
  within-session decision clock) tests whether *rising* persistence — a regime
  that is turning trend — is the load-bearing state rather than the level; the
  2nd derivative tests whether the *acceleration* of persistence adds anything.
- Exact change: a per-signal ENTRY GATE (rule 18, stateful engine rerun via
  `entry_gate`, not a post-hoc trade drop). At every 30-min decision bar compute
  the generalized Hurst exponent (order-1 structure function, `E|X(t+τ)-X(t)| ∝
  τ^H`, log-log slope over lags τ∈{1,2,3,4,5,7,10,15,20} capped at n/2) of the
  session-to-date log-close path X (mfo 0 .. this decision bar; strictly causal,
  anchored at the same session open the band is built from). Derivatives are
  taken ALONG the within-session decision sequence: `dH = H_k − H_{k−1}`,
  `d2H = dH_k − dH_{k−1}` (NaN until enough same-day decisions exist). For a
  chosen signal S∈{H, dH, d2H}, threshold T and direction, keep only decision
  keys with S≥T ("high") or S≤T ("low"); the engine may open only at kept keys.
  Sweep T across the signal's distribution to trace Sharpe and expected net-R per
  trade vs T (the requested sensitivity). Baseline is unchanged.
- Frozen baseline: NQ continuous-stop baseline — `core.session.noise_bands`
  lookback 90, RTH, 30-minute decision clock, VWAP gate, every-bar band/VWAP
  stop, next-open fills, explicit per-instrument costs.
- Primary metric: full-sample zero-trade-session daily net-ATR-R Sharpe uplift
  over baseline at the family-best (signal, direction, threshold) cell, with
  total net R co-primary; expected net-R per trade reported alongside (the
  user's "expected R"). Family-max over the swept grid.
- Kill test: REJECT unless, at the family-best cell, full-sample daily Sharpe
  uplift ≥ +0.10 AND total net R ≥ baseline net R. If (and only if) that real
  gate passes, the uplift must additionally beat BOTH:
    (a) a 200-draw MATCHED-COUNT random-signal-drop null (keep the SAME number of
        candidate breakout signals at random) at frac(null ≥ real) < 0.05 — the
        rarity/variance-filter discriminator that killed every prior selectivity
        gate (KAMA regime, gap veto EXP-0024, RVOL veto EXP-0016), AND
    (b) 30 paired drift-preserving Null-C draws (recompute bands, Hurst, gate on
        the shuffled tape) at z ≥ 2 in net R.
  WEAKEN / treat as rarity filter if gross points-per-trade on the KEPT trades
  does NOT rise with the threshold (falling gross + rising Sharpe = cutting
  exposure, not information), or if the sign does not transfer to the ES sibling
  (a persistence-regime mechanism, being a structural property of the index tape,
  should transfer — cf. the EXP-0018/0020 sibling tests).
- Required controls: stateful engine rerun with `entry_gate` (identical fills,
  exits, costs, dates); causal Hurst (session-to-date, strictly ≤ decision bar);
  numba↔pure-python engine parity asserted on the ungated baseline; (a) 200
  matched-count random-signal-drop draws on the real tape; (b) 30 paired
  Null-C draws only if the real gate passes; ES sibling as the mechanism test;
  gross-per-trade on kept trades vs threshold as the selection-quality read.
- Data scope: NQ primary + ES sibling, clean Databento RTH history through 2026,
  post-lookback-90 eligible sessions. Historically CONSUMED sample — a survivor
  is at best a default-off forward-shadow candidate, never validated alpha.
- Multiple-testing note: 3 signals × 2 directions × ~7 thresholds = a searched
  family; report the family-max and gate the Null-C on the family-best cell that
  first clears the real metric (standing rule [[gate-nullc-on-success-metric]]).
  Strong NO-GO PRIOR: the project's edge is volatility-INDEPENDENT
  ([[nq-noise-vwap-prop-challenge]]) and every "more-selective → higher Sharpe"
  filter tested here has been a variance/rarity amplifier that dies to the
  matched-count and/or Null-C twin. A Hurst regime tag is another such candidate
  screen; the load-bearing reads are the matched-count random-drop null, the
  kept-trade gross/trade trend, and ES transfer. The one way it is real and not
  machinery is if HIGH-Hurst breakouts carry genuinely higher per-trade gross
  that transfers to ES.

## Rationale and evidence

The Hurst exponent is the canonical trend-vs-reversion regime statistic, so it is
a natural filter for a breakout system and a clean single-variable test of "only
break out when the tape is persistent." The three quantities the user asked for —
level, ROC, acceleration — are tested as a family with the family-max gated on
the standing +0.10 Sharpe / net-R rule, then the rarity-filter and Null-C twins.
Distinct from all prior filters here: this conditions on the *path geometry's
self-similarity* (a within-session, anchored-at-open estimate), not on gap,
volume, cross-market state, or exit cadence.
