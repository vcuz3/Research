# HYP-0014 — An asymmetric (per-side) noise band beats the symmetric mean band

- Status: rejected by `EXP-0022`
- Created: 2026-07-20
- Origin idea: transform 3 (anchor/symmetry) of the user's noise-area recast
  programme (cone → quantile → asymmetric); range/EWMA transform dropped by user.
  This is the final and only remaining lever after EXP-0020 (cone) and EXP-0021
  (quantile) established the dispersion is fully summarized by its per-slot LEVEL.
- Builder: Claude (Opus 4.8)
- Proposed mechanism: The baseline sizes BOTH band edges from one symmetric
  statistic, sigma = mean(|move|), pooling up-day and down-day displacements. On a
  drift-asymmetric index (NQ up-days are larger and more frequent) the two half-
  distributions differ, so the pooled width mis-sizes at least one edge: it
  over-widens the down edge (built partly from larger up-day magnitudes) and/or
  under-widens the up edge. Sizing each edge to its OWN causal semi-dispersion may
  select better entries.
- Exact change: swap ONLY the band construction —
  `core/session.py::noise_bands` (sig on both sides) vs
  `core/bands.py::noise_bands_asymmetric(tilt)`, which sets
  `sig_up = sigma + tilt*(2*up - sigma)`, `sig_dn = sigma + tilt*(2*dn - sigma)`
  where `up = mean(max(move,0))`, `dn = mean(max(-move,0))` over the prior
  `lookback` sessions (`up + dn == sigma` identically). Same lookback 90,
  `min_periods=90` (coverage identical), engine, VWAP gate, 30-min clock, every-bar
  stop, next-open fills, costs. tilt-grid {0.5, 1.0, 1.5}; tilt=0 is baseline
  bit-exact.
- Frozen baseline: `core/session.py::noise_bands`, lookback 90.
- Primary metric: zero-trade-day daily net-ATR-R Sharpe uplift of the best tilt
  over the mean baseline, NQ primary + ES sibling.
- Kill test: Reject if the best tilt's Sharpe uplift is below +0.10 on NQ or net R
  falls below baseline. Null C only if the gate passes.
- No width-dial confound: unlike the quantile study, no RAW-vs-MATCHED split is
  needed — the total half-width budget is conserved for EVERY tilt
  (`sig_up + sig_dn == 2*sigma`), so the change is a PURE up/down REDISTRIBUTION,
  never a capacity dial.
- Required controls: total-half-width conservation (proved in `tests/test_bands.py`,
  so the effect cannot be the EXP-0010/0012/0021 width dial); the per-side residual
  sig_up/sigma, sig_dn/sigma by decision slot (is the tape asymmetric, and does it
  transfer to ES?); gross and net; trade count; ES sibling; tilt dose-response.
- Data scope: NQ primary + ES secondary clean Databento RTH through 2026; consumed
  data; first 90 eligible sessions removed.

## Rationale and evidence

The two prior transforms attacked the |move| distribution's SHAPE (cone: its
intraday time profile; quantile: its per-slot distributional shape) and both were
inert/harmful — the dispersion is summarized by its per-slot LEVEL. This transform
attacks a DIFFERENT dimension the LEVEL cannot capture: the up/down SYMMETRY of the
band. The width-conservation construction isolates the redistribution cleanly.

## Result

Rejected by `EXP-0022` on both markets. Every tilt fails the gate:
- NQ: monotonically WORSE in tilt — dSharpe −0.065 (t0.5), −0.095 (t1.0), −0.110
  (t1.5); best tilt 0.5 also cuts netR −5.4R. No Null C (gate failed).
- ES: best tilt 1.0 dSharpe +0.023 / dNetR +1.9R (far below +0.10); tilt=1.5
  collapses (dSharpe −0.093, maxDD 7.0→12.9R). No Null C.

Decisive diagnostic: the per-side residual is a real, cross-market-transferring
asymmetry but a SMALL one — on BOTH markets the up-edge is ~2–5% wider than
symmetric and the down-edge ~2–5% narrower (up/dn ≈ 1.03–1.10), consistently across
ALL 13 decision slots, largest mid-day (mfo 329–359). That up-tilt is exactly the
index's up-drift showing in the up semi-mean. But acting on it via band width HURTS
on NQ: widening the up-threshold rejects the drift-driven up-breakouts the momentum
system rides, while narrowing the down-threshold admits more counter-drift down-
breaks. The symmetric band is not merely adequate — it is actively better than
sizing to the empirical up/down asymmetry, because that asymmetry is a DRIFT
property the strategy already monetizes (VWAP gate + ride-to-close); re-sizing the
band to it double-counts and mis-selects entries.

Synthesis (transforms 1–3 complete): neither a √t reshape (cone), a distributional
reshape (quantile), nor an up/down redistribution (asymmetric) adds risk-adjusted
value. The noise-area band is fully summarized by its per-slot SYMMETRIC LEVEL/width.
The recast programme is exhausted; retain the faithful mean band.
