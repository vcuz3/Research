# HYP-0008 — A fixed gap-against low-RVOL veto improves the NQ continuous-stop baseline

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-07-19
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: Breakouts opposing the overnight gap need contemporaneous participation; below-baseline signal-minute volume identifies failures while preserving aligned or high-volume breakouts.
- Exact change: Veto an otherwise valid entry when side times open_gap is below zero and signal-minute RVOL versus the strictly prior 90-session same-minute baseline is below 1.2; one frozen rule, no threshold search.
- Frozen baseline: NQ continuous-stop baseline: lookback 90, RTH, 30-minute decisions, VWAP gate, every-bar stop, next-open fills.
- Primary metric: Paired full-pipeline net-ATR-R uplift over baseline, compared with the paired Null-C uplift distribution; daily zero-trade-session Sharpe is co-primary.
- Kill test: Reject unless full-sample net R and daily Sharpe both improve, at least 90 percent of baseline trades remain, and net-R uplift beats 30 paired Null-C draws at z>=2; weaken if uplift is not positive in both pre-2023 and 2023+ eras.
- Required controls: Stateful engine rerun with entry_gate; identical fills, exits, costs, and dates; causal prior-90 same-minute RVOL; 30 paired corrected Null-C draws recomputing bands, RVOL, signals, gates, and trades.
- Data scope: NQ clean Databento RTH history through 2026, post-lookback-90 eligible sessions; historically consumed discovery sample.
- Multiple-testing note: One fixed rule derived from EXP-0015. No threshold grid. Historical result remains discovery-confirmatory hybrid and requires future shadow evidence.

## Rationale and evidence

EXP-0015 found flat to negative expectancy where the breakout opposed the gap and RVOL was low or middle; fixed economic thresholds avoid recycling full-sample quantile boundaries as causal parameters.
