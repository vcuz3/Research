# HYP-0016 — A surround-smoothed, short-history noise band beats the lb90 mean band

- Status: rejected by `EXP-0025`
- Created: 2026-07-21
- Origin idea: user proposal. The baseline noise area only looks at the average
  |move| at exactly `mfo` historically; price does not move on a fixed clock, so a
  10:00 estimate ignores the surrounding 9:58/10:02 displacement. The 90-day average
  smooths that but is lagging / slow to adapt to recent volatility. Redefine the
  per-slot dispersion as the average over a local minute neighbourhood on each prior
  day, then average that over a SHORTER history — the neighbourhood pooling should
  cut estimator variance enough to make a short, adaptive history usable.
- Builder: Claude (Opus 4.8)
- Proposed mechanism: the baseline `sigma[d,mfo]` averages ONE thin same-time-of-day
  sample (`|move|` at exactly `mfo`) over the prior 90 sessions — a high-variance
  per-slot estimator, hence the long lagging window. Pooling the local minute window
  `[mfo-w, mfo+w]` on each prior day before averaging over history reduces the
  estimator variance, so a short (more adaptive to recent vol) history becomes
  usable and per-trade selection should improve.
- Exact change: swap ONLY the band construction —
  `core/session.py::noise_bands(bars, 90)` vs
  `core/bands.py::noise_bands_surround(bars, hist, w)`:
  `smooth_move[p, mfo] = mean_{j in [mfo-w, mfo+w]} |close[p,j]/open[p,0]-1|`
  (center-included, `2w+1` minutes, edge-truncated so `mfo==0` averages forward-only),
  then `sigma[d, mfo] = mean over the prior hist sessions of smooth_move[·, mfo]`.
  The forward minutes come only from strictly-prior completed sessions (`shift(1)`),
  so there is NO lookahead. Same engine, VWAP gate, 30-min RTH clock, every-bar
  (continuous) stop, next-open fills, costs. `w=0` reduces to `noise_bands(bars, hist)`
  exactly (proved in `tests/test_bands.py`).
- Grid: surround half-width `w ∈ {5, 14, 30}` × history `hist ∈ {5, 14, 30}`
  (9 surround cells) + `plain(hist)` = `noise_bands(bars, hist)` for each hist
  (isolates the short-history effect from the surround-smoothing effect).
- Frozen baseline: `core/session.py::noise_bands`, lookback 90 (continuous stop).
- Primary metric: zero-trade-day daily net-ATR-R Sharpe uplift of the best surround
  cell over the lb90 mean baseline, on the common post-lb90 date set, NQ primary +
  ES sibling. (User priority: per-trade quality + Sharpe; trade-count reduction is
  acceptable.)
- Kill test: Reject if the best surround cell's Sharpe uplift is below +0.10 on NQ
  or net R falls below baseline. Null C only if the gate passes.
- Required controls: `plain(hist)` rows (short history without surround) to attribute
  any effect; median band-WIDTH ratio vs baseline (a Sharpe move that is really the
  EXP-0021 width/capacity dial is not an edge); gross-per-trade AND net_pt/trade
  (the user's quality metric); trade count; ES sibling; causality + `w=0`-reduction
  proved in tests.
- Data scope: NQ primary + ES secondary clean Databento RTH through 2026; consumed
  data; common post-lb90 date set (first 90 eligible sessions removed so every cell
  is scored on the identical sample).

## Rationale and evidence

Distinct from the EXP-0020/0021/0022 recast programme, which changed the dispersion
PROFILE SHAPE (time profile / distributional shape / up-down symmetry) at fixed long
history and found the band is summarized by its per-slot symmetric LEVEL. This
hypothesis instead changes the ESTIMATOR of that level — its bias/variance tradeoff
via neighbour pooling, and its recency via history length — a genuinely new axis
(temporal adaptivity), not a profile reshape.

## Result

Rejected by `EXP-0025` on both markets — the gate fails on NQ and ES.

- NQ: every cell is WORSE than baseline on Sharpe. Baseline Sh 1.29 / net_pt/t
  3.159. Best surround cell h30 w30: Sh 1.20 (dSharpe −0.083), net_pt/t 3.152,
  netR −4.9R. Shortening history alone (plain h30/h14/h5: 1.22/1.04/1.07) is the
  dominant loss; surround smoothing does not recover it.
- ES: best surround cell h14 w30 dSharpe +0.029 (far below +0.10) but with gross/
  trade FALLING (−0.081) and +179 more trades at wRatio 0.93 — i.e. the tiny blip is
  the width/capacity dial, not the mechanism, and it does not transfer to NQ.

Decisive diagnostics:
1. Per-trade quality (net_pt/trade) — the user's headline metric — never rises above
   baseline on either market. NQ baseline 3.159 is the maximum of the whole grid.
2. The variance-reduction mechanism IS real but only matters once the history is
   crippled: at the shortest history the surround pooling adds Sharpe (NQ h5 plain
   1.07 → h5 w30 1.13, +0.056; ES h30 plain 0.59 → h30 w30 0.67, +0.08) — yet it
   never climbs back to the lb90 baseline. The long 90-day same-slot history is
   already the better bias/variance operating point; the every-bar continuous stop
   already supplies the current-session volatility adaptivity the short history was
   meant to add, so the band only needs a stable long-run per-slot LEVEL.
3. wRatio 0.89–0.99: surround/short bands are slightly narrower → trade a bit more
   with flat-to-lower per-trade quality = mild capacity dial, not alpha.

No Null C spent (real pass fails the primary metric on both markets — standing rule).
Retain `core/session.py::noise_bands` at lookback 90; `noise_bands_surround` stays in
`core/bands.py` as the tested surround-estimator benchmark.
