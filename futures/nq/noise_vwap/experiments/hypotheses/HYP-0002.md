# HYP-0002 — One-second first-touch exits preserve the GO continuous-stop edge

- Status: accepted by `EXP-0009` (preservation only; no improvement claim).
  QUALIFIED by `EXP-0031`: preservation holds only at ~instantaneous fills;
  under a conservative >=1s execution latency the 1s first-touch continuous stop
  falls >0.15 below the 1m continuous baseline (fails the kill test) though it
  stays net-positive. First-touch is not an execution upgrade once latency is
  priced. See `artifacts/runs/EXP-0031/review.md`.
- Created: 2026-07-18
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: replacing a close-confirmed, next-minute-open stop with a
  causal stop-market order at the first one-second touch should reduce exit delay
  without materially changing the underlying Noise Area/VWAP timing edge.
- Exact change: keep the faithful 30-minute entry clock and entry fills unchanged;
  activate the stop level derived from the prior completed one-minute bar, detect
  the first one-second OHLC touch, and fill gap-aware at the stop or worse. Compare
  stop levels refreshed only after decision bars (baseline) and after every minute
  (continuous stop).
- Frozen baseline: lookback 90, RTH, Concretum 30-minute entry clock, VWAP gate,
  raw NQ continuous contract, no overlapping position, forced RTH-close flat.
- Primary metric: zero-eligible-day one-contract daily net Sharpe for the
  every-minute-refreshed one-second first-touch variant at 0.5 tick slippage per
  side plus $2.25 fees per side, compared with the existing one-minute
  close/next-open continuous-stop result on the common eligible sample.
- Kill test: reject preservation if first-touch Sharpe is more than 0.15 below the
  one-minute continuous-stop Sharpe or if its net daily mean is non-positive.
- Required controls: reproduce existing one-minute baseline and continuous-stop
  trades; decision-only stop refresh on the same one-second engine; 0.25/0.5/1.0
  tick slippage sensitivity; gap-through and prior-bar activation invariant tests;
  coverage/alignment audit against the clean one-minute data.
- Data scope: `futures/data/databento/NQ_ohlcv-1s_NQv0_20100606_20260717.parquet`,
  intersected with clean eligible NQ RTH sessions after the 90-session warm-up
  through 2026-07-15. All history is consumed research data.
- Multiple-testing note: one prespecified execution change and one primary
  slippage assumption; cost sensitivities and decision-refresh results are
  controls, not separately selected candidates.

## Rationale and evidence

The current every-bar stop observes the completed one-minute close and trades at
the next one-minute open. A live stop-market order can react earlier, but its level
must not use the current incomplete minute's VWAP or band. Lagging the level by one
completed minute makes the touch test causal while the one-second OHLC feed makes
the trigger and gap-through fill observable at materially finer resolution.
