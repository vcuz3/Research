# HYP-0012 — Recasting the Noise Area as an analytic diffusion cone (√t) matches or beats the empirical per-slot band

- Status: rejected by `EXP-0020`
- Created: 2026-07-20
- Origin idea: user asked how to transform the paper-4824172 noise-area concept in
  a different way to how it is currently calculated; chosen programme = staged
  band re-castings, diffusion cone first.
- Builder: Claude (Opus 4.8)
- Proposed mechanism: Under a driftless random walk, expected absolute displacement
  from the RTH open grows as √(elapsed). The faithful band re-estimates that shape
  independently at each of the 13 decision slots from thin same-slot samples. If
  intraday displacement is well described by pure diffusion, one causal per-session
  volatility scalar times a √t profile should reproduce (or improve on) the band
  while (a) collapsing 90×13 parameters to ~1 and (b) structurally removing the
  Rule-9a per-slot `min_periods` coverage deletion.
- Exact change: swap ONLY the band construction. Baseline =
  `core/session.py::noise_bands` (per-slot empirical mean-|move|). Treatment =
  `core/bands.py::noise_bands_cone`: `sigma[d,mfo] = sigma_session[d]·√(mfo/M)`,
  where `sigma_session[d]` is the mean over the prior 90 sessions of the
  full-session displacement `|session_close/session_open − 1|` (open at mfo 0, last
  bar's close). Identical gap adjustment (`max/min(open, prior_close)`), engine,
  VWAP gate, 30-min decision clock, every-bar stop, next-open fills, and costs. For
  RTH the session close is the mfo==M bar, so `sigma_session` equals the baseline's
  FINAL-slot empirical sigma → the cone matches baseline width at end of day and the
  test isolates intraday SHAPE, not width.
- Frozen baseline: `core/session.py::noise_bands`, lookback 90, the current champion
  config.
- Primary metric: zero-trade-day daily net-ATR-R Sharpe uplift of the cone over the
  empirical baseline on the common post-lookback-90 sample, on NQ (primary) and ES.
- Kill test: Reject if the cone's Sharpe uplift is below +0.10 on NQ, or if net R
  falls below baseline. A near-WASH (|ΔSharpe| ≤ 0.05) with net R ≥ baseline AND
  improved decision-point coverage would instead be an ADOPT-AS-SIMPLIFICATION
  outcome (robustness, not alpha). Per the standing rule the drift-preserving Null C
  runs only if the alpha gate passes.
- Required controls: common sample; gross and net; trade count; the residual
  m(mfo) = mean_empirical_sigma / mean_cone_sigma per decision slot (does the
  empirical shape carry structure beyond √t?); decision-point coverage baseline vs
  cone; ES sibling market.
- Data scope: NQ primary and ES secondary clean Databento RTH history through 2026;
  consumed data; first 90 eligible sessions removed.

## Rationale and evidence

The cone is the null hypothesis of the whole noise-area apparatus: "is any per-slot
empirical shape needed, or is the band just a random-walk envelope?" Three outcomes
were pre-specified: cone ≈ empirical → adopt as a coverage-bug-killing
simplification; empirical > cone → the residual m(mfo) is real intraday vol
seasonality and becomes the target for later transforms; cone > empirical + clears
Null C → a genuine shape edge.

## Result

Rejected by `EXP-0020` on both markets. The cone is worse, not equal: NQ ΔSharpe
−0.101, ΔnetR −1.4R, +705 trades; ES ΔSharpe −0.125, ΔnetR −7.5R, +436 trades.
Because the real pass fails the primary metric, no Null C was spent (standing rule).

The residual m(mfo) diagnoses exactly why and is the study's positive finding: on
BOTH NQ and ES the empirical band is ~1.35–1.55× WIDER than the √t cone in the first
hour (mfo 29–59) and converges to 1.0 at the close by construction. Real intraday
displacement is SUPER-DIFFUSIVE in the morning (an opening-volatility bulge wider
than a random walk). The end-of-day-calibrated cone is therefore too narrow early,
admits noisier morning breakouts, and dilutes per-trade quality (net pt/trade NQ
3.159→2.530). The per-slot empirical shape is load-bearing — specifically the
morning — and that structure transfers across the sibling pair, so it is a real
property of the index tape, not NQ-specific machinery.

Coverage: on liquid NQ/ES the cone recovers only 90 decision points (0.2%, all at
mfo 209 ≈ 13:29 ET). The Rule-9a coverage win is real but negligible here because
the bug bites THIN markets (GC/YM/RTY), not liquid index futures — consistent with
the prior learning. Retain the empirical per-slot band.
