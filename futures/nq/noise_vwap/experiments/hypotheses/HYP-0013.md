# HYP-0013 — A quantile (percentile) noise band beats the mean band at matched width

- Status: rejected by `EXP-0021`
- Created: 2026-07-20
- Origin idea: transform 2 of the user's noise-area recast programme (cone →
  quantile → asymmetric); range/EWMA transform dropped by user.
- Builder: Claude (Opus 4.8)
- Proposed mechanism: The baseline per-slot dispersion is the MEAN of |move|, which
  is pulled up by a few outlier trend days in the trailing 90-session window,
  inflating the band and suppressing entries. A qth PERCENTILE is robust to those
  outliers, so at equal width a quantile-shaped band (which flexes day-to-day with
  the sample's skew) might select better entries than the mean.
- Exact change: swap ONLY the per-slot statistic —
  `sigma[d,mfo] = quantile_q(|move[d-i,mfo]|) · scale` in
  `core/bands.py::noise_bands_quantile` vs the baseline mean
  (`core/session.py::noise_bands`). Same lookback 90, `min_periods=90` (so coverage
  is identical), engine, VWAP gate, 30-min clock, every-bar stop, next-open fills,
  costs. Because it stays a per-slot rolling statistic it PRESERVES the empirical
  intraday shape (incl. the super-diffusive morning EXP-0020 found the cone missed).
- Frozen baseline: `core/session.py::noise_bands`, lookback 90.
- Primary metric: zero-trade-day daily net-ATR-R Sharpe uplift of the best
  MATCHED-width q cell over the mean baseline, NQ primary + ES sibling. Two views:
  RAW q-grid (exposes the width/capacity dial) and MATCHED-width (each q rescaled by
  a fixed constant so median sigma == mean band; isolates distribution SHAPE).
- Kill test: Reject if the best MATCHED-width cell's Sharpe uplift is below +0.10 on
  NQ or net R falls below baseline. Null C only if the matched gate passes. Any
  "improvement" that appears only in the RAW view is the width/capacity dial (known
  non-alpha), not a quantile effect.
- Required controls: RAW vs MATCHED separation; the residual quant/mean by decision
  slot (does the quantile actually reshape the profile, or is it a rescaled mean?);
  gross and net; trade count; ES sibling.
- Data scope: NQ primary + ES secondary clean Databento RTH through 2026; consumed
  data; first 90 eligible sessions removed. q-grid {0.50, 0.65, 0.80, 0.90}.

## Rationale and evidence

Distinguishes two things the mean/quantile choice conflates: (1) the band's overall
LEVEL/width (a capacity dial the project already knows is non-alpha), and (2) the
band's day-to-day response to outlier trend days (robustness), which is the only
mechanism a quantile could add. The MATCHED-width comparison neutralizes (1) to test
(2) directly.

## Result

Rejected by `EXP-0021` on both markets. At matched median width the quantile band is
essentially the mean band: best MATCHED cell (q=0.90) NQ ΔSharpe +0.032 / ΔnetR
+1.4R, ES ΔSharpe +0.067 / ΔnetR +5.2R — both below the +0.10 gate → no Null C.

Decisive diagnostic: the residual quant/mean by decision slot is ≈1.0 everywhere
(0.96–1.01) on both markets — the matched quantile band is just the mean band
RESCALED, with no reshaping of the intraday profile. The per-slot distribution's
SHAPE carries no exploitable information beyond its LEVEL. The only "win" is in the
RAW view (q0.50, narrower than the mean, trades more and books more NQ drift: netR
101.9 vs 91.2, Sh 1.34) — that is the width/capacity dial (a narrower band = more
exposure), not a quantile effect, and it is already understood as non-alpha.

Combined with EXP-0020 (cone): the noise-area dispersion is fully summarized by its
per-slot LEVEL/width; neither a √t reshape (cone) nor a distributional reshape
(quantile) adds risk-adjusted value. Retain the mean band.
