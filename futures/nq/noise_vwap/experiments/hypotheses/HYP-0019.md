# HYP-0019 — Matched-exposure Hurst risk allocation

- Status: rejected (EXP-0028)
- Created: 2026-07-21
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: EXP-0026 found monotonically higher expected return per trade at high entry H, but a hard gate lost exposure; fixed continuous weights may monetize quality without deleting trades.
- Exact change: At each baseline trade entry, assign 0.75 risk units when causal entry H is below 0.5 and 1.25 when H is at least 0.5, then causally normalize using only the expanding prior distribution of defined weights so expected deployed weight is 1.0; retain undefined-H trades at 1.0. Entries, exits, trade population, and costs remain unchanged.
- Frozen baseline: Continuous-stop NQ/ES baseline from EXP-0026/0027, one risk unit per trade.
- Primary metric: Zero-trade-day daily net-ATR-R Sharpe uplift with matched realized aggregate exposure; co-primary total net R and max drawdown.
- Kill test: Reject unless both NQ and ES have positive Sharpe uplift, neither loses more than 1 percent total net R, pooled equal-weight NQ/ES Sharpe improves at least 0.10, and max drawdown does not worsen; run allocation-label null only if the real cross-market gate passes.
- Required controls: Unweighted baseline; exposure reconciliation; identical trade population; inverted weights; 200-draw within-instrument H-label permutation conditional on passing the real gate.
- Data scope: Consumed 2011-2026 NQ and ES RTH history; exploratory only, future shadow required for confirmation.
- Multiple-testing note: One fixed natural threshold H=0.5 and one fixed 0.75/1.25 schedule; inverted schedule is a control, not selectable.

## Rationale and evidence

This is the only materially distinct Hurst use left: risk allocation rather than entry deletion or exit conditioning.

## Outcome (EXP-0028, 2026-07-22)

REJECTED. Fixed causal 0.75/1.25 Hurst sizing increased net R on both markets
(NQ 91.20->95.30R; ES 51.47->56.68R) but did not improve risk-adjusted
performance: NQ Sharpe fell 1.288->1.265, ES rose only 0.698->0.702, and the
equal-risk pool gained just +0.002 Sharpe versus the required +0.10. Maximum
drawdown worsened on both (NQ 4.70->5.15R; ES 7.45->8.65R). The inverted control
lost Sharpe/net R, confirming that high H ranks return correctly, but the proper
allocation buys return by concentrating tail risk rather than adding alpha.
Real gate failed, so the 200-draw allocation-label null was not run. Combined
with EXP-0026 and EXP-0027, Hurst has now failed as selection, exit management,
and sizing; close the historical branch and retain equal sizing.
