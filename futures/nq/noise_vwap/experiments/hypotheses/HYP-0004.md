# HYP-0004 — The paper two-stage RR ladder exit improves NQ risk-adjusted returns over the band stop

- Status: rejected by `EXP-0011`
- Created: 2026-07-19
- Origin idea: `IDEA-0003` (paper 5095349 section 4.5, ladder stop-loss & take-profit)
- Builder: Claude (Opus 4.8)
- Proposed mechanism: A two-stage risk-multiple ladder banks part of a mature move
  and ratchets the remaining stop to breakeven, so a position that runs is not
  given back on a dip to the noise band (paper Figure 2) yet a failed breakout is
  cut at a fixed risk. This may raise risk-adjusted return over the current
  trailing band/VWAP stop and over the single-partial `tp1.0_50` operating point.
- Exact change: Hold the working NQ baseline entry fixed — lookback 90, RTH,
  30-minute decision clock, VWAP entry gate, next-open fills, explicit costs — and
  replace the every-bar band/VWAP stop with the paper's Table-2 two-step ladder:
  Step 0 stop -1R / take-profit +2R, bank 50%, Step 1 stop 0R (breakeven) /
  take-profit +5R, remainder exits at the +5R target or the daily time flat. 1R is
  the entry-frozen distance from the fill to the band-or-VWAP stop reference (the
  strategy's intrinsic per-trade risk), NOT ATR or ticks. The ladder replaces the
  band stop and the flip; entries are unchanged. Implemented default-off in the
  audited `core/engine2.py` (`ladder`, `ladder_levels`, `ladder_frac`); parity of
  the ladder-off path with `core.engine` is re-verified.
- Frozen baseline: current champion `stop_ref="both"` every-bar band/VWAP stop,
  lookback 90, 30m clock, VWAP gate, next-open fills. `tp1.0_50` (tp_atr=1.0,
  tp_frac=0.5) is reported as the bar-to-beat.
- Primary metric: zero-trade-day daily net-ATR-R Sharpe uplift of the ladder over
  the `both` baseline on the common post-lookback-90 sample, evaluated against the
  corrected path-preserving Null C uplift distribution.
- Kill test: Reject if the ladder Sharpe uplift over `both` is below +0.10, if
  ladder net R falls below the `both` baseline, or if the Null C upper-tail p
  exceeds 0.05. Materially weaken if the ladder does not at least match the
  `tp1.0_50` Sharpe, if the sign does not transfer to ES, or if next-open vs
  signal-close fill sensitivity exceeds the uplift.
- Required controls: common sample; gross and net; trade count and top-decile
  winner-R retention; per-era (2023+) delta; NQ next-open vs signal-close fill
  sensitivity; ES sibling market; `tp1.0_50` reference arm; corrected
  path-preserving Null C rerunning band construction, all arms, execution, costs,
  metrics, and the uplift statistic on each draw.
- Data scope: primary NQ and secondary ES clean Databento RTH history through
  2026; all history is consumed discovery data; the first 90 eligible sessions are
  removed for every arm so the comparison is on an identical sample.
- Multiple-testing note: One prespecified ladder (the paper's Table-2 example),
  one confirmatory uplift contrast vs the deployed `both` baseline; no family-max
  correction is needed. The `tp1.0_50` arm is a descriptive reference, not a second
  confirmatory test. The ladder levels are taken from the paper's illustrative
  example, NOT its in-sample-optimized Table-11 values, to avoid importing the
  paper's QQQ search.

## Rationale and evidence

Paper 5095349 reports its strongest in-sample QQQ Sharpe for ladder exits
(Table 7, Ladder #2 Sharpe 3.35), but those levels come from a full-sample Optuna
search with no holdout (see the 2026-07-18 review) and the paper's own Table 8
shows the upper take-profit is often unreachable, collapsing into a time exit.
This test isolates the ladder STRUCTURE (not the paper's fitted numbers) on NQ/ES
futures with entries frozen and gates it on a full-pipeline Null C, and requires
it to clear the project's existing `tp1.0_50` survivor.

## Result

Rejected by `EXP-0011`. NQ ladder ΔSharpe −0.075 / ΔnetR −12.9 vs `both` (fails
the +0.10 gate) and loses to `tp1.0_50` by −0.131 Sharpe; Null C p=0.6129. ES
ΔSharpe +0.014 / ΔnetR −4.2, Null C p=0.3226. The paper's Table-2 ladder cuts
gross R (111→97): the fixed −1R stop and +5R cap discard the trailing band/VWAP
stop's edge, matching the paper's own unreachable-TP finding (Table 8). Retain the
`both` stop; keep `tp1.0_50` as the only validated exit tweak. See
`../../artifacts/runs/EXP-0011/review.md`.
