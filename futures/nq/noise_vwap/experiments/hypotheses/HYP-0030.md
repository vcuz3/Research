# HYP-0030 — Trend-percentile hysteresis reduces one-day regime flips without making the causal 10x504 state model too sticky

- Status: completed (EXP-0042) — PASS for label calibration. A 7.5-percentile-point
  trend buffer is the smallest setting that clears every frozen stability gate.
- Created: 2026-07-27
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: A buffer around the chop/weak/trend percentile boundaries prevents small day-to-day moves from repeatedly changing labels while still allowing large moves to change state immediately.
- Exact change: Freeze EXP-0041 i10_c504 indicators and volatility labels. Sweep trend hysteresis h={0,0.025,0.05,0.075,0.10}; seed the first valid state with raw terciles; allow adjacent state changes at 1/3+/-h and 2/3+/-h. Select the smallest h that clears all label-quality gates. P&L excluded from selection.
- Frozen baseline: EXP-0041 i10_c504 raw trend terciles: median dwell 2 sessions, singleton rate 17.1%.
- Primary metric: Smallest hysteresis buffer clearing median trend dwell>=3, singleton rate<=10%, max annual trend-bucket share<=60%, minimum 12-cell coverage>=150 days, and causal prefix parity.
- Kill test: NO-GO if no h clears every label-quality gate. Do not relax gates or select on strategy Sharpe. If multiple pass, choose the smallest h.
- Required controls: h=0 exact baseline parity; future-mutation and prefix causality; annual occupancy; transition counts; label disagreement versus raw; P&L and recent-year stability only after selection.
- Data scope: NQ clean Databento RTH 2011-12-08 through 2026-07-14; EXP-0041 common 3173-session sample; consumed history.
- Multiple-testing note: Selection is label-only and deterministic. Any later alpha claim based on the selected labels needs its own complete Null-C pipeline; descriptive P&L here cannot promote a trading rule.

## Rationale and evidence

EXP-0041 repaired volatility calibration but its trend labels still had two-session median dwell and 17.1% singletons. User approved a small causal hysteresis sweep.

## Result

- `h=0.075` is the only complete pass. It raises median trend-state duration from
  2 to 3 sessions, cuts one-day labels from 17.1% to 7.4%, and reduces transitions
  from 1,201 to 872 while keeping maximum annual state share at 43.5% and minimum
  12-cell coverage at 152 days.
- `h=0.050` remains slightly too noisy (10.34% singletons versus <=10% gate).
- `h=0.100` is too sticky (minimum cell coverage 143 versus >=150 gate).
- The selected label differs from the raw label on 12.9% of days, never for more than
  four consecutive sessions, and passes causal prefix parity.
- P&L was not used to choose the buffer. Descriptively, full-sample chop/weak/trend
  Sharpe becomes 0.46/1.79/0.83 versus 0.88/1.48/0.76 raw. This is cleaner grouping,
  not a trading rule or alpha claim; no Null C was run.
- Evidence: `artifacts/runs/EXP-0042/`; reproduce with
  `python -u -m futures.nq.noise_vwap.scripts.hyp_0030_trend_hysteresis`.
