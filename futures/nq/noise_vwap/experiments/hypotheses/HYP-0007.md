# HYP-0007 — The fixed EXP-0012 ES exit-band operating point delivers consistent implementation utility

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-07-19
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: A looser exit suppresses low-quality re-entry and turnover, potentially improving net return and drawdown even without predictive alpha.
- Exact change: On ES only, compare the frozen s=0.5 y=0.5 exit band against the both-stop baseline; do not re-optimize parameters.
- Frozen baseline: ES both stop, lookback 90, 30-minute RTH decisions, VWAP entry gate, next-open fills.
- Primary metric: Consistency of net-ATR-R and max drawdown improvement across full, 2020+, 2023+, and 2025+ eras, supported by cost-stressed and vol-targeted portfolio metrics.
- Kill test: Reject production-default adoption if treatment fails to improve both net R and max drawdown on the full sample, or if fewer than 3 of 4 predefined eras improve both; weaken if the benefit disappears at 0.5 tick per side or materially worsens worst-day/weekly tail loss.
- Required controls: Frozen EXP-0012 cell s0.5_y0.5; identical entries, data, fill timing, fees, and eligible zero-trade sessions; costs at 0.25, 0.5, and 1.0 tick per side; paired daily comparison.
- Data scope: ES clean Databento RTH history through 2026, common post-lookback-90 sample.
- Multiple-testing note: No parameter search; one frozen treatment selected by EXP-0012. Four eras are reported as consistency diagnostics under a predeclared 3-of-4 rule.

## Rationale and evidence

EXP-0012 rejected an alpha interpretation but showed large ES turnover, net-R, and Sharpe gains. This test evaluates whether those mechanics are sufficiently consistent and drawdown-improving to justify implementation.
