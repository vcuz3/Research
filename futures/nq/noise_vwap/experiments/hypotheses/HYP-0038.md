## Mechanism

An adverse spike (the move that trips a band+VWAP stop) partially retraces over
the next 1–5 min — DIAG Q1 exit mirror: mean side-signed post-stop forward move
+0.0005 R at 1 min → +0.0050 R at 30 min, ~53% positive, roughly uniform. That
retrace is not tradable as a directional bet (too small, drawdown-costly), but a
fill that would otherwise cross the spread at `next_open` can instead lean
PASSIVE into the retrace and capture the improvement as a better average fill
price. Execution savings do not carry position risk, so this sidesteps the
Sharpe bar the EXP-0047 overlay failed.

Entry vs exit asymmetry is load-bearing (DIAG Q1): entries fire on a FAVOURABLE
breakout whose payoff is continuation (momentum, mean-in-the-tails), so a passive
entry that only fills on a pullback ADVERSELY SELECTS — it misses the deep breaks
that run away, which ARE the edge (LEARNINGS §4/§1). Therefore the scheduler is
scoped to EXITS (aligned with the post-spike reversion); the ENTRY arm is a
built-in PLACEBO — if passive entries "save" money too, the effect is generic
spread capture, not the reversion, and the whole thing is a fill-model artifact.

## Exact change

No `core/engine2.py` signal/accounting change. A post-processing execution layer
re-prices the deployed clock book's ALREADY-DECIDED exit fills against the 1s
path, comparing three policies on the identical trade set (same signals, same
holding, same exit trigger bar):

- **Cross (baseline):** `next_open` marketable — the current fill model.
- **Passive-into-retrace (test):** post a limit at `fill_ref ∓ g·σ_slot` on the
  favourable side; credit a fill only if the 1s path trades THROUGH it by the
  vol-scaled guard (RULES A4 — a touch is not a fill); if unfilled within a
  K-minute window, CHASE at the actual traded price at window end (worse of).
- **Touch ceiling (diagnostic only):** credit a bare touch (`g=0`) — the
  NON-capturable upper bound, to bound the effect (LEARNINGS §6 touch-vs-fill).

Arms: (1) EXIT fills only (the thesis). (2) ENTRY fills only (the placebo —
expected ≤0 net of chase after adverse selection). Guard `g` and window `K` are
swept for DISCOVERY, then ONE `(g,K)` fixed a priori for the confirmatory read.

## Primary metric & split

Mean per-trade EXECUTION improvement (test − cross) on the same trade set, in R
AND points AND ticks, gross and NET of chase cost, with a per-DAY cluster-robust
SE. This is a fill-cost delta on a fixed trade set — it isolates fill quality and
is NOT a strategy-Sharpe claim. Report the distribution, by slot, by era. TRAIN
first 60% / TEST last 40% of common sessions (as EXP-0035/0047).

## Kill test (NQ TEST — ALL must hold, else REJECT)

1. The guarded passive EXIT policy yields a POSITIVE mean per-trade improvement
   NET of chase, per-day cluster-robust CI excluding zero, on TEST.
2. The effect PERSISTS at a deployable guard (`g ≥ 0.10–0.25 σ_slot`) — it must
   NOT live only at `g≈0` (the touch ceiling). If the improvement collapses to
   zero once a real trade-through guard is required, NO-GO (LEARNINGS §6 — the
   passive-exit "edge" that was entirely touch-vs-fill).
3. The ENTRY placebo arm does NOT beat its own touch ceiling by more than the
   exit arm — i.e. the saving is reversion-specific, not generic spread capture.
   (Entry arm positive AND comparable to exit ⇒ artifact ⇒ REJECT.)
4. No look-ahead: limit price and fill decision use only the 1s path AFTER the
   exit-trigger bar; chase price is an actually-traded 1s price at window end.
5. ES TEST transfers with the same sign.

## Kill conditions (any ⇒ NO-GO)

Guarded exit improvement ≤0 or CI includes 0; effect only at `g≈0`; entry placebo
matches the exit arm; any look-ahead in the fill layer; ES sign does not transfer.
A wash is a clean REJECT and a successful outcome (rule 25).

## Parameterization (fixed at build, 2026-08-19)

The prereg text "limit at fill_ref ∓ g·σ_slot" conflated two knobs; the audited
build separates them (matches LEARNINGS §6 touch/guard/floor triad):
- **Offset δ·σ_slot** — how far into the retrace the passive limit is posted; the
  captured improvement if filled. Discovery sweep δ ∈ {0.10, 0.25, 0.50}.
- **Trade-through guard g·σ_slot** — a fill is credited only if the 1s path trades
  through the limit BY g·σ (`g=0` = touch ceiling; deployable g ∈ {0.10, 0.25}).
- **Window K min** ∈ {1,3,5}; unfilled ⇒ chase at the last traded 1s price at K.
- `σ_slot` = trailing-20-session mean of the 1m (high−low) range at that mfo slot
  (causal, per-slot, in points), NOT fixed ticks (RULES A4).
- Improvement (unified exit+entry): `imp = fs·(exec − ref)`, `fs=+1` want-up /
  `-1` want-down; exit `fs=side`, entry `fs=−side`. Confirmatory a priori:
  **δ=0.25σ, g=0.10σ, K=5** (sweeps are discovery, rule 26).
- Reported triad per LEARNINGS §6: touch ceiling (g=0) / guarded book (g≥0.1σ) /
  time-exit floor (never passive-fill, always chase = pure timing, no spread).
