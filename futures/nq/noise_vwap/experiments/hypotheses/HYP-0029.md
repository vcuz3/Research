# HYP-0029 — Causal rolling-percentile volatility and trend regimes are better calibrated than full-sample fixed buckets without relying on strategy P&L

- Status: completed (EXP-0041) — QUALIFIED. The volatility axis has one calibrated
  survivor (`10x504`); the joint vol×trend rule is not promoted because trend labels
  still have a two-session median dwell and the descriptive P&L ordering is unstable.
- Created: 2026-07-27
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: Trailing empirical percentiles adapt regime thresholds to the preceding market environment, reducing era concentration while preserving causal day-t labels.
- Exact change: Sweep indicator lookback {10,20,40} sessions x calibration lookback {252,504,756} prior observations. Primary 20x504; primary neighbourhood 10x504,20x252,20x756,40x504; all nine reported. Select only on label calibration, never P&L.
- Frozen baseline: EXP-0038 full-sample qcut regimes: 20-session volatility/trend with two-sided fixed quartile/tercile thresholds.
- Primary metric: Calibration score from annual occupancy error, annual concentration, one-day flips, dwell time, label coverage, and 12-cell coverage; P&L is descriptive only.
- Kill test: Reject a candidate if any rolling/calendar year has >60% of days in one relative-vol bucket, median regime dwell <3 sessions, post-warmup label loss >15%, or thresholds exhibit discontinuous/noncausal behavior. Do not select on Sharpe.
- Required controls: Causality mutation test; current full-sample labels as descriptive control; primary 20x504 with fast/slow neighbours; yearly and era coverage; recent 2023-2026 P&L shown only after calibration ranking.
- Data scope: NQ clean Databento RTH 2011-12-08 through 2026-07-14; all history consumed; baseline continuous-stop trades at 0.25 tick/side for descriptive attribution.
- Multiple-testing note: No P&L-based family selection. If any later alpha claim searches these nine cells, rerun the complete nine-cell selection inside each Null-C draw using the maximum selected statistic.

## Rationale and evidence

EXP-0038 fixed full-sample quantiles create extreme year concentration (2022 98% high); user approved a small causal stationarity sweep before any further regime use.

## Result

- Nine candidates were ranked on a common 3,173-session sample without P&L.
- `10x504` was the only candidate to pass the registered volatility gates: maximum
  annual single-bucket share 55.2%, median volatility dwell 3 sessions, zero label
  loss, causal prefix parity, and the best calibration score (0.403 versus 0.619 for
  the fixed full-sample control).
- Primary `20x504` narrowly failed the 60% concentration gate (60.8% high in 2022).
- Calibration lookback 504 was the local sweet spot. Both 252 and 756 produced more
  concentrated annual occupancy for every relevant indicator horizon.
- Caveat: `10x504` trend labels have median dwell 2 sessions and 17.1% singleton rate.
  Treat the result as a volatility-axis calibration survivor, not a validated joint
  stationary state model.
- Descriptive P&L did not select the winner and does not support alpha promotion:
  under `10x504`, high-relative-vol Sharpe was the weakest marginal band (0.73), while
  normal was strongest (1.47). No Null C was run.
