# HYP-0032 — Mandatory prop-firm protective stop anchored on the entry-frozen band/VWAP level: cost and sensible placement

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-08-11
- Origin idea: user deployment constraint — the prop firm this would run on
  requires a hard stop-loss order on every trade. This is a CONSTRAINT, not a
  proposed improvement, so the question is not "does it help" but "what does it
  cost and where is the cheapest place to put it".
- Builder: Claude

- Proposed mechanism: none claimed. A mandatory resting stop can only truncate
  the loss distribution and add exits; on a strategy whose soft trail already
  exits on a band/VWAP touch it is expected to be a monotone expectancy TAX
  (cf. `forex/exploration_1`: a compulsory stop was a monotone tax on 4/4 pairs
  and dominated the signal work). The design question is which placement pays
  the smallest tax per unit of tail protection bought.

- Exact change: a default-off resting stop on `core/engine2.py`, entry-frozen
  (rule 14), anchored on the strategy's OWN risk reference:

      long : risk = max(entry_fill - max(upper, vwap)|entry, 0) + b * ATR
             line = entry_fill - risk
      short: risk = max(min(lower, vwap)|entry - entry_fill, 0) + b * ATR
             line = entry_fill + risk

  `b` is the only free parameter. `b = 0` places the stop exactly at the level
  the strategy's own soft trail would exit on, frozen at entry. The `max(.,0)`
  floor is required: a fill can gap through its own reference, which would
  otherwise put the stop above the entry.

  Fill model: a resting order is live on EVERY bar including the fill bar
  (rule 3), independent of the soft trail's check cadence, and is checked before
  any close-based logic because it would execute intrabar first. It fills AT its
  level, except when the bar gaps through it, where it fills at the bar's open —
  the first tradable price (rule 5). A close-confirmed variant is available for
  comparability but is NOT the deployable model.

- Frozen baseline: the two candidate books from EXP-0043, unchanged otherwise —
  (A) `decision` stop + `require_reset` (the EXP-0043 recommendation), and
  (B) `every_bar` continuous stop (the currently deployed book). NQ primary,
  ES transfer. lb90 bands, 30-min clock, VWAP gate, next-open entry fills,
  0.25 tick/side + $2.25/side.

- Primary metric: net-ATR-R daily Sharpe and total net R versus the SAME book
  with no mandatory stop — i.e. the TAX, reported per `b`. Co-reported and
  equally load-bearing for the deployment question:
  worst-trade R, 1st-percentile trade R, mean loss R, max drawdown, and the
  realised per-trade risk distribution in ATR, points and dollars.

- Kill test: this cannot be "rejected" — the stop is compulsory. The decision
  rule instead selects a placement:
  ADOPT the smallest `b` that (i) costs less than 0.15 Sharpe versus the
  no-stop book on BOTH markets, and (ii) actually bounds the tail, i.e. worst
  trade and p1 trade R materially improve versus no stop. If NO `b` satisfies
  (i), report the strategy as materially damaged by the constraint and say so
  plainly rather than picking the least-bad cell and calling it a result.

- Required controls:
  1. **Gap leakage audit.** A resting stop's realised loss can exceed its
     nominal risk when a bar gaps through the level. Report the share of hard
     stops that fill worse than their line and the mean/worst overshoot — this
     is the honest limit of "bounded risk" (rule 5) and is what a trailing
     drawdown rule will actually see.
  2. **Placement-family comparison at matched exposure.** Compare the
     band/VWAP-anchored family against a plain ATR-from-entry stop (existing
     `init_stop_atr`) at matched stop-out rate, so "anchor on the strategy's own
     level" is a tested choice and not an assumption.
  3. **Trigger model.** touch (deployable, resting) vs close-confirmed, to
     quantify how much of the tax is the wick-out rather than the stop level
     (EXP-0031 established these are different RULES, not resolutions).
  4. **Both books.** The tax must be read on the book actually being deployed;
     the constraint may reorder the EXP-0043 recommendation.
  5. No Null C: no alpha is claimed, so there is nothing for a null to test.
     Any `b` that IMPROVED Sharpe would be a searched exit parameter and would
     require its own Null C before being read as anything but noise
     (cf. EXP-0032/0033, where a vol-scaled stop buffer beat baseline in-sample
     and died against the null).

- Data scope: NQ and ES clean Databento 1-minute RTH, 2011-12..2026-07, common
  post-lb90 sample (3628 NQ sessions). Consumed history.

- Multiple-testing note: `b` is swept over a small declared grid
  {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.5} ATR × 2 books × 2 markets, all reported. The
  selection rule above is stated in advance and is a COST threshold, not a
  performance maximisation, precisely so the sweep cannot become a search for a
  flattering exit parameter.

## Rationale and evidence

Measured before designing (`R_entry` = distance from the signal close to the
strategy's own band/VWAP reference, in ATR units): median 0.19, p25 0.085, and
**28.6% of NQ / 30.2% of ES signals sit within 0.10 ATR of their own reference**.
A stop placed exactly at that level (`b = 0`) would therefore give roughly a
third of trades a stop tighter than a tenth of a daily range and be a noise
generator. This is why the buffer term exists and why `b = 0` is included as the
degenerate end of the grid rather than as the recommendation.
