## Proposed mechanism

VEI = ATR(short)/ATR(long) is intended to measure volatility EXPANSION — the change in
vol relative to its own recent baseline — which is a distinct quantity from the vol
LEVEL. But as the denominator's memory grows, ATR(long) approaches a within-session
constant, and dividing by a constant does not form a ratio; it rescales ATR(short). In
that limit VEI degenerates INTO the level.

Study B established that the vol level forecasts forward realized vol at rank IC +0.86,
far better than any VEI variant. So if a variant is scored by `IC_fwdvol`, it can climb
the table purely by becoming more level-like — i.e. by ceasing to measure the thing the
project exists to study. `IC_fwdvol` would then be an anti-selective metric for a ratio.

## Exact change

No feature or estimand change. A new descriptive script scores 10 variants on one
common sample (the intersection where every variant is defined, so estimator is not
confounded with time of day) along four axes: `corr_lvl` (Spearman vs the vol level),
`IC_fwdvol` (the incumbent), `IC_partial` (Spearman vs forward vol with the level
partialled out of both sides, via the new `analysis.partial_spearman`), and the
project's actual primary metric — Study D's count-weighted within-slot high-vs-rest
momentum contrast at a MATCHED selection rate.

Two `LEVEL:` rows are ATR(short) with no denominator at all. They are not candidate
features; they are the degenerate limit, and they are the decisive control.

## Kill test

Declared before the run:

1. **If `corr_lvl` explains `IC_fwdvol` across variants (high R²) AND a no-denominator
   `LEVEL:` control tops the `IC_fwdvol` column, the metric is scoring level-likeness**
   and must not be used to select a ratio. `IC_fwdvol` is then retired for that purpose
   on both markets, and next action 0b is closed by REPLACEMENT, not by adopting the
   argmax.
2. If instead the ratios beat the pure level on `IC_fwdvol`, the metric is measuring
   genuine ratio information, `wilder_20_100`'s lead is real, and the canonical
   estimator should be reconsidered on it.
3. If `contrast` and `IC_fwdvol` rank variants **oppositely**, then the incumbent metric
   is not merely uninformative but actively harmful, and any past selection made on it
   must be re-examined.

## Required controls

- The no-denominator `LEVEL:` degenerate limit (kill test 1's discriminator).
- One common sample across all variants — different warm-ups otherwise mean different
  slots, and this project's standing hazard is exactly that time-of-day confound
  (FINDINGS section A-corrected (b)).
- Matched selection rate for the contrast column, so a variant cannot win by relabelling
  more decisions "high".
- Cross-market (NQ + ES). This is a machinery claim, so agreement is expected and
  disagreement would indicate a tape-specific artefact rather than a metric defect.

## Multiple-testing note

**The `contrast` column is a SEARCH over 10 variants against the project's primary
metric, on fully consumed history.** It is reported so the selection decision is made
with its consequence for the primary metric visible. It is NOT a licence to adopt the
argmax as a finding: Study D is already qualified/inconclusive (EXP-0006), and a variant
scoring higher here has not revived it. Any estimator change motivated by this column
would need its own preregistered test on evidence this sample cannot provide.
