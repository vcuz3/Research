# HYP-0011 — The frozen two-input core transfers to DOWNSIDE semivariance, and the up/down split is itself forecastable

- Status: claim A confirmed / claim B rejected (EXP-0014, 2026-07-31)
- Created: 2026-07-31
- Origin idea: `experiments/IDEA_BACKLOG.md` item 14 (raised by EXP-0013's closing note)
- Builder: Claude
- Proposed mechanism: stop placement, barrier reachability and the drawdown side of any
  book depend on DOWNSIDE volatility, not on total realised volatility. If the adopted
  two-input recipe (causal trailing 90-session same-slot median of the target +
  `range_rv_15m`) forecasts downside semivariance as well as it forecasts total RV, the
  decision-relevant quantity is available at no extra cost. Separately, if the SPLIT
  between the up and down legs is itself forecastable, that is new information rather
  than a rescaling of the total.
- Exact change: no strategy change. Apply the frozen recipe unchanged to three targets —
  `fwd_rv_bp` (the reference), `fwd_dsv_bp`, `fwd_usv_bp` — and to the share target
  `fwd_down_share = fwd_dsv_bp^2 / fwd_rv_bp^2`. Same walk-forward, same learner, same
  hyperparameters; only the target and its own causal same-slot median change. For the
  share target only, a second candidate replaces the symmetric state feature with its
  asymmetric counterpart (`past_down_share_30m`), because `range_rv_15m` contains no
  up/down information at all and would otherwise make claim B a rigged test.
- Frozen baseline: for every target, that target's own causal trailing 90-session
  same-slot median — the "free" forecast a decision maker already has.
- Primary metric: paired WITHIN-SLOT Spearman IC delta of the model over the same-slot
  median, count-weighted across slots, with a 90% session-block-bootstrap CI. Pooled IC,
  log-MSE skill and MAE are co-diagnostics. Within-slot is primary because a pooled IC on
  an intraday panel is dominated by time-of-day ordering that no decision requires
  (established by EXP-0012).
- Kill test, two independent claims:
  - **Claim A (transfer).** Reject "the recipe transfers to downside volatility" unless,
    on BOTH NQ and ES, the `fwd_dsv_bp` within-slot IC delta is positive with its 90% CI
    excluding zero AND is at least **75%** of the delta the same recipe achieves on
    `fwd_rv_bp`. The 75% retention gate follows the convention set by HYP-0006/EXP-0009.
  - **Claim B (asymmetry).** Reject "the up/down split is forecastable" unless, on BOTH
    NQ and ES, at least one of the two share candidates has a positive within-slot IC
    delta over the share's own same-slot median with its 90% CI excluding zero. If
    Claim B passes, it is only credited after the drift control below.
  Claim A and Claim B are reported separately; A passing does not rescue B, and B
  failing does not weaken A.
- Required controls:
  - Rule-23 reproduction of the EXP-0011 notebook pooled ICs before the forecast module
    is used, at the declared 5e-4 tolerance.
  - An executable identity check that `fwd_rv_bp^2 == fwd_dsv_bp^2 + fwd_usv_bp^2`, so
    the two legs provably decompose the reference target and nothing is double counted.
  - **Drift control (the load-bearing one).** `futures/nq/noise_vwap` EXP-0022 found a
    real, sibling-confirmed up/down asymmetry in the noise band that merely restated
    DRIFT and was harmful to act on. If Claim B passes, report the correlation between
    the predicted down-share and the trailing 30-minute return, and re-measure the
    within-slot IC delta inside trailing-return terciles. An asymmetry that survives only
    in the extreme drift terciles is drift, not information.
  - A degenerate control on the share target: how much of the share's variation the
    per-slot median already explains. If the share is a per-slot constant plus noise
    there is nothing to forecast, and that is the finding.
  - Per-year and per-slot deltas, NQ and ES replication, rule-9a coverage accounting.
- Data scope: NQ and ES clean Databento RTH 1-minute. Annual expanding-window
  walk-forward with first tested year 2016 (matching EXP-0011 Part A) through
  2026-07-14. The forward window is undefined at the 15:29 and 15:59 slots. All of this
  history is consumed; a survivor is discovery, not validation.
- Multiple-testing note: two preregistered claims, each with one primary statistic per
  market. Claim B has two declared candidate feature sets, which is a search of width
  two and is reported as such — a single-candidate pass on one market is not a result.
  No hyperparameter is tuned; the learner and window are frozen by EXP-0011.

## Rationale and evidence

EXP-0013 closed the route from this forecast to the existing directional book. Its
closing note identified downside semivariance as one of three surviving directions,
because it is the quantity that actually enters a stop or barrier decision, and because
the semivariance legs were already computed in the EXP-0011 notebook and never used.

The two claims are deliberately different in kind. Claim A is a transfer question and is
expected to pass — volatility clustering is not sign-specific, so the recipe should work
on either leg. It is worth running anyway because a cheap confirmation makes the downside
quantity usable, and a failure would be surprising and informative. Claim B is the
genuinely open question and carries the real risk: if the down-share is a per-slot
constant plus noise, then forecasting downside volatility is forecasting total volatility
times a fixed number, and there is no new information anywhere in the asymmetry.

## Outcome

**Claim A CONFIRMED, Claim B REJECTED** by `EXP-0014`, both on both markets.

Claim A: the frozen recipe applied unchanged to the downside leg gives a within-slot IC
delta over its own same-slot median of +0.4267 [+0.4054,+0.4499] NQ and +0.4560
[+0.4337,+0.4791] ES — 92.3% / 92.8% retention of the total-RV reference, clearing the
75% gate, positive in every one of eleven tested years. The decision-relevant downside
quantity is available at no extra cost.

Claim B: all four share cells span zero and the markets disagree on which candidate is
better. The degenerate control is decisive: the realised down-share is a constant near
0.494 with per-slot means spanning 0.486–0.500, and subtracting its causal trailing
same-slot median *increases* the variance by ~2%. **Downside volatility is total
volatility times a constant, and the constant is one half.** The drift control found no
drift channel either. See `artifacts/runs/EXP-0014/review.md` and FINDINGS section N.
