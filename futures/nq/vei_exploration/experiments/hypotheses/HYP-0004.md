# HYP-0004 — Opening condition explains the legacy VEI momentum uplift

- Status: completed — mechanism confirmed, predictive-information hypothesis rejected
- Created: 2026-07-26
- Origin idea: `IDEA-0001`; follow-up to EXP-0006's legacy-vs-repaired shrinkage
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: the legacy `seed='first'` Wilder recursion is not merely a
  noisier VEI. It embeds the first RTH minute's true range in both ATR legs. A quiet
  first minute followed by larger ranges makes the fast ATR catch up before the slow
  ATR and raises legacy VEI; an opening shock followed by smaller ranges does the
  reverse. The legacy calculation may therefore be an accidental composite of repaired
  VEI and a causal opening-condition feature. That feature, rather than superior VEI
  measurement, may explain the legacy momentum uplift.
- Exact change: hold repaired Wilder(10/50), the 30-minute decision clock, and the
  past/future 30-minute return estimand fixed. Define `open_rel50 = first-minute TR /
  mean(TR[0:50])`, available by the first eligible 10:29 decision, and
  `quiet_open = -log(open_rel50)`. Measure the legacy-repaired classification
  decomposition, continuous quiet-open dose response, and the incremental
  `past_ret * quiet_open` coefficient after repaired VEI, volatility level, and
  slot-specific intercept/slope controls. No trading claim.
- Frozen baseline: EXP-0006 repaired Wilder(10/50), `VEI>1.10`, and its published
  within-slot high-minus-rest continuation contrast. The already-viewed legacy-only /
  repaired-only cell counts and correlations are labelled consumed discovery evidence,
  not preregistered validation.
- Primary metric: session-block-bootstrap 90% CI for the standardized
  `past_ret * quiet_open` interaction coefficient in the controlled forward-return
  regression, separately on NQ and ES.
- Kill test: (1) mechanism reproduction requires within-slot
  `corr(legacy-repaired VEI, quiet_open) > +0.50` on both markets; (2) the opening
  feature is established only if its incremental interaction is positive with a 90% CI
  excluding zero on both markets and exceeds 95% of 1,000 era-stratified session
  re-pairing null draws on both; (3) it must have a correct-signed quiet-open quintile
  spread on both markets and no single era may carry the opposite sign on both markets.
  Same sign but one-market inference failure => qualified/inconclusive. Wrong sign on
  either market or null p>=0.05 on either => reject as predictive information.
- Required controls: absolute `open_rel50` dose response; legacy/repaired overlap and
  boundary decomposition; same-slot controls; current volatility level; selection-count
  matching; four eras; leave-one-session-out/top-session concentration; first 5/10/30
  minute opening denominators as labelled neighbouring definitions; session re-pairing
  null that preserves return paths, VEI, slots, eras, and the opening-feature marginal
  distribution while destroying same-session pairing.
- Data scope: NQ + ES clean Databento RTH 1-minute bars, 2011-2026, same sessions as
  EXP-0006. History is fully consumed; any survivor remains future-holdout pending.
- Multiple-testing note: the mechanism and the legacy/repaired fringe cells were seen
  before registration. Only `open_rel50`, the controlled interaction, and the stated
  kill tests are primary; alternative denominators and bucket views are robustness, not
  independent discoveries.

## Rationale and evidence

EXP-0006 showed the repaired feature reduced the Study-D continuation estimate. A
read-only follow-up then found 92.1% overlap between the two high-VEI sets, with tiny
legacy-only groups associated with quiet first minutes and repaired-only groups with
large first-minute shocks. This experiment tests whether that suggested mechanism is a
continuous, incremental, cross-market relationship or merely a few favourable boundary
observations discovered on consumed history.

## Verdict — EXP-0007

Mechanical channel confirmed (+0.551 NQ / +0.532 ES within-slot correlation between
seed bias and quiet opening), but predictive information rejected. Controlled
past-return × quiet-opening coefficients have 90% CIs crossing zero on both markets;
the re-pairing null fails on ES; dose response is non-monotone and wrong-signed on ES;
eras alternate sign. A shared zero-range opening on 2020-03-16 is load-bearing: removing
it flips both coefficients negative. See `artifacts/runs/EXP-0007/review.md`.
