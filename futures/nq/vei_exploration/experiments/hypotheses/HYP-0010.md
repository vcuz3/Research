# HYP-0010 — Noise-VWAP per-trade edge standardised by the canonical forward-vol forecast is not flat in forecast volatility

- Status: rejected (EXP-0013, 2026-07-31)
- Created: 2026-07-30
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Claude
- Proposed mechanism: The canonical causal forward-30-minute volatility forecast
  (EXP-0011: trailing 90-session same-slot target median + `range_rv_15m`) is a
  risk scale known at entry. A directional book can only gain risk-adjusted value
  from that scale if its per-trade expectancy is NOT proportional to it. If
  expectancy scales exactly with forecast volatility, sizing at `1/forecast` is
  already the complete and optimal use of the forecast and no further tilt exists.
  If the standardised edge rises with forecast volatility, high-vol entries are
  under-weighted at constant risk; if it falls, they are over-weighted.
- Exact change: No change to any strategy. This is a conditioning measurement on
  the frozen `futures/nq/noise_vwap` book. For every existing trade, attach the
  canonical forecast at that trade's own decision slot and regress the per-trade
  standardised edge (net points / forecast points) on the causal trailing
  same-slot percentile of the forecast.
- Frozen baseline: the noise_vwap `continuous_stop` configuration (RTH, 30-minute
  Concretum clock, every-bar stop, honest next-open fills, 0.25 tick/side + fees),
  which is the project's adopted operational spec. The `baseline` decision-clock
  configuration is a declared robustness cell, not a replacement primary.
- Primary metric: the OLS slope of per-trade standardised edge on the causal
  same-slot forecast percentile (centred), with a 90% session-block-bootstrap CI;
  co-primary is the top-quintile minus bottom-quintile difference in mean
  standardised edge with the same CI construction. Both are equal-weighted per
  bet (rule 12) and clustered by session.
- Kill test: Declare the volatility-SIZING channel CLOSED at the intraday entry
  level — i.e. the forecast is a risk normaliser only, with no tilt available —
  unless, on BOTH NQ and ES in the `continuous_stop` primary: (i) the slope's 90%
  session-block CI excludes zero, (ii) the slope has the SAME SIGN on both
  markets, and (iii) the top-minus-bottom quintile difference also has a
  same-signed CI excluding zero on both. Any mixed, sign-flipping, or
  zero-spanning outcome is a REJECT and closes the path; this workspace has
  already rejected four volatility-conditioning variants on this book
  (EXP-0019, EXP-0032, EXP-0035/0036, EXP-0040) and a fifth ambiguous result is
  not a reason to keep looking.
- Required controls:
  - Rule-23 reproduction of the EXP-0011 notebook's pooled ICs by the importable
    forecast module before the forecast is used, with a declared tolerance.
  - A sealed-read invariant proving that building features over the full history
    does not change any earlier row (the leakage check for a causal feature).
  - Within-slot conditioning throughout: the conditioning variable is the CAUSAL
    trailing same-slot percentile of the forecast, never a full-sample quantile,
    so the test cannot become a time-of-day selector (the EXP-0009 defect).
  - Absolute-scale companion: the slope of raw net points on forecast points,
    to show whether expectancy scales with volatility at all.
  - Denominator comparison: the same measurement with the project's existing
    trailing 14-session ATR as the risk scale, so the question "is the new
    forecast a better sizing denominator than what is already deployed" is
    answered alongside.
  - Horizon-match control: repeat on the subset of trades whose realised hold is
    at most the 30-minute forecast horizon.
  - Era split and per-configuration replication; rule-9a coverage accounting for
    every trade dropped by the join.
- Data scope: NQ and ES clean Databento RTH 1-minute data. Forecasts are annual
  expanding-window walk-forward with the first tested year 2013, so the trade
  sample is 2013 through 2026-07-14. The canonical forecast is undefined at the
  15:29 and 15:59 decision slots (no 30-minute forward window inside the
  session), so those entries are excluded and counted. All of this history is
  consumed research history; a survivor would be discovery, not validation.
- Multiple-testing note: one preregistered primary cell (`continuous_stop`,
  standardised-edge slope) per market. Everything else listed above is a declared
  control or robustness view of the same claim, not an independent discovery. No
  parameter is searched; the forecast specification is frozen by EXP-0011 and the
  book is frozen by the noise_vwap project.

## Rationale and evidence

EXP-0011 established forecast ACCURACY and explicitly did not establish economic
value. The cheapest possible test of economic value is not a new strategy: it is
one regression on trades that already exist.

The interpretation is deliberately asymmetric, because the two outcomes mean very
different things:

- FLAT standardised edge ⇒ expectancy is proportional to forecast volatility ⇒
  sizing at `1/forecast` is the correct and complete policy, and there is no
  additional alpha in tilting toward or away from high-volatility entries. This
  would close the sizing channel at the intraday level, matching EXP-0040's
  day-level finding that levering high-variance days adds standard deviation in
  step with mean.
- SLOPED standardised edge ⇒ a genuine, preregisterable sizing hypothesis exists.

A pass does NOT license deployment. It would license one preregistered
re-simulation with sizing applied inside the engine, plus a matched-turnover
control and a path-preserving Null C — because a conditioning slope measured on
completed trades is not the same object as a re-simulated sized book (rule 18).

## Outcome

REJECTED by `EXP-0013`. The kill test fired on both markets: the primary slope is
-0.0047 [-0.1451,+0.1345] NQ and +0.0483 [-0.0822,+0.1838] ES -- flat, CIs spanning
zero, signs disagreeing -- and the co-primary top-minus-bottom quintile difference
likewise. Per-trade expectancy is proportional to forecast volatility, so sizing at
`1/forecast` is the complete and optimal use of the forecast and no tilt remains.
The intraday volatility-SIZING channel is closed. See
`artifacts/runs/EXP-0013/review.md` and `reports/FINDINGS.md` section M.
