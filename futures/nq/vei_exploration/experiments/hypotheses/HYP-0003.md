## Proposed mechanism

Two defects were found in the canonical VEI feature that every study A–F consumed.

1. **Warm-up.** `core/vei.py::_roll` built the Wilder ATR with
   `ewm(alpha=1/n, min_periods=n, adjust=False)`. Pandas seeds that recursion at the
   FIRST observation and `min_periods=n` merely masks the first n−1 outputs; textbook
   Wilder ATR seeds with the first n-bar SMA. On a continuous series this is a one-off
   burn-in, but **this ATR resets every session**, so the bias is paid ~3710 times and
   never washes out. Measured cost: forward-vol IC +0.157→+0.202 (NQ) / +0.195→+0.207
   (ES) on repair, and +0.242/+0.249 if the under-populated bars are instead masked.
2. **Estimator selection was confounded.** EXP-0001 compared `sma(10,50)` with
   `wilder(10,50)` at equal *nominal* n, which is ~2× the effective memory
   (SMA(n) centre-of-mass (n−1)/2 vs Wilder(n) n−1). Memory-matched controls in both
   directions show memory is the operative axis, not estimator form.

If the repaired feature is a better-measured version of the same quantity, the
descriptive findings B–F should survive with equal or larger effect sizes. If any of
them was resting on warm-up noise, it should shrink.

## Kill test

Declared before the run:

1. **If the within-slot Study D contrast loses significance (90% CI includes 0) on
   EITHER market under the repaired feature, Study D is downgraded to inconclusive** and
   FINDINGS/MEMORY must be rewritten to say the momentum-regime result did not survive
   its own feature repair.
2. If the contrast survives on both markets, Study D stands and the repair is recorded
   as a measurement improvement, NOT as new evidence for the edge.
3. **A threshold confound must be excluded.** Repairing the warm-up raises mean VEI
   (0.893→0.917 NQ), so a fixed `VEI>1.10` cut selects a different, smaller set. Every
   regime comparison is therefore run twice: at the absolute `T=1.10`, and at a
   **matched-selection-rate** threshold (the repaired-VEI quantile that keeps the legacy
   high-regime decision count). A result that appears only at one of the two is a
   threshold effect, not a feature effect, and must be reported as such.

## Multiple-testing note

**This is a second look at fully consumed history with a modified feature.** The
estimator/warm-up defect was found by a reviewer, not by searching for a better result,
and the repair was specified before its downstream effects were measured — but the
re-run still cannot generate a deployable claim. Specifically: EXP-0004's verdict of
REJECT stands regardless of what the repaired cell prints; a better number there is a
post-hoc improvement on a consumed sample and is forward-shadow evidence at best
(rule 26). The legitimate outputs of this experiment are (a) a corrected Study A, (b) a
confirmation or withdrawal of B–F, and (c) a repaired feature for future work.
