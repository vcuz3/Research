# HYP-0009 — The two-feature forward-volatility core carries conditional information beyond its causal same-slot median

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-07-30
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: The latest 15-minute realized range should update the expected next-30-minute volatility within a fixed clock slot, rather than merely reconstructing the intraday volatility curve.
- Exact change: Generate annual expanding-window OOS forecasts from the frozen normalized-target range core, convert them back to basis points, and compare them with the causal same-slot median using within-slot and slot-by-weekday rank IC, FWL residualized log-volatility R-squared, and log-MSE skill.
- Frozen baseline: Causal trailing 90-session same-slot median of forward 30-minute realized volatility.
- Primary metric: Pooled within-slot rank IC delta of the raw core forecast versus the same-slot median, with session-block bootstrap uncertainty; residualized log-volatility correlation/R-squared and log-MSE skill are co-primary diagnostics.
- Kill test: Treat the core as calibration-only unless both NQ and ES show positive log-MSE skill versus the median and positive conditional ordering: lower 90% session-block bounds above zero for both the within-slot IC delta and FWL residual correlation. Mixed results remain diagnostic and do not justify strategy work.
- Required controls: Annual expanding-window OOS predictions; exact frozen two-feature model; slot-by-weekday conditioning; per-slot and per-year tables; causal alignment/data-quality audits; NQ and ES replication.
- Data scope: NQ and ES clean Databento RTH 1-minute data, 2011 through latest available; evaluation defaults to annual OOS 2016-2026. All history through 2026-07-14 is consumed diagnostic evidence, not a fresh holdout.
- Multiple-testing note: One frozen core against one free median baseline; no feature or parameter search. Several conditional views diagnose the same claim and are not separate discoveries.

## Rationale and evidence

A high pooled IC may primarily reflect the deterministic intraday volatility curve. Conditional evaluation must establish that the forecast improves decisions made at a fixed time of day.
