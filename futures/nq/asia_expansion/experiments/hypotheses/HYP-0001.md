# HYP-0001 — Asia session range expansion 1.5x as a continuation signal (NQ 5-min)

- Status: rejected for trading; the source's stated directional reason is also rejected
- Created: 2026-08-26
- Origin idea: aligrithm.com, "The Signal Ceiling" (retrieved 2026-08-26), user request
- Builder: Claude (Opus 5)
- Proposed mechanism: A 5-minute bar whose range far exceeds its own trailing average is an information-arrival or liquidity-taking event; if the flow that caused it persists, the bar's own sign predicts the next 5-120 minutes.
- Exact change: Asia window 18:00-03:00 ET, 5-min left-labelled bars, fire when R_t >= 1.5 * mean(R over the 20 bars ending at t-1) with a 2/3 fractional min_periods, direction = sign(close_t - open_t), entry at open[t+1], exit at open[t+1+H] with H=3 bars, truncated to the session's last close. Full spec and every swept alternative in paper/PAPER_SPEC.md.
- Frozen baseline: none (first run of this project); the calibration benchmark is the coin-flip-direction control in EXP-0002, not a prior strategy.
- Primary metric: per-signal mean gross forward return in index points with a trade-date cluster-robust t (Rule 12); net reported on a five-rung cost ladder (0.000 / 0.335 / 1.100 / 1.350 / 2.000 points round trip).
- Kill test: reject continuation if per-signal mean gross <= 0 with cluster-robust |t| >= 2 in the negative direction on BOTH the full archive and the article's window; promote the inverted (fade) reading only if it simultaneously clears net > 0 at the 1.35 pt rung, cluster t >= 2 outside the article window, sign stability across all four Asia windows and all three lookbacks, and >= 30 trades in every year.
- Required controls: coin-flip-direction control on identical firing bars/fills/charge (400 draws) to calibrate how much of any net t-stat is the charge; LEARNINGS #1 fire-rate-by-slot diagnostic read before any conditional P&L; Rule 9a data-quality report on every window; sweeps over k, lookback N, holding period H, Asia window, and overlap policy.
- Data scope: NQ 1-minute Databento archive futures/nq/data/NQ_1m_clean.parquet, 2011-08-01 to 2026-07-15, 3,842 Asia sessions; the article's own 2021-12-01 to 2025-08-31 window reported as a separate row. All historical data is inspected by this project — only future observations are clean holdout.
- Multiple-testing note: 28 cells searched (4 thresholds x 7 horizons), plus 3 lookbacks, 4 window definitions and 2 overlap policies. Exactly one cell (k=2.5, H=18) has a positive net on any rung; it is the grid maximum and is reported as such. Nothing is promoted, so no selection-aware correction was applied; any future promotion would require comparing the real maximum to the null distribution of maxima over the reproduced search.

## Rationale and evidence

The source reports T = -10.96 for this rule and concludes the signal is "worse than
useless ... you are buying the top of a spike that is already reversing". That is a
directional claim, but the reported statistic is computed on P&L net of a flat 2.00-point
charge. The purpose of this hypothesis is to separate the two.

## Result

**Rejected as a trade, and the source's reason is rejected too — in the opposite
direction from the one the source argues.**

Gross continuation is weakly POSITIVE, never negative: +0.0655 pt/signal (t=+1.45) over
15 years, +0.0675 pt (t=+0.50) inside the article's own window. Net is negative on every
cost rung including the cheapest defensible one. The source's T = -10.96 is a statistic
about the charge: on the same window a coin-flip direction prints net t = -15.18
[-17.33, -13.05], i.e. MORE negative than the reported figure.

The one cell with a genuine gross signal (k=2.5, H=3: +0.302 pt, t=+2.50, beats 400/400
coin flips) still nets -0.033 pt on the full NQ contract at a 1-tick spread and -1.048 pt
as an MNQ trade. Enforcing one position at a time collapses gross to +0.003 (t=+0.05).

Evidence: artifacts/runs/EXP-0001/, artifacts/runs/EXP-0002/, reports/FINDINGS.md.

No path-preserving null was purchased: the primary metric fails the net gate by roughly
1.3 points per trade, and the gate rule says not to buy the expensive null until the real
pass clears the success metric.
