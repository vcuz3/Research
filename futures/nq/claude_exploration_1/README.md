# Cross-Asset Macro Exploration — DXY / GC / ES / NQ

Does the **dollar** carry usable information about gold and the equity indices?
Four preregistered hypotheses, five runs, **all four rejected**.

Start with `reports/FINDINGS.md`, then `MEMORY.md`. `reports/FINAL_REVIEW.md` has the
verdict; `reports/DATA_QUALITY.md` is the rule-9a gate that must be read before any
cross-asset number here is believed.

## The answer

The dollar's relationship to gold and the indices is **large and contemporaneous** —
gold's causal dollar beta is −0.90 and its same-window correlation is −0.40 — and it
carries essentially nothing at a lag, in a decomposition, or as a regime label, at any of
the three horizons tested (5-minute, 30-minute, overnight-to-session). On gold the
relationship is roughly **75x larger contemporaneously than at one 5-minute lag**.

| run | hypothesis | verdict | what decided it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EXP-0001 | the dollar decomposition separates factor from flow | REJECTED | KT3: sign disagrees with both siblings; the residual is *worse* than the raw move |
| EXP-0002 | the dollar leads gold/equities at 5 minutes | REJECTED | KT2: the **reverse** direction is 2.4x larger |
| EXP-0003 | factor coherence is a momentum regime | REJECTED | KT1 + KT3 on both preregistered markets |
| EXP-0004 | gold under-reacts to the overnight dollar | REJECTED | all three: wrong sign, R² 0.0017, no sibling differential |
| EXP-0005 | (follow-up) is gold's coherence contrast just volatility? | **yes** | +0.0496 → −0.0047 once matched on volatility |

## What is worth keeping

Three method findings, each of which changed a verdict here and each of which applies
well beyond this project:

- **B — rank IC and mean spread give opposite readings.** Gold: rank −0.036 (significant)
  with a mean quintile spread of −0.15 ticks (zero). ES/NQ: rank ≈ 0 with mean spreads of
  +1.09 / +3.34 ticks (significant). Equity intraday momentum lives in the **tails**, which
  is the mechanical reason a *breakout* rule works on NQ/ES. Report both, always.
- **E — matching a regime split on time of day is not enough; match on volatility too.**
  EXP-0003 matched slot composition exactly and still had a 1.46x volatility gradient
  between its cells; removing it took gold's contrast from +0.050 to −0.005.
- **G — the shared decision-bar close manufactures reversion.** 12-33% of measured
  reversion at 30 minutes, **31-56% at 5 minutes**, worst on a synthetic index. One extra
  column of code catches it.

Plus a reusable artifact: a tested, causal **synthetic DXY** built from published ICE
weights and CME FX futures (`core/data.py`), with a per-slot staleness report and an
EUR-only control arm.

## Layout

`core/` data + features + inference · `scripts/` one per run · `tests/test_core.py`
30 invariant checks · `experiments/` hypotheses + ledger · `artifacts/runs/EXP-XXXX/`
immutable outputs and reviews · `reports/` findings, data quality, final review.

`paper/`, `baseline_replication/` and `backtest_engine/` are **not applicable** here —
there is no source paper and nothing trades — and remain template stubs.

the first command. All history through 2026-07-15 is **consumed**; nothing here earned a
forward test.
