# HYP-0001 — Dollar-decomposed residual momentum in gold and equities

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-07-31
- Origin idea: IDEA-0001 (cross-asset macro decomposition)
- Builder: Claude
- Proposed mechanism: an instrument's intraday move splits into a **dollar-explained**
  component and an **idiosyncratic residual**, and the two should have *opposite*
  continuation properties.
  * The dollar-explained component is a **mechanical repricing**. Gold is quoted in
    USD; when the dollar moves, gold's USD price must move to keep the metal's value
    unchanged, and cross-asset desks arbitrage that link continuously. A repricing
    that is already complete has nothing left to continue, so this leg should show
    **no** forward predictability.
  * The residual is **gold-specific order flow** — COMEX/ETF demand, positioning,
    physical hedging. Institutional flow is split across time to limit impact
    (Kyle/Almgren), so the *unexplained* part of a move is the part most likely to be
    a partially-executed parent order, and therefore the part that **continues**.
  Prediction: past RESIDUAL return predicts forward return better than past RAW return,
  and better than the past FACTOR component.
- Exact change: (single variable) replace the momentum predictor
  `past_<I>_30 = lp[m] - lp[m-30]` with
  `resid_<I>_30 = past_<I>_30 - beta_I * past_dxy_30`,
  where `beta_I` is the through-the-origin OLS slope of the instrument's 1-minute log
  returns on the dollar's, fitted on the **20 strictly prior sessions** (never on the
  window being residualised). Nothing else changes: same rows, same clock, same target.
- Frozen baseline: `past_<I>_30` -> `fwd_<I>_30`, within-slot count-weighted Spearman IC
  on the common sample where every arm is defined.
- Primary metric: **paired within-slot Spearman IC delta**
  `IC(resid_GC_30, fwd_GC_30) - IC(past_GC_30, fwd_GC_30)`,
  with a 90% session-block bootstrap CI (rule 12; 400 draws). Primary cell is declared
  in advance as **W=30, H=30 on GC**. ES and NQ are the sibling/mechanism markets.
- Kill test: (all declared before the run; any one firing rejects)
  * **KT1** — the primary paired delta's 90% CI **includes zero** on GC. The
    decomposition then adds nothing to what raw momentum already says.
  * **KT2 (degenerate control, the decisive one)** — the pure factor leg
    `factor_GC_30 = beta_GC * past_dxy_30` predicts `fwd_GC_30` **at least as well** as
    `resid_GC_30` does. If the dollar-explained component alone carries the
    predictability, the residual split is not the thing doing the work. This is the
    `vei_exploration` finding-H discipline: score the degenerate limit of the
    construction, not just the construction.
  * **KT3** — the sign of the paired delta **disagrees** between GC and *both* of
    ES/NQ. A flow mechanism that is real should not be gold-only *and* sign-flipped
    elsewhere; disagreement means market-specific noise.
- Required controls: (enumerated below)
  1. `factor_<I>_30` scored on its own (KT2 above).
  2. Residualised TARGET `fwdresid_<I>_30` — the return a dollar-hedged position
     actually earns. A residual-momentum claim that only works on the *unhedged*
     forward return is a dollar bet in disguise.
  3. `beta = 0` reproduces the raw baseline exactly (asserted in-run, rule 23).
  4. Era split (2011-2018 / 2019-2026) and the (W, H) surface, both read as a SEARCH:
     report the slope across cells, never the argmax (finding-H method note).
  5. Dollar-fresh subsample (`dxy_fresh`) — a residual computed against a stale dollar
     is a mismeasured residual.
- Data scope: NQ/ES/GC 1-minute RTH 09:30-16:00 ET, 2011-08-01 -> 2026-07-15,
  3,710 near-complete non-degraded sessions; synthetic 4-leg DXY.
- Multiple-testing note: one primary cell is declared above. The (W, H) surface is 3x3
  and the era split is 2 cells; both are labelled descriptive search, not confirmatory.
  All history here is consumed research history — a pass earns a preregistered forward
  test, never a retroactive one (rule 26).

## Rationale and evidence

The workspace has repeatedly rejected cross-market *filters* (`futures/gc` EXP-0007
"trade GC in ES's direction" destroyed the edge; `futures/nq/noise_vwap` EXP-0018
NQ-gated-on-ES was a turnover lever). This hypothesis is deliberately **not** a filter:
it does not drop trades or condition direction on another market's sign. It
**decomposes** the instrument's own move and asks which part carries information. That
is a different estimand, and the GC EXP-0007 result is in fact weak evidence *for*
testing it — ES's contemporaneous direction carried no usable information about GC,
which is consistent with the cross-asset link being a fast mechanical repricing rather
than a lead.

Gold is the right primary market: its causal dollar beta is large (median -0.90) and its
contemporaneous 30-minute correlation with the dollar is -0.40, so there is a
substantial factor component to remove. ES/NQ have beta ~= -0.19 and correlation ~= -0.20,
so the decomposition is a much smaller perturbation there — which makes them an honest
sibling test rather than a duplicate of the primary.

- Objections: (a) the residual inherits the beta's estimation error, so a null result
  could be measurement noise rather than absence of signal — mitigated by also reporting
  the factor leg, whose own IC does not depend on the split being accurate; (b) gold's

  concentrates — this is exactly what HYP-0004 tests separately.
