# HYP-0003 — Macro factor coherence as a momentum regime for equity indices

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-07-31
- Origin idea: IDEA-0001 (cross-asset macro decomposition)
- Builder: Claude
- Proposed mechanism: **what the tape is being driven BY, not how much it is moving.**
  When index futures are moving *with the dollar* — i.e. when a single macro factor is
  repricing every asset at once — the driver is a persistent, slow-moving
  reassessment (rates, growth, policy) that keeps pushing in the same direction for
  the rest of the block. When the index is **decoupled** from the dollar, the move is
  idiosyncratic: sector rotation, single-name flow, index arbitrage, noise — which has
  no reason to persist. Prediction: intraday momentum (trailing 30-minute return
  predicting the next 30 minutes) is **stronger in high-coherence conditions**.
- Exact change: label each decision with
  `coh_I = |corr(1-min returns of I, 1-min returns of DXY)|` over the trailing 60
  minutes of the same session, computed on **dollar-fresh minutes only**, then
  normalised to the trailing 90-session **same-slot z-score** `cohz_I`. Split the
  panel into the top and bottom 30% of `cohz_I` **within each slot** and compare
  momentum between the cells.
- Frozen baseline: the unconditional within-slot momentum correlation
  `corr(past_I_30, fwd_I_30)`.
- Primary metric: the **regime contrast**
  `within-slot corr(past_30, fwd_30 | high cohz) - (| low cohz)`
  at a matched 30% selection rate with identical slot composition by construction,
  90% session-block bootstrap CI (400 draws). Primary markets declared in advance:
  **ES and NQ**. GC is reported as a third market.
- Kill test: (all declared before the run; any one firing rejects)
  * **KT1** — the contrast's 90% CI **includes zero** on *either* ES or NQ, or the
    signs disagree between them. These two are near-identical tapes; a regime effect
    that does not survive on both is not a regime effect.
  * **KT2 (degenerate control, the decisive one)** — the same contrast computed with
    the **volatility level** `rvz_I` as the selector, at the same matched rate, is
    **equal or larger**. The workspace has rejected five volatility overlays on this
    family and `vei_exploration` finding H showed the pure volatility level has a
    momentum contrast of essentially zero (-0.005 NQ / +0.023 ES). If a coherence
    label cannot beat the label that is already known to be worthless, it is
    volatility re-badged.
  * **KT3 (rule-18 re-pairing null)** — the real contrast falls **inside** the null
    band produced by re-pairing each session's equity path with a *different*
    session's dollar path, drawn from the same calendar year. That null preserves the
    dollar's own volatility, autocorrelation, calendar and roll structure and destroys
    only the contemporaneous pairing, which is exactly the information the hypothesis
    claims to use. 30 draws; interpret the null's **centre** as well as its tail
    (a null centred at the real value means a rarity/turnover filter; a null centred
    at zero with the real value outside means genuine cross-information).
- Required controls: (enumerated below)
  1. KT2 volatility-level selector and KT3 re-pairing null, above.
  2. Signed coherence (not just `|corr|`) as a secondary label — checks whether the
     effect is about *co-movement strength* or about the *sign* of the equity-dollar
     relation, which itself flips between rates-driven and risk-driven regimes.
  3. Raw `coh_I` versus the same-slot z-score `cohz_I`: FX liquidity collapses after
     the London close, so raw coherence has a strong time-of-day profile and a fixed
     cut on it would be a clock (`vei_exploration` finding I). Both are reported.
  4. Era split 2011-2018 / 2019-2026.
  5. Selection rate 20% / 30% / 40% — sensitivity, read as a slope not an argmax.
- Data scope: as HYP-0001. The 60-minute coherence window means the 09:59 decision is
  undefined; 12 of 13 slots survive, uniformly across sessions.
- Multiple-testing note: this is the *fifth-plus* conditioning variable tried on the
  NQ/ES intraday-momentum tape in this workspace. The prior is strongly negative and the
  bar is correspondingly high: all three kill tests must fail to fire. Descriptive
  passes on consumed history earn one preregistered forward test and nothing else.

## Rationale and evidence

`futures/nq/noise_vwap` and `futures/nq/vei_exploration` have between them rejected
volatility level, volatility expansion (VEI), Hurst exponent, realised-vol gates,
adaptive stop width, gap magnitude, and forecast-volatility sizing as conditioners of
this tape. The standing caution in the VEI backlog is explicit: *"A new overlay proposal
needs a mechanism that is not volatility level, expansion, or scale."*

Factor coherence is precisely such a mechanism. It is a **second-moment cross-asset**
quantity, not a first-moment own-asset one: two days with identical ES volatility can
have completely different dollar coherence. The workspace's synthesis — *"volatility sets
WHERE the edge is, not how to size or gate it"* — is a statement about the volatility
axis only, and leaves the composition-of-drivers axis untested.

The construction deliberately imports the two lessons that cost this workspace the most:
the same-slot z-score (finding I — a fixed cut on a feature with a time-of-day profile is
a clock) and matched selection rates with identical slot composition (findings H and I).

## Review before registration

- Reviewer: pending
- Decision: registered, pre-run
- Objections: (a) a 60-observation rolling correlation is a noisy statistic and its noise
  is itself volatility-dependent, so KT2 is doing double duty as a confound check —
  noted; (b) dollar staleness rises through the session (0.5% in the first hour to 10.7%
  in the last for the 4-leg basket), so coherence is measured less accurately late in the
  day; the fresh-minute restriction and the same-slot z-score both address this but
  neither eliminates it.
