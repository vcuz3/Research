# HYP-0002 — DXY leads gold and equities intraday (cross-asset lead-lag)

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-07-31
- Origin idea: IDEA-0001 (cross-asset macro decomposition)
- Builder: Claude
- Proposed mechanism: **macro news is priced in FX first.** The FX complex is the
  deepest continuously-traded macro market and is the direct expression of a rate or
  growth surprise; gold and equities express the *same* surprise second-hand. If the
  transmission is not instantaneous — because gold's COMEX book is thinner and its
  participants more heterogeneous — then a dollar move should be followed by the
  corresponding gold move, i.e. **lagged** dollar returns should predict forward gold
  returns *beyond* what gold's own past already says.
- Exact change: measure, at a 5-minute non-overlapping clock, the within-slot **partial**
  Spearman IC of `past_dxy_5` on `fwd_<I>_5`, controlling for `past_<I>_5`. No trading.
- Frozen baseline: the own-momentum term `past_<I>_5 -> fwd_<I>_5`. The claim is
  strictly incremental to it, which is why the statistic is a partial IC.
- Primary metric: within-slot count-weighted partial Spearman IC
  `pIC(past_dxy_5, fwd_GC_5 | past_GC_5)` with a 90% session-block bootstrap CI
  (400 draws). Primary cell declared in advance: **W=5, H=5 on GC, 5-minute clock.**
  Expected sign is **negative** (a dollar rally is followed by gold falling further).
- Kill test: (all declared before the run; any one firing rejects)
  * **KT1** — the primary partial IC's 90% CI **includes zero**. No lead.
  * **KT2 (symmetry control, the decisive one)** — the REVERSE partial IC
    `pIC(past_GC_5, fwd_dxy_5 | past_dxy_5)` is of **comparable magnitude** (within a
    factor of two) to the forward one. A genuine lead is *asymmetric*; a symmetric
    "each predicts the other" result is the signature of overlapping or mismeasured
    contemporaneous information (non-synchronous quotes, stale prints), not of
    transmission.
  * **KT3 (staleness control)** — the effect **disappears on the EUR-only dollar
    proxy**. 6E is the thickest leg (0.42% forward-filled minutes vs 4.70% for the
    4-leg basket); a lead-lag that lives only in the basket is a stale-price artifact,
    which is the single most common way this measurement is faked.
- Required controls: (enumerated below)
  1. KT2 reverse direction and KT3 EUR-only, above.
  2. `dxy_fresh` subsample — decisions where the dollar was not forward-filled at
     either window endpoint.
  3. ES and NQ as siblings. ES is *more* liquid than the FX basket, so the news
     mechanism predicts a **weaker** lead into ES than into GC. A lead that is equally
     strong into ES supports the staleness explanation instead.
  4. Contemporaneous correlation reported alongside, so the reader can see how large
     the same-window link is relative to the claimed lagged one.
  5. Horizon term structure H in {5, 10, 30} — descriptive; a real transmission lag
     must **decay** with H, not grow.
- Data scope: as HYP-0001; the 5-minute clock gives 78 non-overlapping decisions per
  session (~289k rows).
- Multiple-testing note: one primary cell. The H-surface and the sibling markets are
  descriptive. Even a pass is a *measurement*, not an edge: 5-minute cross-asset
  predictability must clear a round-trip cost (GC 0.10 pt tick plus spread) before it
  means anything tradable, and that comparison is reported in the run.

## Rationale and evidence

This is the cheapest test of the strongest naive cross-asset story, and the workspace's
prior is that it should FAIL. `futures/gc` EXP-0007 found ES's contemporaneous direction
carried no usable information about GC; NQ/ES EXP-0018 found cross-market confirmation is
a turnover lever, not alpha. Both are consistent with fast, efficient transmission.

The reason to run it anyway is that neither prior study measured a **lag** at a
sub-30-minute scale with the own-momentum term partialled out, and both used direction
*filters* rather than an incremental information measure. The result is also a
precondition for HYP-0001 and HYP-0003: if the dollar leads at a 5-minute scale then the
contemporaneous beta used to decompose returns is mis-specified, and both other
hypotheses would need a lagged beta instead.

The symmetry control (KT2) is what makes this worth writing down. Almost any two
correlated series measured with non-synchronous or stale quotes will appear to lead each
other; only an asymmetric result is informative.
