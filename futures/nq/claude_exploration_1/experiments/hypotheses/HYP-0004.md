# HYP-0004 — Overnight dollar impulse under-reaction completed during RTH

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-07-31
- Origin idea: IDEA-0001 (cross-asset macro decomposition)
- Builder: Claude
- Proposed mechanism: **a liquidity mismatch, not a forecasting claim.** The dollar
  trades continuously and deeply through the Asian and European sessions; gold's US
  order book is thin over the same hours. A dollar move that happens at 03:00 ET is
  therefore priced *fully* in FX but only *partially* in COMEX gold, because the
  participants who would arbitrage it are not at their desks and the book cannot absorb
  size. When New York liquidity arrives at 09:30, the repricing completes.
  This predicts a specific, signed, decomposable quantity: the **shortfall**
  `short_GC = beta_GC * on_dxy - on_GC` — how far gold still is from where the
  overnight dollar move implies it should be — should predict gold's **RTH** return with
  a **positive** sign.
- Exact change: one regression per session (no trading). Predictor `short_GC`, target
  `rth_GC = lp_GC[16:00] - lp_GC[09:30]`. `beta_GC` is the same causal 20-prior-session
  dollar beta used everywhere else in this project.
- Frozen baseline: two degenerate predictors that between them span the shortfall —
  the raw overnight gold gap `on_GC` alone (the well-documented overnight-reversal
  effect) and the raw overnight dollar move `on_dxy` alone. The shortfall is a
  *constrained* linear combination of these two, so it is only interesting if the
  constraint is where the information is.
- Primary metric: within-year-clustered Spearman IC of `short_GC` against `rth_GC`,
  90% block bootstrap CI over sessions (400 draws), plus the OLS slope in basis points
  of RTH move per basis point of shortfall. Primary market declared in advance: **GC.**
- Kill test: (all declared before the run; any one firing rejects)
  * **KT1** — the primary IC's 90% CI **includes zero**, or its sign is **negative**
    (which would be overnight over-reaction, i.e. the opposite mechanism; it would be
    an interesting finding but it kills *this* hypothesis).
  * **KT2 (degenerate control, the decisive one)** — in the unconstrained
    two-variable regression of `rth_GC` on `(on_GC, on_dxy)`, the coefficients do
    **not** have the pattern the mechanism requires (negative on `on_GC`, and the
    `on_dxy` coefficient of the same sign as `-beta_GC`), or the constrained shortfall
    explains **no more** than `on_GC` alone. If the raw overnight gap does all the
    work, this is the known gap-reversal effect wearing a dollar costume.
  * **KT3 (differential prediction)** — the effect is **as strong or stronger on ES**
    as on GC. ES's overnight book is far deeper than gold's, so a liquidity-mismatch
    mechanism predicts a *weaker* effect there. An equal effect points to something
    common to the dollar decomposition itself rather than to gold's overnight
    illiquidity.
- Required controls: (enumerated below)
  1. KT2 unconstrained pair and KT3 sibling differential, above.
  2. Weekend/holiday gaps (`gap_days > 1`) reported separately — a 65-hour gap is a
     different object from a 17-hour one.
  3. Era split 2011-2018 / 2019-2026.
  4. Economic units: the IC converted into gold points and dollars per contract, and
     compared against a round-trip cost, so a statistically real but sub-tick effect is
     labelled as such (rules 19-21).
  5. Rule-18 re-pairing null on the dollar leg, run only if the primary clears KT1
     and KT2 (the standing "gate the expensive null on the success metric" rule).
- Data scope: as HYP-0001, but one row per session (~3,709 observations after the first
  session, which has no predecessor).
- Multiple-testing note: one primary cell, one primary market, sign declared in advance.
  This is the only hypothesis in the set whose horizon (a full RTH session) is long
  enough that a real effect could plausibly clear costs, so it carries the most weight
  and is held to the sign requirement as well as the CI.

## Rationale and evidence

The three intraday hypotheses all ask whether the cross-asset link carries information
*inside* the window where both markets are liquid and arbitrage is cheap. The workspace's
accumulated prior says that is where edges go to die. This hypothesis asks the opposite
question: whether the link fails to hold **where one leg cannot trade properly**, and
whether the failure is repaired on a predictable schedule. That is a market-structure
claim with a specific mechanism, a specific sign, and a specific place to look — the kind
of claim that has survived in this workspace (`futures/gc` EXP-0002: gold's edge lives in
equity RTH, not gold's own pit hours) far better than the conditioning filters that keep
dying.

The decomposition is what makes it testable rather than a restatement of overnight gap
reversal. Gap reversal says "a big overnight move partly reverses". This says something
narrower and falsifiable: the *dollar-explained part* of the gap should NOT reverse
(it is a genuine repricing), while the *shortfall* should be *completed*. KT2 is
constructed so that the ordinary gap-reversal effect cannot pass as a confirmation.
