HOW MUCH OF K1 IS ALGEBRA?  (asked before K1 is read, not after)
PHI0 and the realised lag-1 P&L share 159 of their 160 weighted lags --
PHI0 sums rho(1..160), the realised rule is an exact identity with
PHI1 = rho(2..161).  So a high K1 is mostly the SAME statistic at a
one-lag offset, and the honest question is how much the offset moves it.

  Spearman(PHI1, realised gross)          +0.9804   <- near +1 is expected: identity
  Spearman(pred1, realised gross)         +0.9822   <- same, in engine units
  Read K1 as 'the published lag-0 form survives the execution lag and the
  cost', NOT as 'the formula predicts'.  Only K2 can say that.

  LIQUIDITY CONFOUND -- is the net arm a trend result or a cost ranking?
  Bid-ask bounce makes rho(1) negative in proportion to tick-over-
  volatility, and that same ratio IS the cost.  So PHI0 and net P&L can
  share a driver that has nothing to do with trend.  If rho(cost,net) is
  large, the net arm is a liquidity ranking wearing the formula's coat.

K2b -- ANNUAL OUT-OF-SAMPLE, EQUAL 4-YEAR ESTIMATION WINDOWS
Three era transitions is too few to settle K2, so the same question is
asked once per year: PHI from the trailing 4 years ranks the NEXT year.
The window is a FIXED length for every product and every year, because a
growing window has a shrinking sampling sd and would make later years'
PHI systematically better estimated than earlier ones (LEARNINGS
2026-08-03).  The degenerate benchmark uses the identical window.

  WHY -- three diagnostics, each one line
  (i)   Spearman(PHI, degenerate) within year, pooled  +0.9808
        PHI over a window IS that window's in-sample trend P&L plus a
        drift term, so 'the theory' and 'the backtest' are near enough
        the same predictor.  The closed form does not add information
        to a backtest; it is the backtest, written in closed form.
  (ii)  rank persistence of PHI itself, year to year     +0.7133   (n=344)
  (iii) rank persistence of REALISED P&L, year to year   -0.0045
        If a product's own PHI does not persist from one window to the
        next, no screen built on it can rank the next window -- and the
        limit is then the STABILITY of the autocorrelation function,
        not this particular formula.  That is a claim about every
        autocorrelation-based screen at this horizon, not just this one.
