## Proposed mechanism

VWAP is the FX/equity execution benchmark because a participant working an order
through the day realises a *volume*-weighted, not a time-weighted, average price.
Displacement from VWAP is therefore usually read as "how far is price from where
the market's inventory was actually accumulated".

That reading has a precondition almost nobody checks: **VWAP has to differ from
TWAP.** VWAP is `sum(tp*v)/sum(v)`; at constant `v` it collapses *identically* to
`mean(tp)`. So the volume weighting only does work to the extent that the
intraday volume profile is (a) non-flat and (b) non-flat in a way correlated with
where price went, rather than merely non-flat.

CME FX futures are the only venue in this workspace where the question is even
well posed. `forex/noise_vwap` established that IBKR cash-FX bars carry no size at
all (`volume == -1` on 100% of 22.0M rows), so every "VWAP" in the spot project is
a TWAP wearing a VWAP's name, and that project's own control found the fully
volume-free variant matched the published rule on 8/8 cells. Futures publish
real, exchange-reported, centrally-cleared volume, and FX volume is famously
concentrated in the London/NY overlap — the single most favourable setting the
volume weighting could ask for.

This is the "score the bare numerator" control (LEARNINGS 2026-07-27) applied to
an anchor instead of a ratio: TWAP is not a rival indicator, it is the degenerate
limit of the indicator under test.

## Exact change

None — this is a measurement, not a strategy change. Compute, on the 30-minute
wall-clock decision grid:

1. the per-session intraday volume profile (share of session volume by 30-min slot);
2. `sep = VWAP - TWAP` in pips, by slot, per product and per year;
3. `sep` scaled by the same-slot causal dispersion of `close - VWAP`, i.e. how
   large the anchor disagreement is relative to the displacement the anchor is
   used to measure;
4. `corr(dev_vwap, dev_twap)` on the decision grid.

## Frozen baseline

`core/vwap.py::add_anchors` at the commit registered in the EXP-0001 ledger row;
panel from `core/frame.py::build_panel(product, scope="explore")`.

## Primary metric

`R_sep` = median over decisions of `|VWAP - TWAP| / scale_vwap`, where
`scale_vwap` is the causal trailing same-slot mean of `|close - VWAP|` (the
denominator the deviation feature is z-scored by). Reported at the 16:29 ET
decision (the last liquid decision, where the anchors have had the whole session
to separate) and pooled over all decisions.

## Kill test — written before the run

**The volume weighting is declared non-load-bearing if, on BOTH 6E and 6B, either**

- `R_sep < 0.10` pooled — the two anchors disagree by less than a tenth of the
  displacement they are used to measure, so no downstream statistic can
  distinguish them; **or**
- `corr(dev_vwap, dev_twap) > 0.99` on the decision grid.

In that case every later "VWAP" result in this project must be relabelled a TWAP
result, and the reason for preferring futures over spot for this study disappears.

**Conversely**, `R_sep > 0.25` on both products establishes that the anchors are
materially different objects and hands HYP-0002 a real question to answer.

Note what this test can and cannot do: it establishes SEPARATION, not
INFORMATION. Two anchors can differ a lot and predict identically. HYP-0002
carries the information arm; the separation result here is what makes that arm
interpretable rather than vacuous.

## Required controls

- Per-product: 6E and 6B must agree, or the result is instrument-specific.
- Per-era: report by year. 6E's tick halved in 2016 and volume has trended; a
  separation that is a 2010-2012 artifact is not a property of the contract.
- Per-block: report by liquidity block. Separation must be largest late in the
  session (the anchors have had longer to diverge) — if it is not, the
  construction is wrong, not the market.

## Rationale and evidence

- `forex/noise_vwap/core/session.py` and its TWAP == VWAP-at-constant-volume test.
- LEARNINGS 2026-08-01 sub-lesson (b): the volume-free variant matched the
  published rule on all eight FX cells — but on a venue with NO volume, which
  cannot distinguish "volume does not help" from "volume is not available".
  Futures separate those two.
- LEARNINGS 2026-07-27: the degenerate-limit control is the cheapest decisive test.
