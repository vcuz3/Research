## Proposed mechanism

EXP-0004 established that finding C's block shape is not a volatility gradient,
and that on 6E the *block* label survives conditioning while the volatility label
dies. What it could not establish is **why** the block label matters, because on
6E and 6B "thin book" and "these particular ET hours" are the same thing:

| | asia share (vs flat) | overlap share | **asia/overlap** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6E | 0.32x | 2.44x | **0.131** |
| 6B | 0.30x | 2.30x | **0.130** |
| **6J** | **0.65x** | 2.08x | **0.313** |

(Per-minute share of session volume, explore sample, measured before this
hypothesis was written; the blocks have unequal lengths so shares are normalised
by minutes.)

6E and 6B are effectively the same experiment run twice — their liquidity ratios
agree to three decimals. **6J is a different dose**: the yen leg trades in Tokyo,
so its Asia block carries 2.4x more of its session volume, relative to its own
overlap, than EUR or GBP.

- **H_liquidity.** Reversion is caused by thin books absorbing transitory order
  flow badly. The contrast should scale with the liquidity gap, so 6J's should be
  **materially smaller**.
- **H_clock.** The contrast is a property of the hours themselves — the
  US/European news cycle, dealer shift handovers, or simply that CME is a US
  venue and these are its off-hours regardless of the counter currency. Then 6J
  should look like 6E and 6B.

**Honest statement of what this test can and cannot do.** 6J is a *dose*, not a
reversal: even for 6J the Asia block is still below flat (0.65x) and the overlap
is still its busiest, because the USD leg and the venue are American on every one
of these contracts. So a null result is weaker evidence for H_clock than a
positive result would be for H_liquidity, and a full dose-response needs the
wider panel (6A/6C/6N/6S, all already downloaded). With three products and
effectively two distinct doses this is a two-point comparison, and it is declared
as such — it is not a dose-response curve.

## Exact change

None to the feature, target, threshold, entry convention, horizon, or the
stratification machinery. Three additions:

1. **6J is added to `core/data.py`** — file, notional (12,500,000 JPY), tick eras
   and reporting unit. The tick grid was verified against the archive rather than
   assumed: ≥0.9998 of closes sit on the 1e-6 grid every month through 2015-05,
   0.842 in 2015-06 and ~0.51 from 2015-07, so the tick **halved 1e-6 → 5e-7
   mid-2015** and 2015 is a transition year assigned the finer tick.
2. **A per-product reporting unit.** 6J quotes dollars per YEN near 0.0091, where
   0.0001 would be an ~11-big-figure move. Its unit is **1e-6**, because
   12,500,000 × 1e-6 = **$12.50** — exactly a 6E pip and exactly one pre-2015 6J
   tick. `pip_size` returns 1e-4 for 6E/6B, so their results are unchanged.
   *Note this is NOT the conventional USD/JPY pip of 0.01 yen*: the futures quote
   is the reciprocal of the spot convention, so 0.01 yen maps to a
   price-level-dependent number of quote units (~8.3e-8 at USDJPY 110) and cannot
   serve as a fixed unit at all.
3. **A risk-equalised estimand for cross-product comparison.** Per rule 12 the
   default for equal-risk bets is the equal-weighted per-bet effect; equal risk
   means sizing inversely to the product's own dispersion. So the cross-product
   statistic is `pnl / scale` — P&L as a fraction of that decision's causal
   same-slot displacement scale. Native units and dollars are reported alongside,
   but **two products' "pips" are never placed side by side.**

## Kill test — written before the run

Let `R(p)` be the risk-equalised, volatility-matched `asia` − `overlap` contrast
for the `vwap` anchor at H=30, `rv_60` quintiles, common support — the EXP-0004
primary in risk-equalised units. Let `B = mean(R(6E), R(6B))`.

1. **H_liquidity SUPPORTED** if `R(6J) < 0.60·B`. A linear-in-liquidity mechanism
   predicts ≈0.42·B from the ratio table; 0.60 allows for non-linearity while
   still requiring a clear attenuation.
2. **H_clock SUPPORTED, and finding C's liquidity mechanism WITHDRAWN on all
   three products**, if `R(6J) >= 0.60·B` **and** `R(6J)`'s bootstrap CI excludes
   zero. In that case FINDINGS §C keeps the shape and loses the explanation, and
   `MEMORY.md`'s inventory reading is marked invalidated.
3. **INCONCLUSIVE** if `R(6J)`'s CI contains both 0 and `0.60·B`. Then the test
   was underpowered, nothing is claimed in either direction, and that is recorded
   rather than resolved by picking the nearer point estimate.

A negative `R(6J)` (overlap more reversionary than asia on 6J) counts as
H_liquidity supported under arm 1 but must be reported separately as an
inversion, not folded into "attenuated".

  coverage floor.

## Rationale and evidence

- `artifacts/runs/EXP-0004/review.md` — finding C survives volatility matching on
  6E; the mechanism is confirmed on 6E only, and the mirror arm already disagrees
  between 6E and 6B, so a third product is the natural next instrument.
- LEARNINGS 2026-07-31 — "prefer a mechanism prediction that is ASYMMETRIC across
  your markets over the usual 'both markets agree' check": 6E and 6B agreeing is
  nearly free, since their liquidity clocks are identical to three decimals. 6J
  is the asymmetric prediction.
- LEARNINGS 2026-07-20 — a single-market pass does not validate a mechanism that
  predicts cross-market transfer; the sibling market is the mechanism test.
- `MEMORY.md` next-actions item 3, which already names 6J as the informative
  addition for exactly this reason.

## Review after execution

- Reviewer: Claude (Opus 5) self-review, 2026-08-03
- Decision: **INCONCLUSIVE (arm 3)**
- Basis: `R(6J)` = **−0.0038**, boot95 [−0.0331, +0.0255]; `B` = +0.0331, gate
  +0.0198. The CI contains both 0 and the gate, so arm 3 fires and pre-empts
  arm 1. The point estimate is −0.11×B, i.e. marginally *inverted* rather than
  merely attenuated, and is reported as such — but the preregistered rule stands.
- Design fault, owned: the benchmark `B` was the mean of 6E and **6B**, and 6B's
  own contrast spans zero (+0.0073 [−0.0677, +0.0712]). Averaging a precise
  estimate with a null one made the gate noisier than assumed when this file was
  written, and that is what forced arm 3. Fix before IDEA-0008: benchmark against
  a product whose estimate is precise, and bootstrap the DIFFERENCE.
- What the run did establish (post-hoc arms, labelled as such in the review):
  **H_clock weakened** — `R(6E) − R(6J)` = +0.0627 [+0.0103, +0.1107], CI excludes
