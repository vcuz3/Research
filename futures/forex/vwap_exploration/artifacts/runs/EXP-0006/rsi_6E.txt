-- RULE 9a: where is RSI undefined, and does it track liquidity? ----------
   A 14-period feature that resets at session-sequence breaks deletes
   decisions. If the deletion tracks time-of-day liquidity it is a hidden
   filter (finding G).

   A recursive feature does not merely drop the bar it is missing: under a
   RESET policy the run needs n fresh increments, and on a 30-minute grid
   n=14 is ~7 HOURS, so the deletion lands in a DIFFERENT BLOCK from the gap
   that caused it. Both policies are reported; `bridge` is used throughout.
