# HYP-0004 — Partial-TP + looser clock exit fits GC's reversion/no-drift profile

- Status: rejected (EXP-0006, 2026-07-19)
- Created: 2026-07-19
- Origin idea: IDEA_BACKLOG (exit-family port); prompted by re-reading the NQ
  STUDIES.md exit work against GC's EXP-0004 continuous-stop inversion.
- Builder: claude
- Proposed mechanism: GC is noise-dominated with **no positive intraday drift**
  (EXP-0000 always-long control net -0.084 pt, Sh -0.10; EXP-0004 established the
  minute-scale tape is whipsaw). The NQ partial-TP survivor (STUDIES.md,
  `tp1.0_50` / `tp0.75_67`) does NOT monetize drift — it banks a fraction of the
  position after a `tp_atr`-ATR favourable extension and lets a runner trail, and
  its Null-C **sign flips** (noise says partial-TP lowers Sharpe; the real NQ tape
  raises it) because it harvests a real **intraday mean-reversion after a ~1 ATR
  extension** that a runner-only stop gives back. Gold, being reversion-/noise-
  dominated, should exhibit that give-back at least as strongly as NQ — so an exit
  that banks the extension is *aligned* with GC's profile, the mechanistic inverse
  of why the continuous stop (which tightens the runner and clips winners)
  inverted on GC in EXP-0004. Separately, EXP-0004's lesson was "the 30-min
  clock's looseness is load-bearing on gold"; the untested direction of that
  finding is a **looser** (60-min) decision/stop clock, giving noise even more
  room before a stop check.
- Exact change: single-variable EXIT changes vs the frozen EXP-0005 baseline
  (entries, bands, lookback=90, k=1.0, VWAP gate, next-open fills, decision-clock
  stop all held):
  1. **Partial-TP + runner:** once price runs `tp_atr` ATR in favour, bank
     `tp_frac` of the position at the next open (momentum-confirmed close +
     next-open fill = honest, conservative vs an intrabar limit); the runner keeps
     the baseline decision-clock band/VWAP stop. Grid
     `tp_atr in {0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5}` x `tp_frac in {0.5, 0.67}`.
  2. **Looser clock:** decision/stop clock period 60 min (vs baseline 30), no TP.
  3. **Combined:** the best surviving TP config on the 60-min clock.
  Engine: `core/engine2.py` (`tp_atr`/`tp_frac` already wired, EXP-0003); parity
  vs `core/engine.py` at `tp_atr=0`, 30-min clock, decision stop asserted to the
  digit (rule 23).
- Frozen baseline: `reports/BASELINE.md` / EXP-0005 (relaxed bands, 2692 trades,
  gross +0.362 / net +0.217 pt/trade @0.50tick, net Sharpe 0.48, net day-$ t=+1.30).
- Primary metric: **per-day-clustered net daily Sharpe @0.50 tick/side**, with net
  day-$ t reported alongside (rule 12/22). Gross reported too (rule 20). A variant
  passes the screen only if it beats the frozen baseline net Sharpe AND does not do
  so by inflating gross (i.e. the improvement is variance reduction / reversion
  capture, not manufactured fills).
- Kill test: (stated before the run, rule 24) no exit variant raises net daily
  Sharpe over the frozen baseline by >= +0.05 at 0.50 tick, OR any apparent uplift
  is produced by inflating gross pt/trade, OR (if a variant clears the screen) the
  Sharpe uplift fails a claim-matched paired Null-C (real uplift within the
  path-preserving return-shuffle uplift distribution, z < 2). Cost-fragility is
  explicit: the partial adds no net contract volume (enter 1 + partial f + runner
  1-f = 2 sides, per-contract cost unchanged), but the edge is small so it must
  clear on **net**, not gross.
- Required controls: (a) fill-feasibility assert (0 same-bar fills) + TP
  next-open-vs-signal-close artifact (rule 1/2/3); (b) gross-vs-net side by side
  (rule 20); (c) **claim-matched paired Null-C gated on a positive real pass**
  only (`gate-nullc-on-success-metric`) — `core/nulls_session.py` return shuffle,
  diffusivity gate, compare real (variant - baseline) uplift to the null uplift
  distribution over >=30 draws; (d) loser-tail / winner-tail retention so a Sharpe
  gain that only shrinks variance is labelled as such, not as edge.
- Data scope: GC RTH 09:30-16:00 ET, `data/GC_1m_clean.parquet`,
  2011-08-01..2026-07-16, relaxed bands (BAND_MIN_FRAC=0.9). All history is
  CONSUMED research data — a survivor is a paper/screen result, not deployable
  evidence; only a future shadow is clean (rule 26).
- Multiple-testing note: 8 TP cells + 2 clock variants = a small exit-family
  search. If any clears the screen, the Null-C is the honest bar; if several do,
  report the family and use the paired Null-C on the best, acknowledging the
  search. tp_atr/tp_frac are a-priori/intuitive params carried from the NQ result,
  not tuned to GC.

## Rationale and evidence

- NQ partial-TP is the ONLY Null-C survivor of the NQ exit/entry study battery
  (`futures/nq/noise_vwap/STUDIES.md`): grid `tp0.75_67` dSharpe +0.097 at
  family-wise z=+3.96, mechanism = reversion-after-+1ATR-extension, sign flips vs
  noise. It was measured on NQ's continuous-stop baseline; here it is measured on
  GC's decision-clock baseline (GC's continuous stop is dead, EXP-0004).
- GC profile that motivates transfer: no intraday drift (EXP-0000), noise-
  dominated at the minute (EXP-0004), timing edge genuine-but-tiny and beats
  Null-C (EXP-0001 z+2.66). The continuous stop clipped winners because it
  tightened the runner; partial-TP does the opposite — it *takes* the extension
  gold hands back, so its failure mode on GC is different and worth a direct test.
- This is the mechanistic inverse of EXP-0004 and directly tests the shared
  learning "exit-timing value scales with the drift/noise ratio" from the take-
  profit side rather than the stop-tightening side.

## Outcome (EXP-0006, 2026-07-19)

**REJECTED — sub-threshold; Null-C not run (gate).** Best partial-TP variant
(tp1.0_67 / tp1.5_50) lifts net daily Sharpe by only +0.023 / +0.021 @0.50tick,
below the +0.05 kill-test gate. Importantly, partial-TP is **benign** on GC (gross
held/nudged up, win% 33.6→34.0) — the mechanistic inverse of EXP-0004's
continuous-stop inversion — so the "banking the extension is aligned with gold"
thesis is directionally confirmed but too small to matter. The tp ordering **inverts
vs NQ** (tighter tp0.75 hurts, wider tp1.0–1.5 helps → gold needs more room). Looser
60-min clock is harmful (netSh 0.475→0.258). Moot under the EXP-0001 sizing NO-GO.
