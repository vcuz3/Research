# HYP-0001 — Gold-native COMEX pit hours (08:20-13:30 ET) beat the equity-RTH window

- Status: REJECTED (2026-07-19; see `artifacts/runs/EXP-0002/review.md`)
- Created: 2026-07-19
- Origin idea: `experiments/IDEA_BACKLOG.md` (session-window study)
- Builder: claude
- Proposed mechanism: The Noise Area + VWAP intraday-momentum strategy was
  designed for the equity cash session. The GC baseline port inherited the equity
  window 09:30-16:00 ET, which does not match where gold actually trades. Gold's
  liquidity and directional information concentrate in the COMEX floor session
  (08:20-13:30 ET) — it opens with the US macro data cluster (08:30 ET) and spans
  the London-US overlap. If the strategy's edge is driven by genuine intraday
  momentum in the liquid window, aligning the session, VWAP anchor, noise-band
  open, and decision clock to the COMEX pit hours should strengthen it; if the
  weak baseline is just the wrong window, this is where a real gold edge would show.
- Exact change: replace the session window [09:30, 16:00) ET with the COMEX pit
  window [08:20, 13:30) ET. Everything else identical: 90-session noise lookback,
  Concretum 30-min decision clock measured from the window open (decisions 08:49,
  09:19, ..., 13:19 ET), VWAP reset at the window open, `max/min(open, prior
  window close)` band reference, next-open honest fills, forced flatten at the
  window's last bar. Implemented in `core/session_hours.py`.
- Frozen baseline: the equity-RTH baseline in `reports/BASELINE.md` (EXP-0000),
  re-run through the same generalized loader for an exact apples-to-apples compare.
- Primary metric: per-day net Sharpe and day-clustered net t-stat at 0.50 tick/side,
  before vol-target sizing. Secondary: gross Sharpe, Null-C excess.
- Kill test: the COMEX window does NOT beat the equity baseline's net Sharpe by a
  meaningful margin, OR its gross edge fails to exceed a claim-matched Null-C (the
  timing signal is machinery/drift, not real intraday momentum). Either outcome
  rejects the hypothesis.
- Required controls: (1) exact equity-window re-run through the generalized loader
  (parity guard); (2) claim-matched Null-C (path-preserving return shuffle) on the
  COMEX window through the full pipeline.
- Data scope: GC RTH clean data 2011-08-01..2026-07-16, all usable sessions. This
  history is consumed research data, not a sealed holdout.
- Multiple-testing note: this is a single, mechanism-motivated window (the standard
  COMEX pit session), not a searched grid of start/end times. If it fails, do NOT
  sweep windows to find a survivor on consumed history; treat any such sweep as
  discovery requiring fresh holdout.

## Rationale and evidence

The baseline (EXP-0000) established a positive but weak, cost-fragile gross edge
(gross Sharpe 0.77) that collapses under vol-target sizing (CAGR 0.1%, Sharpe 0.07,
maxDD -45.7%) and has a marginal net t-stat (~1.25). One leading explanation is
simply the wrong session window: gold's information is not in equity cash hours.
This hypothesis tests that directly.

## Outcome (2026-07-19)

**REJECTED — hypothesis inverted.** COMEX pit hours (08:20-13:30 ET) are *worse*
than the equity window on every metric (net Sharpe 0.21 vs 0.46, Δ −0.25; gross
Sharpe 0.53 vs 0.77; day-net t 0.56 vs 1.25) AND the COMEX window fails its own
Null-C (real +6.42 vs null +6.18, z=+0.04, p=0.476, 96% machinery capture). The
marginal-but-real timing trace exists only in the equity 09:30-16:00 window
(Null-C z=+2.66, p=0.048 at 0.50 tick). Both kill conditions fired. The strategy's
gold edge is tied to the equity cash session, not gold's own floor hours. Evidence:
`artifacts/runs/EXP-0002/`.
