# HYP-0001 — The weekday 09:30 ET Noise-VWAP timing rule transfers to BTCUSDT

- Status: completed; narrow first-screen survival, independent review pending
- Created: 2026-08-16
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: large BTC moves after the U.S. cash open may persist when
  they exceed the normal same-minute displacement and agree with session VWAP.
- Exact change: transfer the audited NQ signal, stop cadence, and next-open fill
  convention to BTCUSDT while replacing the natural futures session with an
  explicit, configurable ET anchor.
- Frozen baseline: weekday-only 09:30-15:59 ET, 30-minute decisions, 90-session
  band, 90% observation floor, 5 bps per side, fixed 1x notional.
- Primary metric: temporal-OOS net daily Sharpe over 2023-01-01 through
  2026-08-12, with zero-return eligible sessions included.
- Kill test: reject transfer if OOS net Sharpe is not positive, or if OOS mean
  gross return per trade does not exceed the 10 bps round-trip cost assumption.
- Required controls: gross versus net, always-long same-window drift, long/short
  split, annual results, data-quality gates, and exact-next-minute fill asserts.
- Data scope: Binance Spot BTCUSDT 1-minute bars loaded from 2019-01-01;
  in-sample 2019-06-01 through 2022-12-31; temporal OOS begins 2023-01-01.
- Multiple-testing note: one prespecified baseline cell. Any later anchor or
  parameter sweep consumes history and must reproduce selection in its null.

## Rationale and evidence

The 09:30 ET anchor is selected for economic, not P&L, reasons: it is the U.S.
cash-equity open used by the source strategy and a recurring U.S. liquidity and
price-discovery transition for Bitcoin. The 390-minute decision universe is
held fixed to avoid confounding an anchor change with a session-length change.

EXP-0001 passed the literal kill rule narrowly: temporal-OOS net Sharpe was
0.230 and mean gross return was 11.835 bps/trade against 10 bps round-trip
cost. This is not a stable edge finding: in-sample Sharpe was -0.223, the
cost cushion was only 1.835 bps/trade, and 2025-2026 were negative.
