# HYP-0001 — Low-volatility false breakouts revert

- Status: provisionally supported; validation pending
- Created: 2026-09-19
- Origin idea: none

- Proposed mechanism: Breakouts from unusually compressed ATR regimes are liquidity overshoots that reverse over the next 12 hours.
- Exact change: Contrarian entry after a close beyond the prior 10-bar range when ATR is below the trailing 100-bar 15th percentile; fixed 1.5 ATR stop and target.
- Frozen baseline: No-trade / zero-expectancy benchmark.
- Primary metric: Pooled gross mean R per trade with date-cluster bootstrap CI.
- Kill test: NO-GO if pooled gross mean R is not positive, its date-cluster bootstrap 95% CI includes zero, or fewer than 3 of 4 pairs have positive gross mean R.
- Required controls: Four sibling FX pairs, cost sensitivity, exit attribution, UTC-slot coverage; path-preserving null and neighbour sweep required for confirmation.
- Data scope: EURUSD GBPUSD AUDUSD NZDUSD clean 1-minute archives, all available history through 2026-07-17.
- Multiple-testing note: One frozen baseline configuration; four correlated pairs are not independent replications.
