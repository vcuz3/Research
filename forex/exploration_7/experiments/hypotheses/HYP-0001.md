# HYP-0001 — Weekend gap mean reversion on 9 FX pairs

**Status:** frozen 2026-08-12, before any outcome was computed.
**Builder:** Claude. **Origin:** a prior AUDJPY result (external, not reproduced
here) reporting that weekend gaps beyond some size revert toward the gap.

---

## 0. Registration fields

- Proposed mechanism: the weekend break forces position adjustment into one
  illiquid reopen print, so the reopen overshoots the information that accrued
  over the weekend and the overshoot is unwound as normal liquidity returns.
  This predicts reversion that is largest for large gaps, decays quickly, and
  is concentrated in the thinnest hours.
- Exact change: none - this is a standalone event study, not a modification of
  an existing baseline. Fade the weekend gap at `abs(z) >= k`, entry at the
  open `d` minutes after the reopen, exit at a fixed horizon.
- Frozen baseline: no-trade. The comparison is against zero expectancy, plus a
  weeknight-break placebo arm and a gap-sign-randomised null.
- Required controls: C1 weeknight-break placebo, C2 continuation view, C3 full
  threshold x horizon term structure, C4 era split incl. the padded JPY
  vintage, C5 reopen-spread cost proxy, plus validity gate G1a/G1b/G2.
- Multiple-testing note: the grid is 6 thresholds x 6 horizons x 4 entry
  delays. The primary specification (k=1.5, d=5, h=1440) is fixed in advance
  and is the only confirmatory test. Every other cell is discovery and must be
  reported as such; any cell selected after seeing the surface is scored
  against the distribution of the MAXIMUM statistic over the reproduced search,
  not against its own t.
- Kill test: P1 pooled clustered t>=2 AND P2 >=6/9 pairs positive AND P3 AUDJPY
  positive AND P4 60-minute-delay retention >=0.5 AND P5 net of cost > 0.
- Data scope: 9 FX pairs, 782 canonical weekends 2011-07..2026-07, 1-minute mid
  OHLC from forex/data/.
- Primary metric: per-event mean fade return in own-sigma units, SE clustered
  on weekend date.

## 1. Claim

After the weekend break, if the first tradable price differs from Friday's
close by more than a threshold, price tends to move back **toward** the Friday
close. Trading against the gap ("fade") therefore has positive expectancy.

Direction is unambiguous: fade = short after an up-gap, long after a down-gap.

## 2. Population and data

* 9 pairs: EURUSD, AUDUSD, GBPUSD, NZDUSD, USDJPY, AUDJPY, NZDJPY, EURJPY,
  GBPJPY. 1-minute mid OHLC from `forex/data/`.
* 782 canonical weekends, 2011-07 .. 2026-07, imposed from the EURUSD session
  calendar on every pair (three archives are a padded vintage and cannot locate
  their own boundary — see `reports/DATA_QUALITY.md`).
* ~6,900 pair-weekends; 565 weekends carry all 9 pairs.

## 3. Construction

| Element | Choice | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gap | `log(reopen_open / friday_close)` | both prices observable at the reopen instant |
| Scale | `sigma` = SD of the pair's own **prior** 26 weekend gaps | causal; a fixed pip cut is a volatility-regime selector |
| Signal | `z = gap_ret / sigma`, fire on `abs(z) >= k` | scale-free, comparable across pairs and eras |
| Entry | OPEN of the bar `d` minutes after the reopen | entering at the reopen print itself trades on the price that defines the gap |
| Exit | CLOSE at `h` minutes after the reopen | fixed horizon; no barriers, so no intrabar path ambiguity |
| Return | `-sign(gap) * log(exit/entry) / sigma` | "R" units; per-event, equal risk per bet |
| Inference | mean with SE clustered on **weekend date** | all 9 pairs share each weekend; they are not independent |

Estimand is the **per-event mean**, not the weekend-averaged mean: signal count
is endogenous to volatility and block-averaging can invert the sign
(LEARNINGS §4).

Grid: `k` in {0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5}; `d` in {1, 5, 15, 60};
`h` in {15, 60, 240, 1440, 2880, 7200} minutes.

**Primary specification, fixed in advance:** `k = 1.5`, `d = 5`, `h = 1440`
(one day), pooled across all 9 pairs, gross of costs.

## 4. Kill test

The hypothesis is **REJECTED** unless *all five* hold at the primary
specification:

* **P1 — sign and significance.** Pooled mean R > 0 with weekend-clustered
  |t| >= 2.0.
* **P2 — breadth.** At least 6 of 9 pairs individually positive. Four USD
  majors are ~2 effective bets and the JPY crosses share a JPY leg, so a
  result carried by one bloc is not breadth.
* **P3 — origin pair.** AUDJPY individually positive. The claim was made about
  AUDJPY; if it fails there the reported result does not replicate.
* **P4 — not a boundary artifact.** The effect must survive delaying entry.
  On this archive `open[t] == close[t-1]` for ~100% of contiguous minutes, so a
  reopen print carrying pricing error inflates the measured gap **and** makes
  the fade look profitable from the next bar (LEARNINGS §6). Requirement:
  mean R at `d = 60` retains >= 50% of its value at `d = 1`, measured on the
  **paired** event set (identical weekends, not two separately filtered
  samples).
* **P5 — survives cost.** Net mean R > 0 after the reopen spread charge. The
  reopen is the widest spread of the week and we hold mid-only data, so the
  charge is estimated (§6) and the result is reported as cost-model-dependent
  if gross ≈ cost.

Any single failure ⇒ NO-GO for that specification. A NO-GO is a successful
outcome and will be recorded, not retried until it passes.

## 5. Validity gate (runs BEFORE the verdict is read)

A null is uninterpretable without a positive control: "no effect" and "my
measurement cannot see an effect" are the same output (LEARNINGS §5).

* **G1 — synthetic injection.** Inject a known reversion of size `m` sigma into
  the post-gap path and confirm the pipeline recovers `m` within tolerance,
  for `m` in {0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20}. This also yields the **minimum
  detectable effect** at this sample size.
* **G2 — non-degeneracy.** Selected-event counts, per-pair and pooled, printed
  next to every statistic. Any cell whose count moves between arms invalidates
  that comparison (LEARNINGS §2).

**If G1 fails, the run is INCONCLUSIVE and the method is repaired — it is not
reported as NO-GO.**

## 6. Controls

* **C1 — weekday-break placebo.** The identical pipeline on ordinary
  weeknight breaks. If the same "reversion" appears there, it is not a weekend
  phenomenon. Counts printed per arm (coverage-matched).
* **C2 — continuation arm.** Report the un-signed forward return as well, so
  reversion and continuation are distinguished rather than assumed.
* **C3 — term structure.** Report all horizons. A one-day claim that is
  entirely its first 15 minutes is not a one-day claim.
* **C4 — era split.** Report by year-block; also report the JPY-padded
  vintage (2021-2023, USDJPY/NZDJPY/EURJPY) separately.
* **C5 — cost estimate.** Reopen-minute range vs mid-session range, per pair,
  as the spread proxy.

## 7. What would make this a GO

Pooled positive, clustered t >= 2, >= 6/9 pairs positive including AUDJPY,
retaining most of its size at a 60-minute delayed entry, and still positive
after a realistic reopen spread. Then, and only then, it advances to a barrier
/ execution study.
