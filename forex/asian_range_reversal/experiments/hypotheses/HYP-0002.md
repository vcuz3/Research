# HYP-0002 - London first-trade bounded-RSI midpoint fade

- Status: rejected by EXP-0002 locked-era kill test
- Created: 2026-08-03
- Origin idea: post-hoc EXP-0001 diagnostics plus user thesis clarification
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: London's first moderate momentum break of the observed
  Asian boundary is more likely to be a liquidity sweep and reversal; severe RSI
  represents continuation, and repeat entries no longer represent the thesis.
- Exact change: restrict completed-bar signals to London, bound RSI to 70-75 or
  25-30, use midpoint target only, and cap at the first executed trade per pair/day.
- Frozen baseline: `baseline_replication/configs/hyp_0002.json`.
- Primary metric: 0.5-pip-cost mean pips/trade, separately for each pair.
- Kill test: reject if 0.5-pip net mean is non-positive in either pair or either
  direction, or if a required pair-direction slice contains fewer than 100 trades.
  Also report gross, daily Sharpe, exit mix, and equal-risk net R without using
  them to override the primary verdict.
- Required controls: 0/0.5/1.0-pip cost; long/short; yearly stability; daily
  accounting including zero-trade eligible days; adverse one-minute ambiguity;
  count signals ignored while positioned and suppressed by the daily cap.
- Data scope: EURUSD/GBPUSD, 2021-01-01 through 2023-12-31 only. Do not use 2012-2020
  to alter this frozen validation configuration and do not load 2024+.
- Multiple-testing note: configuration was selected after EXP-0001 decompositions.
  The interval is strategy-specific locked evidence, not a pristine market holdout,
  because related Asian-sweep and RSI exploration previously viewed these years.

## Rationale and evidence

EXP-0001 rejected unrestricted entries. Its most coherent cross-pair diagnostics
were London timing, deterioration at severe RSI, and weaker later entries. This
revision uses one symmetric bounded-RSI rule and one-trade cap; it deliberately
omits additional ATR, range-width, direction, and sequence searches.
