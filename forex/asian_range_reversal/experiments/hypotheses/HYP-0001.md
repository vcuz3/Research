# HYP-0001 - Stateful Asian-range breaks with five-minute RSI extremes

- Status: rejected by EXP-0001
- Created: 2026-08-03
- Origin idea: user follow-up to `forex/exploration_1/asian_range_sweep_results.json`
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: an outside-Asian-range state accompanied by a five-minute RSI
  extreme identifies exhaustion; the slower feature clock avoids one-minute noise.
- Exact change: paper/PAPER_SPEC.md and baseline_replication/configs/hyp_0001.json
- Frozen baseline: every qualifying five-minute outside-range/RSI state while
  flat, next-5m-open entry, 2x ATR stop, midpoint target; opposite boundary is
  secondary. Signals occurring during a trade are discarded.
- Primary metric: 0.5-pip-cost mean pips/trade for the midpoint target.
- Kill test: reject the initial midpoint strategy if gross mean is non-positive
  in either pair or either direction, or if 0.5-pip-cost mean is non-positive in
  either pair. Treat fewer than 100 trades in a required slice as inconclusive.
  Opposite-target failure cannot be rescued by selecting midpoint post hoc.
- Required controls: 0/0.5/1.0-pip costs; long versus short; midpoint versus
  opposite target; adverse same-minute ordering; trade and daily accounting;
  signal frequency and ignored-signal audit.
- Data scope: EURUSD/GBPUSD, 2012-01-01 through 2020-12-31 only for EXP-0001.
  Do not load or score 2021-2023 in this run.
- Multiple-testing note: the setup follows inspected pre-2024 sweep and RSI work.
  Both target variants and all diagnostics are consumed-history development tests.

## Rationale and evidence

The first-sweep exploration found later opposite-side reach near 58-60%, but a
day re-pairing null explained the unconditional frequency and naive next-open
fades were weak/asymmetric. RSI is added as a causal exhaustion conditioner.
