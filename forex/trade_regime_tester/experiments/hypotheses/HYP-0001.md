# HYP-0001 — Frozen-strategy outcomes vary across causally defined market regimes

- Status: proposed
- Created: 2026-08-07
- Origin idea: none
- Builder: Codex
- Proposed mechanism: Volatility, trend, distribution, dependence, macro, liquidity, calendar, and strategy-state conditions can change a frozen strategy's conditional expectancy.
- Exact change: Apply reusable decision-time regime features and training-only thresholds to a completed-trades input table; do not change strategy execution.
- Frozen baseline: Unconditional frozen-trade performance by split.
- Primary metric: Same-sign train/test contrast with circular-shift FDR q-value and minimum cell coverage.
- Kill test: Reject a candidate regime if its fixed train contrast reverses in test, fails minimum coverage/cell counts, or does not beat the circular-shift null after FDR control.
- Required controls: Timestamp as-of joins; holdout predicate lock; training-only GARCH/thresholds/clusters; circular-shift null; thin-cell audit; interaction limits.
- Data scope: Default smoke: AUDUSD sma_80 frozen trades, 2019 train and 2021 test; 2024+ sealed. Notebook defaults: pre-2020 train, 2021-2023 test, 2024+ sealed.
- Multiple-testing note: Benjamini-Hochberg FDR across all regime contrasts; pairwise interactions are exploratory only.

## Rationale and evidence

Build the reusable diagnostic pipeline before evaluating or promoting any specific regime gate.
