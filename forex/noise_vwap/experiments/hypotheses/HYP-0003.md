# HYP-0003 — The FX Noise-Area failure is caused by an ARBITRARY session anchor; anchoring at a real structural boundary restores entry information

- Status: preregistered (written before the material run, RULES.md rule 24)
- Created: 2026-08-11
- Origin idea: user question — "does the noise area fail on FX because spot FX has
  no real open?"
- Builder: Claude (Opus 4.8)
- Frozen reference: EXP-0001 / EXP-0002 primary cells (`fxday` = 17:15 ET anchor,
  1,425-minute session, lookback 90, ET wall-clock `:29`/`:59` decision clock).
- Multiple-testing note: 24 placebo anchors are swept ON PURPOSE to form a null
  distribution. The primary cell is fixed in advance (below) and the structural
  anchors are compared to the **distribution of the placebo family**, not to a
  single hand-picked placebo. This is the LEARNINGS §1 "rerun the scheduled clock
  at ALL phases of its interval" control, where the phase here is the anchor hour.

## Proposed mechanism

The Noise-Area construct measures |displacement from a session anchor| against the
trailing same-slot distribution of that displacement. On NQ the anchor is the
09:30 ET cash open — a genuine auction boundary where overnight information is
cleared in one print, positioning resets, and the day's price discovery starts
from a reference every participant agrees on. `futures/nq/noise_vwap` EXP-0020
showed the per-slot empirical shape is load-bearing and transfers to ES: the
displacement profile from that anchor is real, repeatable structure.

Spot FX trades 24×5 and has **no such boundary**. `fxday`'s 17:15 ET anchor was
chosen for archive-defect reasons (`reports/DATA_QUALITY.md`), not because
anything economic happens then. If displacement is measured from an arbitrary
timestamp, the reference price is itself noise, the band's denominator is
mis-centred, and the construct would carry no information *even if* FX had
perfectly tradable intraday continuation.

That is a **distinct and untested explanation** for the EXP-0001/0002 NO-GO.
EXP-0002 established the failure is an absence of directional information
(finding C), but attributed nothing. Every subsequent NQ band study varied the
*band mathematics* (cone, quantile, asymmetric, surround, Laplace — all
rejected). None varied the *anchor*, which is the one input the mechanism says
should matter most.

Candidate real boundaries in spot FX, each with a stated reason:

| Anchor | Structural claim |
| --- | --- |
| 17:15 ET (NY roll) | The daily value-date/swap boundary; the one true day-break in spot FX. Also the incumbent `fxday` anchor. |
| 08:15 Europe/London | The largest single liquidity regime change of the day; European corporate and hedging flow arrives as a step. |
| 09:15 Asia/Tokyo | The Asian session's own liquidity step; the only anchor whose ET offset moves with US DST, so it is not an ET artifact. |
| 16:15 Europe/London | 15 minutes AFTER the WM fix window closes. Flagged: LEARNINGS §8 — an entry inside the fix's own averaging window measures the event, not a reaction. Placed deliberately outside the window. |

## Exact change

Nothing about the strategy changes. Only the **session anchor** moves.

- **Placebo family (the null distribution):** 24 anchors, one per ET hour, at
  `HH:15` on the America/New_York wall clock. `:15` matches the frozen `fxday`
  convention and keeps every anchor out of the residual `:00–:14` archive
  thinness (`reports/DATA_QUALITY.md`; measured minute-of-hour coverage ratio is
  0.959 at `:00` for EUR/GBP/AUD). The incumbent `fxday` anchor (17:15 ET) is a
  MEMBER of this family, so the known NO-GO calibrates the sweep.
- **Structural anchors:** the four above, each derived from its OWN timezone with
  real DST (LEARNINGS §8 — hardcoding an ET offset for a London or Tokyo event
  misfiles sessions across DST transitions).
- Session length is held at 1,425 minutes for every anchor, so the bar sample is
  near-identical and only the reference price, the session grouping and the TWAP
  move.
- Decision clock stays pinned to the ET wall clock at `:29`/`:59`, so the
  decision ROWS are the same across anchors.
- Band: lookback 90 sessions, rule-9a fractional `min_periods` = 0.9 × lookback.

## Primary metric

**Per-signal mean signed forward return at h = 60 minutes, expressed in band
half-widths, pooled across the four pairs, at MATCHED fire rate, with a t clustered
on session date.** The ranking statistic per anchor is `|pooled_t|`.

Fixed primary cell, declared now so it cannot be moved: `gate=ON` (the faithful
close-beyond-band AND close-beyond-TWAP signal), `h=60`, **embargo=1** (fill at
`open[t+2]`), matched fire rate. Everything else — `gate=OFF`, other horizons,
`embargo=0` — is secondary robustness and cannot carry the verdict.

Two protocol requirements, both from prior failures in this workspace:

- **Matched fire rate (LEARNINGS §1).** Different anchors produce different σ and
  therefore different selection rates, and a threshold whose rate varies is a
  selector for whatever drives the rate. For each (anchor, pair) the band
  multiplier `k` is chosen off a fine grid (0.40 → 3.00, step 0.02) as the grid
  point whose fire rate is closest to a common target. The threshold is **picked
  off the grid, never interpolated** — `np.interp` clamps and silently converts a
  rate-match into an extrapolation.
- **Paired 1-bar embargo (LEARNINGS §6, CONFIRMED on this exact archive).**
  `open[t+1] == close[t]` on 99.99998% of contiguous minutes here, so filling at
  the next bar's open does NOT separate the decision window from the forward
  window, and manufactures reversion. The primary arm fills at `open[t+2]` on the
  identical signal set. `embargo=0` is reported beside it as the diagnostic, not
  as the result.

## Kill test

The anchor explanation is **REJECTED** unless **all three** hold in the primary
cell:

1. at least one structural anchor reaches `|pooled_t| >= 2.0`;
2. that anchor's effect carries the SAME SIGN on >= 3 of the 4 pairs;
3. that anchor's `|pooled_t|` exceeds the 90th percentile of the 24-anchor placebo
   family — i.e. `frac(placebo |t| >= structural |t|) <= 0.10`.

Criterion 3 is the real gate. Criteria 1–2 alone are close to free: with three
structural anchors and 24 placebos, landing one in the top 4 by chance is roughly
a coin flip. This is the LEARNINGS §1 lesson in its exact form — the `:29`
scheduled clock looked special until it was rerun at all 30 phases and came in
rank 30/30.

## Positive control (VALIDITY GATE — must pass or the run is INCONCLUSIVE)

A null result on FX is uninterpretable on its own: it cannot distinguish "FX has
no real anchor" from "this diagnostic cannot detect an anchor." So the identical
sweep runs on **NQ ETH** (`futures/nq/data/NQ_1m_clean.parquet` covers all 24 ET
hours), where 09:30 ET is a known-real boundary, added as a structural anchor
against its own 24-hour placebo family under the same 1,425-minute construction.

- If NQ's 09:30 anchor satisfies criteria 1–3, the diagnostic demonstrably
  detects a real anchor, and the FX result — whichever way it falls — is
  interpretable.
- If NQ's 09:30 anchor FAILS, the run is reported as **INCONCLUSIVE**, not as
  evidence about FX, and the method is the thing to fix.

## Required controls

- 24-anchor placebo family as the null distribution (LEARNINGS §1 phase sweep);
- NQ positive control through the identical code path (rule 18);
- matched fire rate off a swept grid (LEARNINGS §1);
- paired 1-bar embargo (LEARNINGS §6);
- per-signal mean with date-clustered SE, never session-averaged — signal count
  is endogenous here and the two estimands disagree in SIGN on this exact data
  (EXP-0002 finding D, LEARNINGS §4);
- per-anchor coverage and anchor-price stability reported (rule 9a): fraction of
  sessions whose anchor minute is actually present, and the band's decision-slot
  coverage. An anchor whose stability falls below 0.98 is flagged and cannot win.

No Null C. This is a diagnostic on a construct whose real pass already failed its
primary metric in EXP-0001; the gate-Null-C standing rule forbids spending it.

## Amendment 1 — 2026-08-11, AFTER seeing the first control result

**This amendment was written with knowledge of a result and is labelled as such.
It is not a fresh preregistration and nothing in it may be read as confirmatory.**

The first execution held session length at 1,425 minutes for every instrument so
that only the anchor moved. Under that construction the NQ positive control
**FAILED**: the real 09:30 ET open scored `|pooled_t| = 1.60` with
`frac(placebo >= real) = 0.208`. By the validity gate above, that outcome makes
the FX arm uninterpretable and makes the METHOD the thing to fix.

The likely defect is mine, not the diagnostic's: forcing NQ to a 1,425-minute
session spreads its decisions across all 24 ET hours, so the overwhelming
majority of decisions land in illiquid overnight hours where the construct was
never claimed to work. That dilutes the RTH structure the 09:30 anchor exists to
organise, and it would suppress a real anchor effect regardless of whether one is
present.

The fix, which the original text already anticipates ("the method is the thing to
fix"), is to make session LENGTH an explicit parameter and run the control at
NQ's native 390-minute RTH length. At length 390 the placebo family is simply 24
different 6.5-hour windows, one starting each ET hour, so the anchor remains the
only swept variable and the sweep design is unchanged.

Four arms now run. The validity gate moves to the first:

1. **NQ @ 390** — native length. THE validity gate.
2. **NQ @ 1425** — the failed construction, retained so the length effect is
   measured rather than asserted.
3. **FX @ 1425** — the `fxday`-comparable arm (the original FX arm).
4. **FX @ 390** — FX at the length where the control is valid. Added now, so it
   is a second FX arm and the FX verdict is read across both.

Adding a second FX arm widens the search, so the FX verdict is stated as PASS
only if a structural anchor clears all three criteria in an arm whose control
passed, and the arm is named in the verdict. The three kill-test criteria,
the primary cell, the matched-rate protocol and the embargo are all unchanged.
