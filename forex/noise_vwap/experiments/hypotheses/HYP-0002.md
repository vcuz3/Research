# HYP-0002 — The FX failure is an INVERTED entry, not an exit or cost problem, and the inversion is too small to trade

- Status: preregistered (written before the material run, RULES.md rule 24)
- Created: 2026-08-01
- Origin idea: EXP-0001 result — the Noise-Area momentum port loses at the GROSS
  level on all four FX pairs, in both session definitions, in 88 of 96 declared
  cells, with 92% of trades stopping out.
- Builder: Claude (Opus 5)
- Frozen baseline: EXP-0001 primary cell (`gate=on`, `stop_ref=both`,
  `every_bar`, 0.5 pip/side).
- Multiple-testing note: no parameter is searched here. Every arm is a
  prespecified control or diagnostic, and the futures arm is a fixed external
  reference, not a candidate.

## Proposed mechanism

EXP-0001 rejects the port, but a rejection does not say WHY. Three explanations
are separable and only one is interesting:

1. **exit geometry** — the trailing band/TWAP stop is too tight for FX, so real
   entry information is destroyed by whipsaw (92% stop-out rate is consistent
   with this);
2. **cost** — the entry carries information but less than 1.0 pip round trip;
3. **inverted entry** — displacement beyond the FX noise band *mean-reverts*
   rather than continuing, so the signal is real but points the wrong way.

Explanation 3 is the workspace-relevant one, because it would mean the
Noise-Area construct is not a broken feature on FX but a correctly-measuring one
whose *direction* is asset-class-specific — the same statement as the
`claude_exploration_1` finding that equity intraday momentum lives in the tails
while gold rank-reverts.

## Exact change

No strategy change. Three prespecified arms on the frozen EXP-0001 signal:

**A. Entry-information diagnostic (exit-neutral, rule 15).** At every decision
bar with a band, take the faithful signal. Measure the SIGNED forward return
from the honest fill (next bar's open) over horizons {30, 60, 120, 240 minutes,
to session close}. No stop, no target, no exit rule at all, so nothing of the
payoff geometry can contribute. Report the mean, the median, the hit rate and
the session-clustered t, in pips and normalised by the band half-width.

**B. Re-executed mirror (rule 16).** Run `fade=True` through the whole engine —
short an upper-band break, long a lower-band break — with `stop_ref` in
{`both`, `none`}. The mirror is RE-RUN, never obtained by multiplying realized
P&L by -1, which would share the same fills and be an algebraic identity.
`stop_ref="none"` (hold to session close) is the exit-neutral arm that isolates
the entry from the trailing stop.

**C. Sibling reference (mechanism test).** Run diagnostic A unchanged on NQ and
ES 1-minute RTH through the *same code path*, only the data differing. This is
the cross-market discriminator: the interesting claim is a SIGN FLIP between
asset classes, and it is only credible if the identical measurement is positive
on the futures that the strategy was built for.

## Primary metric

Mean signed forward return after a band breakout, normalised by the band
half-width at the decision bar, with session-clustered t. Reported alongside the
median and hit rate, because a rank statistic and a mean can disagree in sign on
the same rows (LEARNINGS 2026-07-31 (a)).

## Kill test

The "inverted entry" explanation is REJECTED unless **all three** hold:

1. mean signed forward return is negative with a session-clustered t <= -2 on at
   least 3 of the 4 FX pairs, at 2 or more horizons;
2. the same measurement on NQ and ES is POSITIVE (the sign flip is the claim; if
   futures are also negative the measurement is broken, not the market);
3. the re-executed fade beats the momentum version on gross pips/trade on at
   least 3 of the 4 pairs.

Separately, and declared now so it cannot be moved later: the inversion is
TRADABLE only if the re-executed fade's **net** pips/trade is > 0 on at least 3
of 4 pairs at 0.5 pip/side. The preregistered expectation is that it is NOT,
because EXP-0001's gross deficit (~0.5 pip/trade) is smaller than the 1.0 pip
round-trip cost — i.e. this is expected to be the Rule-16 mirror trap in its
cost form (GC 2026-07-19: the negative of a cost-dominated loser is a bigger
cost-dominated loser, and flipping rescues nothing when |gross| < cost).

## Required controls

- exit-neutral arm (`stop_ref="none"`) — separates entry from exit geometry;
- re-executed mirror rather than a sign flip of P&L (rule 16);
- the NQ/ES sibling through the identical code path (rule 18 cross-market);
- per-hour decomposition, to check the effect is not one time-of-day cell;
- mean AND median/hit-rate reported together.

No Null C: the real pass already fails the primary metric in EXP-0001, and the
gate-Null-C standing rule forbids spending it on a failing real pass.
