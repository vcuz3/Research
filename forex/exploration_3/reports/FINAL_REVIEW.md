# Final review

Verdict: **NO-GO**.

The implementation passes its causal/fill/accounting fixtures and data quality
is high, but the economic result does not clear modeled costs. The pre-2020
training set supplied no positive net candidate; the frozen SMA(80) selection
then failed every OOS and historical-holdout criterion. More tuning on these
consumed dates would be data mining.

The small positive midpoint gross result after 2021 may be researched only as a
new hypothesis, starting with measured quote spreads and a path-preserving null.
It does not justify deployment or reopening the historical holdout.
