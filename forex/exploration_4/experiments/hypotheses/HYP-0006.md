## 1. Why this axis, and why second

The run-book (§5) puts **volatility regime second**, after depth. The mechanism question:
does the tau=5 same-slot z-fade's reversion depend on the **ambient volatility regime** —
is it stronger when background vol is **compressed** (range-bound, mean-reverting) or when it
is **expanded** (large moves overshoot and snap back)? If a regime cleanly separates
capturable from un-capturable reversion, it is a **sizing/veto overlay** on the frozen book.

This is a cleaner test than it would have been before EXP-0003: the entry z is now same-slot
normalized, so a vol overlay is **not** re-measuring its own entry clock (the pre-EXP-0003
all-hours z was itself a time-of-day/vol selector).

**The hard prior (run-book §7, and LEARNINGS "depth near-sufficient statistic"):** most
overlays on this family are redundant with displacement depth `|z_slot|`. The dominant risk
here is that a vol-regime veto **secretly selects depth** — because `|z_slot| = |ret|/σ_slot`,
anything that moves σ moves depth. So the decision is read as **excess over the EXP-0005 depth
benchmark at matched count**, never versus zero, and the confound is measured directly.

## 2. Mechanism and the regime feature (causal, same-slot, depth-orthogonal by design)

- **Ambient vol** = `sigma_abs`, the trailing all-hours 28,800-minute σ already carried on
  every signal. It is deliberately the SLOW window, not `sigma_slot`: `sigma_slot` is the
  denominator of depth, so a regime built on it would be mechanically depth. `sigma_abs` has
  almost no intraday profile (median 3.156→3.179 pips across 24 UTC hours, EXP-0003), so a
  regime built on it is a multi-session compression/expansion level, not a time-of-day cut.
- **Regime feature** `vr_z` = same-slot z-score of `sigma_abs`: `(sigma_abs − μ_slot) / σ_slot`,
  where `μ_slot`, `σ_slot` are the trailing 90-same-slot-session mean/std of the `sigma_abs`
  series (prior sessions only, 2/3 fractional floor) — the identical causal construction as
  the entry's `same_slot_moments`, applied to the σ series instead of returns. `vr_z` high =
  ambient vol elevated vs its own norm = **expansion**; `vr_z` low = **compression**.
- **Two symmetric arms, both pre-registered:** a **compression veto** (keep the lowest `vr_z`)
  and an **expansion veto** (keep the highest `vr_z`). Reporting only the winning arm would be
  a hidden two-sided test; both are swept and both are reported.

## 3. Exact change (overlay on the frozen book — entry NOT changed)

- Base book: the frozen EXP-0004 **stateful, guarded-anchor, delay-1** book, reconstructed by
  importing `_run_stage_b` (identical signals, no-entry rule, cost, non-overlap). The overlay
  **never changes the entry** (`|z_slot| ≥ 2.0` first crossing stays fixed).
- Overlay = a **regime veto**: keep book-eligible trades on one side of a `vr_z` cut, then
  **re-apply the one-position non-overlap** (Rule 13 / Stage-B F1: vetoing frees occupancy, so
  the stateful book is recomputed, never filtered post-hoc).
- Sweep the cut as a **keep-fraction quantile** of `vr_z` (0.10…0.90, step 0.05) for each arm,
  so the trade count is dialled directly and matched-count comparison to depth is exact. A
  keep-fraction of 1.0 reproduces the base book (parity check).

## 4. Primary metric and decision

**Primary:** per-signal **mean net R_slot** (base cost) on the **re-non-overlapped stateful
book** at each regime cut, UTC-day cluster-robust 95% CI, trade count printed beside it. Gross
R_slot, R_abs and pips alongside; consumed history only for the decision.

**Vol regime is ACCEPTED as a rescue** only if, for either arm, some cut at a **deployable
count** (≥ ~2,000 consumed trades, ≥ ~2 pairs) has **net R_slot CI strictly > 0** *and*
**beats the EXP-0005 depth benchmark at matched count** (its gross/net exceeds the
`depth_frontier.csv` value read at the nearest depth-grid count — read directly, never
interpolated: `np.interp` clamps). **Vol regime is REJECTED as a rescue** if net stays ≤ 0 at
every deployable cut on both arms, OR if any positive read fails to beat depth at matched
count (i.e. it is just re-selecting depth).

**Gated null (`gate-nullc-on-success-metric`):** the claim-matched null is spent only if the
primary first clears. If it does, run a **regime-permutation null**: permute `vr_z` across
trades within (era, σ_slot quintile) strata, rebuild the frontier over repeated draws, and
compare the real selected-max net to the null distribution of maxima (selection-aware). If the
primary does not clear, the null is unnecessary and that is stated.

## 5. Controls / diagnostics in the same pass

- **Depth confound (the central control):** `corr(vr_z, |z_slot|)` on consumed eligible
  trades, and the **mean `|z_slot|` of each kept set** at every cut. If the winning arm's kept
  set is systematically deeper, the "regime effect" is depth. Excess-over-depth at matched
  count is the quantitative version of this control.
- **Both arms reported** (compression and expansion), with per-cut counts beside every stat.
- **Both risk units** (R_slot sizing, R_abs yardstick); gross and net; cost pips.
- **Keep-fraction = 1.0 parity** with the frozen EXP-0004 book (same n, same mean).
- **Mechanism decomposition:** anchor-fill rate, timeout rate, mean σ_slot and mean σ_abs by
  regime cut — to see whether a regime changes the fill/payoff or merely the cost scale.
- 2024+ holdout read once at the end, on the arm/cut the consumed decision points to, and only
  after the consumed decision is made.

## 6. What this run cannot do

- It cannot make a sub-cost gross edge deployable; if a regime orders gross but net stays
  negative, or it merely re-selects depth, vol regime is exhausted as a rescue (the expected
  outcome per the honest prior, run-book §7).
- It cannot rehabilitate the ruler: any positive read is conditional on EXP-0004 surviving
  re-verification and on the Stage-A repairs.
- Midpoint OHLC only; cost is modelled; the guarded fill is a model. 2024+ stays sealed until
  the end.
