## 1. What this is (and is not)

This is **pure description**. No strategy is committed, no GO/NO-GO is issued, and
no parameter is selected on strategy PnL. The single decision Stage A produces is
the **(decision grain τ*, holding-horizon cap H*)** that Stage B will freeze its
reference book at, and that decision is made on a **descriptive reversion
property**, not on net P&L.

Prior: `exploration_1` already exhausted a large conditioner search on this z-fade
family, and `EXP-0001` closed NO-GO on a stopped 5-minute version. Stage A is not
expected to find an edge. It is expected to produce an honest map.

## 2. Parameterization (frozen)

The run-book's ladder is **5, 15, 30, 60, 120 minutes**. Stage A binds the ladder
to one symbol, τ, meaning **the displacement timescale = the decision bar width**:

- Bars are built on a τ-minute epoch-anchored, left-labelled, left-closed grid from
  1-minute midpoint OHLC. A τ-bar is tradable only if **all τ source minutes exist**.
- Displacement: `z_τ(t) = (close_t / close_{t-1bar} − 1) / σ_τ(t)` — a one-bar,
  τ-minute displacement. Because it is one bar by construction, the displacement
  lookback is **exactly τ minutes at every rung**; there is no bar-count lookback
  that can silently multiply across grains (RUNBOOK trap 1).
- `σ_τ` = trailing standard deviation of one-bar τ-returns over a **fixed TIME
  window of 28,800 minutes (20 × 24h)** — i.e. `28800/τ` bars at every rung, so the
  σ estimator spans the same wall-clock history at every grain. `min_periods` is a
  **fractional floor** `ceil(0.9 × window_bars)`, never strict equality
  (LEARNINGS §2).
- `σ_τ` is causal: it is computed from returns up to and including bar `t`, and it
  is used only to standardize bar `t`'s own displacement for a decision executed at
  bar `t+1`'s open.

This choice makes view 1 and view 2 **the same τ**: `VR(τ)` measures the serial
dependence of a τ-minute move; view 2 measures what happens after a τ-minute move
is extreme. That is the comparison the run-book asks for.

### Anchor definition (view 2)

The **anchor** is the price at the start of the displacement window:
`P_anchor = close_{signal_bar − 1}`. "Reversion to anchor" means price trades back
to `P_anchor`. This is a fixed, interpretable level, unlike a rolling `z → 0`
condition, which a one-bar displacement satisfies trivially. The rolling `z → 0`
rate is still reported as a secondary column because Stage B's exit is defined that
way and Stage B needs to know how the two differ.

### Risk unit

`R ≡ σ_τ` at the signal bar, in return units. A signal fired at `|z| = 2` that
retraces exactly to its anchor earns **+2R**. Pips are reported alongside every R
figure. Costs are converted to R with each trade's own `σ_τ` and price.

### Other frozen axes

| Axis | Values |
| --- | --- |
| Pairs | EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, NZDUSD (≈2 effective independent) |
| Entry threshold `k` | 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0 (`\|z_τ\| ≥ k`, **first crossing** only) |
| Holding horizon `H` (minutes, wall clock) | 15, 30, 60, 120, 240, 480 |
| Sessions | DST-aware: Tokyo 09:00–15:00, London 08:00–16:30, New York 08:00–17:00 local. Labels: `overlap` (London∩NY), `london`, `ny`, `asia`, `off` |
| Eras | `early` 2012-01-01…2020-12-31, `late` 2021-01-01…2023-12-31, `holdout` 2024-01-01+ |

Side is a **fade**: `side = −sign(z_τ)`. Entry is the **open of the next complete
τ-bar** after the signal bar closes (Rule 2). Exit for a fixed horizon `H` is the
**open of the 1-minute bar H minutes after entry** (path column `m = H`).

## 3. Constraint set applied (from RUNBOOK)

- **No compulsory stop.** View 2 is unstopped. View 3's bounded double barrier is a
  *diagnostic for signal cleanliness*, explicitly not a reference book.
- **No fixed-clock entry rule** — every signal is an event (a first crossing).
- **News blackout** (±30 minutes around a high-impact scheduled release for that
  pair) is a standing veto. Primary view-2/view-3 statistics are **post-veto**;
  vetoed counts are reported separately.
- **Flat over the weekend.** The strict complete-path requirement below already
  excludes any window spanning the weekend gap; the count truncated by the Friday
  flat rule is reported explicitly.

## 4. Coverage rules (Rule 9a) — declared in advance

- A forward return at horizon `H` from entry minute `e` is used **only if every
  1-minute bar in `[e, e+H]` exists** (a strictly complete path). No stale-price
  interpolation, ever.
- Because that sample shrinks with `H`, the surface is reported **twice**: once on
  each `H`'s own maximal sample (with the per-cell count printed beside every
  statistic), and once on the **common sample** of signals complete at `H = 480`.
  If the surface's shape differs between the two, the shape is a coverage artifact
  and must be read as such (LEARNINGS §2: placebos and surfaces must be
  coverage-matched).
- Every table prints its **n**. Any statistic whose cell count moves relative to its
  comparison cell is flagged, not silently compared.
- NZDUSD's known late-era 18:00–19:00 UTC holes and the unavailable 17:00 New York
  rollover bar are expected to bite; they are enumerated by era × UTC hour, not
  summarized away.
- Variance-ratio autocovariance pairs `(r_t, r_{t−k})` are used only when both
  1-minute returns are individually valid **and** the two timestamps are exactly
  `k` minutes apart. Session-conditional cells additionally require both endpoints
  in the same session. Per-cell pair counts are reported at every lag used.

## 5. The three views

**View 1 — Variance ratio `VR(τ)`.** On 1-minute log returns.
`VR(τ) = 1 + 2·Σ_{k=1}^{τ−1}(1 − k/τ)·ρ̂(k)`. `<1` reversion, `=1` random walk,
`>1` trend. Inference by the **Lo–MacKinlay heteroskedasticity-robust** statistic
`z* = (VR−1)/sqrt(Σ_k [2(τ−k)/τ]² δ̂_k)`, because volatility clustering inflates the
estimator's standard error without moving its point estimate. Reported per pair ×
era × session with pair counts. Strategy-free and level-free: volatility
compression cannot move it, only serial dependence can.

**View 2 — Conditional extreme→anchor reversion.** `E[fade return | |z_τ| ≥ k]` over
the `τ × H × k` surface, in **R units**, **gross and net**, with the
**anchor-retrace touch rate** (50% and 100%) and the rolling `z → 0` rate.
Estimand: **per-signal mean R with a UTC-day cluster-robust SE** — never a
session/day-averaged R as the headline (it inverts sign when signal count is
endogenous; LEARNINGS §4).

**View 3 — Gross-R profit factor by grain.** `Σ(gross R > 0) / Σ|gross R < 0|` from
the audited `_bracket_engine.simulate_bracket` (adverse intrabar tiebreak, Rule 3;
gap-through fills, Rule 5) on 1-minute paths, with **barriers in vol units and RR
fixed across grains** (TP = SL = 1.0σ_τ, RR = 1.0). Reported at a **fixed 240-minute
time cap** (equal time budget across grains — the *raw* view) **and** at a
**τ-scaled cap of 8τ** (equal bar budget — the *matched* view). Prior RSI work held
RR constant but not vol-unit barriers, so part of its 15-minute advantage was
geometry; this view re-checks that cleanly. Non-overlap is enforced greedily on the
timeout window for the daily-P&L/Sharpe rows (Rule 13); the profit factor itself is
per-signal.

**Also reported (description only).** Realized-vol profile by UTC hour and session;
return-distribution skew / excess kurtosis / `>3σ` tail mass across the ladder;
naive **always-long and always-short drift** per pair × session × H, so no later
overlay can be credited with baseline drift; the weekend/Friday-gap distribution and
the number of entries the Friday-flat rule truncates.

## 6. Pre-committed grain-selection rule

Applied mechanically to the outputs, in this order:

1. **Eligibility.** A rung τ is eligible only if, at that rung, (a) view 1 and view 2
   **agree in direction** (`VR(τ) < 1` *and* the pooled consumed-history gross mean R
   at `k = 2.0` over its best `H` is `> 0`), and (b) it has **no unreported coverage
   defect** — every coverage rule's exclusion count is enumerated in
   `reports/DATA_QUALITY.md`.
2. **Primary score.** Among eligible rungs, rank by **descriptive reversion
   strength** = the pooled consumed-history `1 − VR(τ)` (view 1), and independently
   by the pooled consumed-history **gross mean R at `k = 2.0`** maximized over `H`
   (view 2). **τ\* is the rung that is top-ranked on both.**
3. **Disagreement.** If the two rankings disagree, no rung is selected by score;
   the disagreement is documented, and τ\* is taken from **view 2** (the conditional
   view tests the actual signal; VR is unconditional and linear and cannot see
   extreme-conditional behaviour). This substitution must be stated in the report as
   a departure.
4. **Ties** (within 0.005 R on view 2, or within 0.005 on `1 − VR`) break toward
   **lower turnover / lower cost-per-R** — i.e. the coarser rung.
5. **`H*`** = the `H` maximizing pooled consumed-history gross mean R at τ\* and
   `k = 2.0` **on the common (H=480-complete) sample**, so the horizon is not chosen
   by a coverage artifact. If gross mean R is within 0.005 R across several `H`, take
   the **shortest** (least exposure).
6. **View 3 is corroboration, not a tiebreaker.** If view 3's profit-factor ranking
   contradicts the selected τ\*, the contradiction is reported and the exit gate is
   marked *documented disagreement*, not *agreement*.
7. **Strategy net PnL is never an input to this rule.**

## 7. Exit gate for Stage A

Passes when: views 1–3 agree on a grain **or** the disagreement is understood and
written down; the rule above resolves to a specific `(τ*, H*)`; and no coverage
defect at τ\* is unreported.

## 8. Holdout

2024+ is **sealed**. Every number driving the selection rule comes from consumed
history (2012-01-01…2023-12-31). The holdout is opened **once**, at the end of the
report, as a descriptive stability check only — it cannot change τ\* or H\*.
