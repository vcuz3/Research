# HYP-0004 — Stage B: freeze the same-slot z-fade reference book (EXP-0004)

## 1. Purpose

Freeze **one** honest mean-reversion book under the Stage-B constraint set, to serve as the
**ruler** every Stage-C overlay is measured against. This is not a search and not a GO/NO-GO
hunt: parameters are fixed by fiat from Stage A (`RUNBOOK.md` §4.1), **nothing is tuned to
PnL**, and a negative book is an acceptable, useful ruler (Rule 25).

## 2. The frozen book

Entry, feature and grain are exactly the EXP-0003 recommendation (`RUNBOOK.md` §4.1):

- 5-minute epoch-anchored complete-only bars.
- Feature `z_slot = (ret_5 − μ_slot)/σ_slot`; slot = UTC minute-of-day // 5; moments from
  **90 prior sessions of the same slot**, `min_periods = 60`, prior sessions only, estimated
  on the full slot grid and read at the events (`same_slot_moments`, under test).
- Entry: fade `|z_slot| ≥ 2.0`, **first crossing** only. `k = 2.0` by fiat — the EXP-0003
  value 2.14 exists only to rate-match the all-hours arm and is **not** inherited.
- Entry fill: **delay-1** primary (open of the bar two bars after the signal bar), **delay-0**
  diagnostic. The shared-endpoint artifact (`open[i]==close[i-1]` for 99.99998% of minutes)
  makes delay-0 an over-stated ruler, so the book is defined on delay-1.
- Holding cap: 240 minutes. No stop.
- Vetoes: news blackout ±30 min (high-impact, that pair); force-flat Friday 16:55 NY.
- One position per pair (greedy non-overlap) for the book/Sharpe accounting.

### 2.1 The exit — decided and frozen here (`RUNBOOK.md` §4.2)

The run-book's old "exit at z=0" is degenerate at τ=5 (zcross is 0.96 by H=15, a time exit
under another name). The discriminating exit is **anchor retrace**: exit when price returns
to the **anchor** (close of the bar *before* the signal bar). A touch is **not** a fill
(Rule 4), and with midpoint data and no volume there is no queue evidence, so the frozen
book uses a **volatility-scaled trade-through guard**:

- **The book (frozen):** guarded anchor-retrace. The exit limit sits at the anchor. It is
  credited only when the favourable extreme trades **through** the anchor by
  **g = 0.25·σ_slot** (in that trade's slot-σ, ~0.75 pip at τ=5 — a volatility-scaled guard,
  never a fixed tick, per Rule 4 / LEARNINGS §6). The fill is credited **conservatively at
  the anchor price** — never the better gap-through price (Rule 1: no unavailable price
  improvement). If not triggered within the (Friday-capped) 240-minute window, exit at the
  bar open at the cap (a market exit).
- **Floor reported alongside:** pure 240-minute **time exit** (market at cap, no anchor
  logic). Requires no fill assumption; the conservative lower bound.
- **Ceiling reported alongside:** anchor **touch** (g = 0), limit credited on first touch.
  Optimistic upper bound.

The three treatments **bracket** the fill model so its effect is visible. `g = 0.25σ` is a
declared modelling choice, frozen, not optimised.

### 2.2 Units (`RUNBOOK.md` §4.3)

P&L is computed in **pips**, then expressed as:

- `R_slot = pnl_pips·PIP / (σ_slot·entry)` — the unit the book **sizes in**; the headline.
- `R_abs  = pnl_pips·PIP / (σ_abs·entry)`  — σ from the 28,800-min window; cross-hour and
  cross-arm **yardstick**.

Net = gross − cost in **each** unit (a gross figure in `R_slot` against a cost in pips is not
a net result). Because σ_slot tracks the intraday profile while the modelled round trip is
roughly flat in pips, net in `R_slot` tilts toward busy hours **structurally** — mechanical,
reported, not a discovery, and no overlay is built on it here.

## 3. Estimand and inference

Per-signal **mean R** with a **UTC-day cluster-robust** SE and 95% CI (`cluster_summary`,
under test). Never block-averaged R as the headline (it inverts sign when signal count is
endogenous). Pooled across pairs, and split by pair and by era.

## 4. What is reported, in order (`RUNBOOK.md` §4.4)

1. **Gross first**, all three exit treatments, both entry arms.
2. Cost curve (optimistic/base/pessimistic + vol term) and the **breakeven round-trip pip**.
3. **Net by era**, in `R_slot` and `R_abs`.
4. The **three Sharpes** (zero-day / trade-days-only / vol-targeted) on the one-position book.
5. The sealed **2024+ holdout**, opened **once**, at the end.

## 5. Pre-registered expectations (recorded so results cannot be re-narrated)

- Gross ≈ 0.35–0.40 pips (delay-1); **net negative** in every cell at base cost, gross ~3×
  short of the ~1.21-pip modelled round trip (0.70 pip commission alone).
- τ=5 delay-1 gross CI expected to exclude zero on the positive side (EXP-0003: 0.1056 R_abs
  [0.045, 0.166]); if it does **not** under the book's exit, that is a reportable change.
- A busy-hours net tilt in `R_slot` is expected and mechanical.

## 6. What this run cannot do

- It cannot establish a real spread: the archive has no bid/ask (`RUNBOOK.md` §7). If the
  decision hinges on cost, the honest next step is quote data, not another overlay.
- UTC slots do not track DST; a slot's meaning shifts by an hour twice a year. Documented,
  not corrected. A local-time-slot variant is a possible one-run follow-up if Stage C needs it.
- 2024+ is read exactly once, at the end, and does not influence any frozen choice.
