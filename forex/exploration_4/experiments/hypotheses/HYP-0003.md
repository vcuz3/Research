# HYP-0003 — Stage-A addendum: is the `|z|` cut a time-of-day selector? (EXP-0003)

**Status:** frozen pre-run. Written and committed *before* any same-slot statistic was
computed. Corrections go in `artifacts/runs/EXP-0003/review.md`, never here.

**Numbering note.** The run-book assigns EXP-0003 to Stage B. This addendum takes
EXP-0003 because the ledger requires `EXP-\d{4}`; **Stage B therefore becomes EXP-0004.**

---

## 1. Why this run exists

EXP-0002 measured `z_τ = ret_τ / σ_τ` with `σ_τ` a trailing **28,800-minute all-hours**
window. A four-week window cannot see the intraday volatility profile, so `σ_τ` is
almost flat across the clock (median 3.156–3.179 pips across all 24 UTC hours at τ=5)
while actual bar sizes are not. The fixed `|z| ≥ 2` cut therefore fires on **0.96%** of
decisions at 04:00 UTC and **10.11%** at 14:00 — a **10.5× swing, selection-rate
CV 0.707**.

That is the failure mode in `ai_shared_memory/LEARNINGS.md` §1: *a threshold that is a
function of something is a selector for that something*. The reference fix there —
normalise against the trailing **same-slot** distribution with a z-score — took a CV of
0.93 down to 0.05 without destroying the information.

**This addendum does not re-open the EXP-0002 selection rule.** It asks three specific
questions whose answers Stage B needs.

## 2. The two arms

| Arm | `σ` estimator | Question it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `abs` (EXP-0002, unchanged) | trailing 28,800 min of market time, all hours pooled | how large is this move in absolute terms |
| `slot` (new) | trailing **90 prior sessions of the SAME time-of-day slot** | how unusual is this move *for this hour* |

`slot` construction, frozen:

- Slot = UTC minute-of-day `// τ`, so slot width equals the decision grain at every rung.
- `z_slot(t) = (ret_τ(t) − μ_slot(t)) / σ_slot(t)`, a **full z-score**, not a
  ratio-to-mean: a ratio removes per-slot location but leaves per-slot scale, which is
  the thing that varies here. `μ_slot` is expected to be immaterial (returns are already
  centred near zero); its size relative to `σ_slot` is reported so that is checked, not
  assumed.
- `μ_slot`, `σ_slot` use **prior sessions only** (`shift(1)` along the session axis).
  They are estimated on the **full slot grid** and read at the events — never estimated
  from the events themselves.
- Sessions are compacted to trading days before rolling (holes are dropped, not
  zero-filled), and `min_periods` is a **fractional floor of 2/3 × 90 = 60** prior
  sessions, never strict equality.

Everything else is held identical to EXP-0002: pairs, grain ladder, first-crossing event
clock, next-bar-open fill, news blackout, strict path completeness, the paired one-bar
entry-delay control, cost model, and the per-signal mean R with a UTC-day cluster-robust
SE.

Reduced axes, to keep the addendum focused: horizons **{60, 240}** (H\* plus one shorter
check) and a finer threshold grid **k ∈ {1.5, 1.75, 2.0, 2.25, 2.5, 3.0}** so both arms
can be compared at a matched firing rate **without interpolation** (`np.interp` clamps,
so a matched-rate comparison built on it silently becomes an extrapolation).

## 3. Units

Reported per arm:

- `gross_R_own` — R = that arm's own σ, i.e. the risk actually sized on.
- `gross_R_abs` — R = the **absolute** σ_τ for both arms, as a common yardstick.
- `gross_pips`.

Neither arm's own-R figure is comparable across arms on its own; `gross_R_abs` is the
cross-arm comparison and is the number the decision rules below use.

## 4. The three questions, with decision rules written before the run

**Q1 — Does the fix work?** Per-hour selection-rate CV at τ = 5, k matched.
*Pass:* `slot` CV **< 0.25** (against `abs` at 0.707). If `slot` CV ≥ 0.25 the
normaliser has not done its job and Q2/Q3 are not interpretable.

**Q2 — Was the session localisation real, or was it the clock?** Compare the
`off`/`asia` versus `london` gap in `gross_R_abs` at matched per-hour firing rate,
delay-1 arm, k matched to `abs` k=2.0.
*The thin-hours localisation SURVIVES* only if, under `slot`, at least one of `off` or
`asia` still exceeds `london` by more than the sum of the two cells' 95% CI half-widths.
*Otherwise the EXP-0002 session breakdown is retracted as a clock artifact* and
`MEMORY.md` must say so explicitly.

**Q3 — Does the grain choice survive?** Rank the τ ladder by `gross_R_abs` under `slot`,
delay-1 arm, at matched rate.
*τ\* = 5 is CONFIRMED* if τ=5 is top-ranked. *If not*, τ\* is **re-opened**: the
addendum reports the conflict and Stage B may not freeze a grain until it is resolved.

**Q4 (secondary) — Does normalising destroy the information?** Compare arms at matched
firing rate on `gross_R_abs`. Report both; neither is presumed correct. A `slot` arm that
is materially worse at matched rate is itself a finding (it would mean the absolute cut's
regime selection was carrying real information, not just noise).

## 5. What this run cannot do

- It cannot rescue the cost verdict. The breakeven-pip arithmetic from EXP-0002 is
  unchanged by renormalising a threshold; if `slot` shifts which signals are taken it
  changes the breakeven, and that is reported, but no result here makes a 0.4-pip gross
  edge clear a 1.21-pip round trip.
- UTC slots do not track DST, so a slot's meaning shifts by an hour twice a year against
  London and New York local time. This blends two adjacent hours' volatility for part of
  the year. Documented as a limitation, not corrected.
- 2024+ stays sealed. No number here is read from it.
