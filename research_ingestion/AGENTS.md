# Research ingestion instructions

Before project work, read `PROJECT_GUIDE.md` and `MEMORY.md` in addition to the
workspace research rules. This project gathers reading material; it does not
make or validate trading claims.

- Preserve original PDFs byte-for-byte and record their SHA-256 hashes.
- Never bypass paywalls, authentication, robots controls, licences, or source
  rate limits.
- Never commit API keys, OAuth client secrets, refresh tokens, or email tokens.
- AI routing is zero-cost only: one explicitly configured OmniRoute free-only
  route, no paid or secondary fallback.
- Keep publication, update, retrieval, and source timestamps distinct.
- Update `MEMORY.md` when sources, guarantees, or operational risks change.
